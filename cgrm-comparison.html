CGRM — Comparison & Main Difference

What Makes CGRM Different

  • Most standards define rules.
    CGRM defines how governance actually operates.

  • Most standards are checked periodically.
    CGRM operates continuously.

  • Most standards describe processes.
    CGRM assigns responsibility.

  • Most standards rely on certification.
    CGRM relies on accountability.

  • Most standards are static.
    CGRM is a live system.

Structural Difference

Traditional standards are:
  • document-based
  • audit-driven
  • periodically verified
  • externally controlled
 CGRM is:
  • system-based
  • operational
  • continuously active
  • internally enforced

Methodology-Based vs. Document-Based

Most standards are not built on a full methodology structure.

They define:
  • requirements
  • controls
  • recommendations
But they do not define:
  • how governance operates in real time
  • how responsibility is structurally enforced
  • how systems behave under pressure
 CGRM is built on a methodology framework.

It includes:
  • standard (rules)
  • modules (implementation)
  • self-declaration (entry)
  • implementation layer (execution)
  • compatibility (system state)

Operational vs. Theoretical

Traditional standards:
  • describe what should exist
  • rely on interpretation
  • are applied through documentation
 CGRM:
  • defines what must operate
  • requires real-time conditions
  • exists as an operational state

Responsibility vs. Process

Traditional standards:
  • focus on processes
  • allow responsibility diffusion
 CGRM:
  • anchors responsibility
  • requires accountability
  • eliminates anonymity in decision-making

Certification vs. Accountability

Traditional systems:
  • require external certification
  • delay implementation
  • create dependency
 CGRM:
  • allows immediate self-declaration
  • creates responsibility instantly
  • enables operation without delay

System Reality

Traditional standards:
  • can exist without real enforcement
 CGRM:

cannot exist without operation

Core Difference

Traditional standards describe governance.

CGRM makes governance operational.

Closing Statement

CGRM is not an evolution of existing standards.

It defines a different category.
