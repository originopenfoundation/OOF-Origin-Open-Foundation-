Governed Space

Evidence Attribution

Canonical Definition

Evidence Attribution Module (EFAM™) defines the governance framework for
assigning, preserving, validating, and governing the attribution of every
governed evidence object to its originating audit observation, responsible
entities, operational sources, governing authorities, and formation
mechanisms. It establishes attribution principles, relationship requirements,
traceability controls, validation mechanisms, and governance conditions
necessary to ensure that evidence remains continuously attributable throughout
the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

EFAM governs the attribution layer of evidence formation by ensuring that
every evidence object maintains an unambiguous and verifiable relationship to
its operational origin, responsible entities, governing authority, and
evidence formation process.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Evidence Attribution Requirements

Identify the entities, audit observations, systems, identities, authorities,
processes, and operational sources that every evidence object must be
attributable to.

2. Define Attribution Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for assigning, maintaining, validating, and
preserving attribution relationships between evidence and its operational
origin.

3. Define Attribution Logic

Define how attribution is evaluated for authenticity, completeness,
consistency, traceability, governance conformity, and relationship integrity
before evidence proceeds to the integrity lifecycle.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for missing attribution, conflicting attribution,
broken traceability, unauthorized attribution changes, attribution
inconsistencies, and corrective actions where required.

5. Preserve Attribution Records

Maintain attribution relationships, responsible entities, governing
authorities, source identifiers, validation results, governance decisions,
timestamps, and supporting documentation throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An autonomous AI platform generates governed evidence from multiple validated
audit observations produced by cooperating intelligent agents.

Application

EFAM governs the attribution of every evidence object to its originating
observations, autonomous agents, operational systems, responsible authorities,
and evidence formation process.

Result

Every evidence object remains fully attributable, continuously traceable, and
suitable for trustworthy audit and independent verification.

Use Case 2 — Digital Identity Governance

Scenario

A digital identity platform generates evidence supporting identity lifecycle
operations, authority changes, and ownership transitions.

Application

EFAM governs the attribution of every evidence object to the originating
identity events, responsible entities, governing authorities, and operational
systems.

Result

Identity evidence remains continuously attributable, transparent, and suitable
for governance oversight, operational reconstruction, and independent audit.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Attribution Module establishes the canonical governance framework for
assigning, validating, preserving, and governing attribution throughout the
evidence formation lifecycle. By ensuring that every evidence object remains
continuously linked to its originating audit observations, responsible
entities, governing authorities, and operational sources, EFAM enables
trustworthy evidence, continuous traceability, accountable governance, and
independent verification throughout the complete lifecycle of every governed
entity.
