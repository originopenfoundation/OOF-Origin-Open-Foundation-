Governed Space

Audit Response

Core Question

How can organizations respond to governed audit findings in a structured,
accountable, timely, and independently verifiable manner?

Canonical Definition

Audit Response Standard defines the governance conditions required to
evaluate, plan, approve, execute, monitor, and verify organizational responses
to governed audit findings. It establishes response methodology, governance
controls, accountability mechanisms, implementation requirements, and
continuous response assurance necessary to ensure that audit findings are
addressed consistently, transparently, effectively, and in accordance with
governed organizational objectives.

Governance Flow Position

Audit Observation → Evidence Formation → Evidence Integrity → Evidence
Correlation → Evidence Verification → Audit Execution → Audit Findings → Audit
Response → Audit Preservation → Audit Assurance

Standard Abstract

Audit findings only create value when they lead to governed action.

Organizations require a standardized methodology for determining how findings
will be accepted, prioritized, assigned, implemented, monitored, and verified.

Audit Response Standard establishes the canonical governance framework for
transforming validated audit findings into accountable organizational actions.

Operational Role

Audit Response Standard governs the organizational response layer of audit
governance by establishing standardized methods for planning, approving,
executing, monitoring, and verifying responses to validated audit findings.

Operational Space

Audit Response Standard governs:

  • response planning
  • response approval
  • response implementation
  • response monitoring
  • response verification
  • response accountability
  • response governance.


Why This Standard Exists

Producing objective audit findings alone does not improve governance.

Organizations must respond through governed, documented, accountable, and
measurable actions.

Audit Response Standard exists because Audit Response represents the eighth
governed space of ADIT® — Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance
Architecture™, ensuring that validated findings lead to structured
organizational improvement rather than remaining static observations.
