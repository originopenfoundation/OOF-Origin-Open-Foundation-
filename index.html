What this is

OOF™ Origin Open Foundation™ is a global methodological authority. We define how reality is recognized, interpreted, validated, and governed
across human systems and artificial intelligence. This website is a canonical reference system.It does not execute services. It does not sell products.
It does not implement solutions. OOF™ defines the rules of meaning, validity, and admission into governed reality.

Why OOF™ exists

Modern systems fail not because of lack of data or technology, but because meaning, responsibility, value, and truth are undefined or fragmented.
AI systems interpret inconsistently. Regulators reason retroactively. Markets assign value without ethical or factual grounding.
Standards contradict each other across jurisdictions and languages. OOF™ exists to solve this at the root level.

transactions and legitimacy AI participation and limits, simulation before reality. truth validation. ethics and auditability.
