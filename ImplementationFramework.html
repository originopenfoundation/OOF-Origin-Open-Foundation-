Regional Methodology Operators

Global Call for Regional Methodology Infrastructure

OOF™ Origin Open Foundation™ hereby opens a global call for Regional Methodology Operators to co-build and operate the execution layer
of the global methodology ecosystem. This call is issued under the authority of OOF™ as a non-executable global methodology authority and applies
only within the defined scope and conditions below.

Regions Open for Applications

At the time of publication, OOF™ is opening the following regions for consideration: European Union ,United Kingdom,Switzerland,
North America, South America, China, India, Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific


OOFTM reserves the exclusive right to:
merge or split regional scopes, limit the number of operators per region,
delay activation of any region, or decline to activate a region entirely.
No exclusivity is granted by default.

Call Duration

This call is open for a period of six (6) months from the official date of publication of this page. After the six-month period: entry conditions may change,
regions may close without notice,selection thresholds may increase, or the call may be suspended entirely. Late submissions are not guaranteed to be reviewed.

Role of a Regional Methodology Operator

A Regional Methodology Operator is a locally executed, globally governed entity responsible for: operating regional methodology infrastructure,
enabling registration of methodologies as intellectual assets, providing licensing, certification, and implementation support,
connecting methodologies to real markets and institutions, localizing methodologies without altering canonical meaning,
supporting authors, innovators, and methodology creators, protecting the OOF™ brand and methodological integrity,
building long-term regional methodology ecosystems. This role is operational, not advisory.
OOF™ remains a non-executable authority and does not perform market execution.

Ownership & Control Model

Each Regional Methodology Operator is structured as follows: 50 % ownership held by OOF™ (non-executable authority, governance, canonical control)
50 % ownership held by the regional operator / investor (executive power, operations, market execution)
OOF™ retains: canonical meaning authority, system governance, compatibility validation, global integrity oversight.
The operator retains: operational execution, regional business development, partner and client engagement, implementation delivery.

Why MIP™ Exists (Reality Check)

Copyright protects recordings, not meaning, Patents protect mechanisms, not interpretation ,Trademarks protect names, not behavior
MIP™ protects the layer where reality becomes defined.
This allows protection of: music before it becomes a file, design before it becomes a product, video before it becomes content
AI output before it enters the market

Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must demonstrate: long-term financial stability, understanding of methodology as an intellectual asset, capability to protect IP and brand integrity,
commitment to innovation and ecosystem building, ability to operate across regulatory and market layers, readiness to support third-party methodology authors,
capacity to translate methodologies into real-world execution, This is not a short-term commercial opportunity.

Selection Process

The selection process is multi-stage and competitive.
Initial evaluation during the 6-month call window
Subsequent rounds with stricter entry requirements
Final selection based on: region, strategic importance, demonstrated capability long-term alignment with OOFTM principles
OOFTM may select: a single operator, multiple operators, a consortium, or no operator for a given region.

Strategic Context

Methodologies will increasingly govern: AI interpretation and decision-making, compliance and regulatory alignment, ethical and economic state validation,
trust and auditability across markets. Whoever builds and operates methodology infrastructure shapes how reality is interpreted and executed.
This call is about building that infrastructure.
