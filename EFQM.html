Evidence Qualification

Canonical Definition

Evidence Qualification Module (EFQM™) defines the governance framework for
determining whether validated audit observations satisfy the required
conditions to become governed evidence. It establishes qualification criteria,
acceptance requirements, evaluation logic, governance controls, and
qualification mechanisms necessary to ensure that only eligible audit
observations enter the governed evidence lifecycle throughout the complete
lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

EFQM governs the qualification layer of evidence formation by evaluating
whether validated audit observations satisfy the governance requirements
necessary to become trustworthy evidence.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Evidence Qualification Requirements

Identify the qualification criteria, acceptance conditions, governance
objectives, responsible authorities, and operational requirements necessary
for evidence formation.

2. Define Qualification Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for evaluating audit observations against
qualification criteria, governance requirements, completeness expectations,
and evidence acceptance conditions.

3. Define Qualification Logic

Define how audit observations are assessed for eligibility, operational
relevance, authenticity, completeness, governance conformity, and readiness
for evidence formation.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for observations that fail qualification
requirements, require additional validation, contain deficiencies, or must be
rejected before evidence formation.

5. Preserve Qualification Records

Maintain qualification decisions, evaluation results, governance approvals,
rejection records, supporting documentation, timestamps, and qualification
history throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An autonomous AI platform continuously produces validated audit observations
during operational decision-making.

Application

EFQM evaluates whether each validated observation satisfies the predefined
governance criteria required to become governed evidence.

Result

Only qualified audit observations become trustworthy evidence capable of
supporting continuous auditability and independent verification.

Use Case 2 — Financial Services

Scenario

A financial institution generates validated audit observations during payment
processing and fraud detection activities.

Application

EFQM governs the qualification of those observations before they are accepted
as official evidence for audit, regulatory oversight, and operational
accountability.

Result

Evidence entering the governed evidence lifecycle is consistently qualified,
trustworthy, and suitable for independent audit.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Qualification Module establishes the canonical governance framework
for qualifying audit observations before evidence formation. By governing
evidence qualification criteria, evaluation logic, acceptance conditions, and
governance decisions, EFQM ensures that only eligible and trustworthy audit
observations become governed evidence throughout the complete lifecycle of
every governed entity.
