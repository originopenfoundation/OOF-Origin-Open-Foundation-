A. Standard Abstract

The Carbon Visibility Index™ Standard (CVI™) establishes a structured measurement
framework for expressing lifecycle carbon exposure per product.

The standard converts lifecycle carbon transparency into a measurable and comparable
carbon-per-product value.

CVI™ does not regulate emissions and does not impose environmental targets.
It provides the measurement architecture required to quantify carbon exposure associated
with the lifecycle of a defined product unit.

By introducing a consistent carbon-per-product reference, CVI™ enables objective comparison
across products, production systems, and supply chains.

B. Canonical Definition

Carbon Visibility Index™ (CVI™) is a product-level carbon measurement framework that converts
lifecycle carbon exposure into a measurable value expressed per defined functional product unit.

The Carbon Visibility Index becomes structurally valid when lifecycle boundaries, production
energy sources, material origin, packaging impact, and logistics pathways are transparently
documented.

CVI™ defines the measurement architecture for carbon per product.

C. Purpose

The purpose of Carbon Visibility Index™ is to:

  • establish measurable carbon-per-product visibility
  • enable transparent comparison between products
  • support lifecycle carbon accountability
  • provide a structural foundation for future carbon-per-product reporting systems
CVI™ transforms lifecycle carbon transparency into quantifiable product-level information.

D. Measurement Principle

The Carbon Visibility Index is expressed as:

Total lifecycle carbon exposure per defined product unit

Examples of functional units may include:
  • kg CO₂e per product
  • kg CO₂e per kilogram of product
  • kg CO₂e per liter of product
  • kg CO₂e per defined service unit
The declared functional unit must remain consistent to maintain comparability.

E. Structural Requirements

A Carbon Visibility Index declaration is structurally valid only when:
  • lifecycle boundaries are clearly defined
  • material origin and processing stages are documented
  • production energy sources are declared
  • packaging systems are disclosed
  • transport modes and distribution pathways are identified
  • the carbon calculation methodology is transparently stated
  • a responsible entity accepts accountability for the declared carbon index
Incomplete lifecycle definition weakens index comparability.

F. Carbon Measurement Principle

Carbon Visibility Index™ is based on the principle that:

carbon exposure becomes measurable when lifecycle transparency exists.

When production origin, energy inputs, packaging systems, and logistics pathways are documented,
carbon exposure per product can be quantified.

Quantification enables comparison.
Comparison enables optimization.

G. Strategic Role

Carbon Visibility Index™ functions as:
  • a product-level carbon measurement unit
  • a comparability instrument for supply chain optimization
  • a structural bridge between carbon transparency and economic evaluation
  • a foundation for potential future product-level carbon reporting systems
CVI™ transforms lifecycle transparency into measurable product-level carbon information.

H. Long-Term Role

Carbon Visibility Index™ establishes the canonical measurement architecture required for:
  • carbon-per-product disclosure
  • transparent product comparison
  • lifecycle carbon accountability
  • future carbon pricing readiness
It defines the structural unit of carbon measurement without imposing regulatory mandate.
