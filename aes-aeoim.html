Canonical Definition

Authority Escalation Origin Integrity Module (AEOIM) defines the
structural conditions under which escalation events remain
attributable to identifiable, legitimate, traceable, governable,
accountable, and operationally valid escalation triggers throughout
the escalation lifecycle.

AEOIM governs escalation origin.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine where an escalation originated, what operational condition
triggered the escalation, who initiated the escalation, and whether
the escalation trigger remained legitimate.

Module Operational Role

AEOIM defines the escalation-origin governance layer of AES.

Its role is to preserve visibility of the operational conditions
that caused authority escalation.

Module Operational Space

AEOIM governs:

  • escalation origin
  • escalation triggers
  • escalation-source traceability
  • escalation attribution
  • escalation initiation
  • trigger legitimacy
  • escalation provenance
  • escalation accountability


The module applies wherever governance requires reconstruction
of escalation origins.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • identifiable escalation triggers
  • traceable escalation origins
  • reconstructable escalation history
  • accountable escalation initiation
  • governance-valid escalation attribution
  • operationally valid escalation provenance


Its function is to ensure that governance can determine why
escalation occurred and where it began.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Escalation Origin Object

The organization must define which escalation environments
require origin governance.

This may include:

  • management structures
  • supervisory environments
  • contractor networks
  • compliance systems
  • incident-management systems
  • AI governance frameworks
  • autonomous agent environments
  • Human-AI operational systems


2. Define Escalation Origin Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which escalation
origin remains valid.

This includes:

  • trigger-identification requirements
  • attribution requirements
  • traceability requirements
  • accountability requirements
  • legitimacy requirements
  • governance-valid origin conditions


3. Define Origin Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how escalation-origin failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • unidentified escalation triggers
  • undocumented escalation events
  • unverifiable escalation origins
  • governance-blind escalation
  • hidden escalation paths
  • illegitimate escalation initiation


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
escalation-origin failures.

Governance response may include:

  • escalation review
  • trigger verification
  • governance intervention
  • escalation reconstruction
  • corrective actions
  • escalation-path review
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • escalation triggers
  • escalation initiation events
  • validation activities
  • governance reviews
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting escalation states


An authority-escalation environment must not remain origin-valid if
materially significant escalation events cannot be attributed,
reconstructed, reviewed, validated, or governed.

Use Case 1 — Organizational Incident Escalation

Scenario

A supervisor encounters a safety issue exceeding authorized decision
limits and escalates the issue to senior management.

Application

AEOIM reconstructs the origin of the escalation and identifies the
trigger that initiated escalation.

Result

Governance gains visibility into why escalation occurred and whether
the escalation trigger remained legitimate.

Use Case 2 — Autonomous Agent Escalation

Environment

Scenario

An AI agent encounters a high-risk condition outside its authority
scope and escalates the matter to a supervisory AI system.

Application

AEOIM reconstructs the escalation trigger and preserves traceability
of escalation initiation.

Result

Governance gains visibility into the origin of escalation across
intelligent operational systems.

Canonical Closing Statement

Authority Escalation Origin Integrity Module (AEOIM) defines the
structural conditions under which escalation events remain
attributable to identifiable, legitimate, traceable, governable,
accountable, and operationally valid escalation triggers throughout
the escalation lifecycle.

Every escalation begins with a trigger. Authority escalation origin
integrity therefore becomes a foundational condition of
trustworthy escalation governance.
