Governed Space

Integrity Response Authorization

Canonical Definition

Integrity Response Authorization Module (IRAM) defines the governance
framework for authorizing responses to verified integrity breaches. It
establishes the authorization criteria, decision authorities, approval
mechanisms, accountability requirements, and governance controls required to
ensure that every integrity response is initiated by appropriately authorized
entities and executed under approved governance conditions throughout the
complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

IRAM governs the authorization layer of integrity response management by
ensuring that no integrity response is initiated without documented authority,
approved governance conditions, and accountable decision-making.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Response Authorization Criteria

Identify the governance conditions, breach classifications, severity
thresholds, and operational circumstances that require authorization before
response activities may begin.

2. Define Authorization Authorities

Establish who is authorized to approve, reject, delegate, or escalate
integrity response activities and under which governance conditions those
authorities may act.

3. Define Authorization Validation Logic

Define how response authorizations are verified for legitimacy, completeness,
accountability, policy compliance, and consistency with the approved
governance methodology.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for approved authorizations, denied requests,
delegated authority, emergency authorizations, authorization conflicts, and
escalation procedures.

5. Preserve Authorization Records

Maintain authorization decisions, approvals, delegations, supporting evidence,
timestamps, governance justifications, and audit documentation throughout the
complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Enterprise AI Governance

Scenario

A verified integrity breach requires the immediate suspension of autonomous
decision-making within a production AI platform.

Application

IRAM determines the authorized decision-makers, validates emergency authority,
records the approval, and authorizes containment actions according to the
organization's governance policies.

Result

The response begins under documented authority with complete accountability
and auditability.

Use Case 2 — Critical Infrastructure Operations

Scenario

A verified integrity breach affects operational control systems within a
national power grid.

Application

IRAM authorizes emergency response actions, validates executive authority,
records governance approvals, and ensures that containment measures are
initiated only by authorized personnel.

Result

Critical response activities are executed under controlled governance,
preserving operational integrity, accountability, and regulatory compliance.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity Response Authorization Module establishes the canonical governance
framework for authorizing integrity responses through documented authority,
accountable decision-making, and approved governance conditions. It ensures
that every integrity response begins with legitimate authorization, preserving
governance integrity, operational control, and full auditability throughout
the complete lifecycle of every governed entity.
