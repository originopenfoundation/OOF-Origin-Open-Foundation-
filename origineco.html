A. Standard Abstract

This standard defines the structural transparency conditions under which
environmental origin, material composition, and production process
information become visible within product and production systems.

Origin Eco™ establishes a structural environmental transparency model
focused on origin disclosure, material visibility, and process traceability.

The standard does not replace environmental regulation, certification
schemes, or public law.
It defines transparency conditions for environmental origin and production
structure.

B. Canonical Definition

Origin Eco™ is a structural environmental integrity state in which the
origin of materials, composition of inputs, and core production processes
are documented and made visible within a production system.

Origin Eco™ establishes that environmental credibility begins with
transparent origin and traceable production structure.

C. Structural Base

This standard is based on the following principles:

  • Origin Transparency Principle
  • Material Composition Visibility
  • Production Process Traceability
  • Responsibility Attribution
  • Non-Substitution of Regulatory Authority

Origin Eco™ defines structural transparency conditions rather than
environmental performance metrics.

D. Structural Transparency Requirements

Systems referencing Origin Eco™ must maintain transparent
documentation of the following elements.

1. Origin Disclosure

The primary origin of materials or resources used in production must be
identifiable.

Origin disclosure may include:

  • own cultivation or production
  • supplier origin
  • regional or geographic source
  • imported materials

Where applicable, the origin should allow clear distinction of sourcing
pathways.

2. Material Composition Transparency

Primary materials used in production must be visible and identifiable.

Documentation should allow visibility of:

  • material categories
  • natural or synthetic origin
  • principal inputs used in production

The objective is to establish clear structural visibility of material
composition.

3. Production Process Traceability


Core production processes must be describable and attributable.

Process transparency includes:

  • description of production stages
  • responsible entity

This ensures that production structure is understandable and accountable.

E. Scope

This standard may apply to:

  • agricultural products
  • herbal products
  • food products
  • natural extracts
  • raw materials
  • packaging processes
  • water-dependent production systems
  • soil-based production systems

Origin Eco™ focuses on transparency of origin and production structure
rather than certification of environmental performance.

F. Compatibility

Origin Eco™ may operate alongside other environmental transparency
frameworks including:

  • Water Integrity™
  • Soil Integrity™
  • Carbon Lifecycle Integrity™
  • broader Environmental Integrity Standards

Origin Eco™ functions as a foundational element within the
Environmental Integrity Stack.

Origin Eco™

Minimal Self- Declaration Framework

Framework Type: Implementation Framework
Related Standard: Origin Eco™ Standard
Version: 1.0

Purpose

This framework provides a structured method enabling producers to
declare alignment with the Origin Eco™ Standard through transparent
disclosure of origin, materials, and production processes.

The objective is to establish visible environmental transparency through
producer responsibility.

Implementation Principle

Implementation of the Origin Eco™ Standard at the declaration level
operates through public structural disclosure of origin, material
composition, and production processes.

Responsibility for the declaration remains with the declaring entity.

Declaration Conditions

A product or production process may be declared under the Origin Eco™
Standard when the following information is documented and publicly
accessible.

1. Origin Disclosure

The origin of primary materials or resources used in production must be
declared.

Examples may include:

  • own cultivation or production
  • local supplier sourcing
  • regional sourcing
  • imported materials

Where applicable, geographic origin may be specified.

2. Material Transparency

Primary materials used in the product must be declared.

The declaration should provide visibility of:

  • material categories
  • natural or synthetic origin
  • principal inputs used in production

The objective is structural transparency of material composition.

3. Production Process Transparency

The core production process must be described.

The declaration should include:

  • description of production stages
  • responsible entity

This ensures visibility and responsibility for production structure.

Responsibility Declaration

The producer publishes a public declaration confirming that the
information disclosed reflects the actual production structure.

Example declaration:

“This product is declared under the Origin Eco™ Standard. Origin of
materials, material composition, and production process transparency are
publicly disclosed under producer responsibility.”

Documentation Requirements

Implementation typically requires:

  • one origin disclosure sheet
  • one material transparency sheet
  • one production process description
  • one responsibility declaration

The information should remain publicly accessible.

Structural Benefit

The declaration framework transforms:

implicit environmental claims
into
structurally documented environmental transparency.

This strengthens:

  • supply chain clarity
  • customer trust
  • regulatory defensibility
  • environmental accountability.

Closing Principle

Environmental credibility begins with transparent origin.







About Origin Eco™ Standard

Origin Eco™ Standard establishes a structural transparency model for
environmental origin in production systems.

The standard focuses on three fundamental elements that define
environmental credibility in production:

  • origin of materials
  • material composition
  • production process transparency

Origin Eco™ introduces a simple principle: environmental credibility
should be based on visible origin and transparent production
structure, not on marketing claims.

Canonical Definition

Origin Eco™ is the structural environmental integrity condition in which
the origin of materials, the composition of inputs, and the core production
processes are documented and made visible within a production system.

The standard establishes environmental transparency through origin
disclosure and traceable production structure.

What Origin Eco™ Is

Origin Eco™ is a structural transparency standard designed to make the
environmental origin of products and production systems visible.

It focuses on:

  • documenting where materials originate
  • identifying what materials are used
  • describing how products are produced

The standard introduces a baseline of environmental transparency that
allows production systems to demonstrate structural credibility.

What Origin Eco™ Is Not

Origin Eco™ is not:

  • a certification label
  • a marketing eco badge
  • a sustainability scoring system
  • a regulatory compliance scheme

The standard does not assign grades or ratings.

Instead, it establishes structural transparency conditions that allow
environmental origin to be clearly documented.

Structural Difference

Most environmental labeling systems focus on claims, ratings, or
certification processes.

Origin Eco™ focuses on structural visibility of origin and production.

Rather than relying on:

  • marketing language
  • sustainability narratives
  • complex certification frameworks

Origin Eco™ introduces a simpler principle:

Environmental credibility begins with transparent origin.

Long-Term Stability

Origin Eco™ is designed to remain valid regardless of technological or
regulatory change.

Because the standard defines structural transparency conditions, not
specific technologies or environmental thresholds, it remains applicable
across different production systems and time periods.

The principles of:

  • origin disclosure
  • material transparency
  • production traceability

remain relevant regardless of industry evolution.

Why the Standard Matters

Environmental claims are becoming increasingly common in modern
markets.

However, without structural transparency, such claims often remain
unverifiable.

Origin Eco™ establishes a minimal structural foundation that allows
environmental origin and production structure to become visible.

This strengthens:

  • supply chain credibility
  • consumer trust
  • regulatory defensibility
  • long-term environmental accountability.

Closing Perspective

Environmental integrity begins with transparent origin.

Origin Eco™ establishes the structural conditions that make that
transparency possible.
