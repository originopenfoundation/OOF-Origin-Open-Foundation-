About the Origin Declaration Standard (ODS™)

OOF™ Origin Open Foundation™

Independent Methodological Authority

Standard: Origin Declaration Standard (ODS™)
Category: AI and Interpretation


What This Standard Is

The Origin Declaration Standard (ODS™) defines a simple and universal way
to declare the origin of digital content.

It establishes whether content is:

  • human generated
  • AI generated
  • or undeclared

ODS™ does not analyze content.
ODS™ defines its declared state.

What This Standard Is Not

ODS™ is not:

  • a detection system
  • an AI classifier
  • a content moderation tool
  • a quality evaluation framework

It does not decide what is true, good, or acceptable.

It defines only one thing:

whether origin is declared or not.

What You Gain

ODS™ creates a new layer in digital content:

  • clarity instead of assumption
  • declaration instead of guessing
  • responsibility instead of anonymity

It transforms content from:

  • unknown → declared
  • assumed → defined

Why This Matters Now

Digital content has changed.

AI can generate video, voice, and identity at scale.
Human and synthetic content are no longer distinguishable.

Without declaration:

  • trust collapses
  • responsibility disappears
  • monetization becomes ambiguous

ODS™ restores a single missing element:

declared origin

Why It Works

ODS™ is designed to be:

  • extremely simple
  • globally applicable
  • independent of technology
  • usable without infrastructure

Only three states.
One decision.
Immediate clarity.

Fastest Standard to Implement

ODS™ is one of the fastest standards to implement.

No system integration is required.
No technical validation is required.

Implementation requires only:

  • one declaration
  • one line of text

This allows instant adoption across:

  • creators
  • platforms
  • media systems

Use Case 1 — Platform Revenue Protection

Scenario
Platforms host large volumes of undeclared AI-generated content.

Problem

  • unclear origin
  • copyright conflicts
  • advertiser risk
  • loss of trust

Application

  • creators declare origin using ODS™
  • undeclared content defaults to UGC
  • monetization is restricted without declaration

Result

  • reduction of monetization abuse
  • clearer advertiser environment
  • protection of platform revenue
  • scalable governance without detection

Use Case 2 — Creator Monetization Clarity

Scenario
Creators produce original content but compete with undeclared AI content.

Problem

  • unfair competition
  • unclear ownership
  • monetization dilution

Application

  • creators declare origin (HGC / AGC)
  • responsibility is assigned
  • monetization eligibility is enabled

Result

  • fair monetization conditions
  • clear ownership signal
  • increased trust with audience and platforms

Value for Creators

  • clear ownership signal
  • monetization eligibility
  • differentiation from undeclared content
  • long-term trust with audience

Value for Platforms

  • reduced legal and compliance risk
  • protection against monetization abuse
  • scalable transparency without detection systems
  • alignment with emerging AI regulation

Why It Is Simple

ODS™ does not attempt to solve everything.

It solves one problem:

origin must be declared.

Everything else remains unchanged.

Closing Statement

Content is easy to create.
Trust is not.

Canonical Line

If origin is declared, value can be created.
If origin is unknown, value cannot be claimed.
