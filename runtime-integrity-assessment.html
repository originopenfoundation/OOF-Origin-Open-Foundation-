What Is Runtime Integrity Assessment™?

Runtime Integrity Assessment™ (RIA™) is an
independent OOF® assessment service that evaluates whether
governance integrity is preserved during the real-world operation of
AI systems, autonomous systems, digital processes, operational
environments, and intelligent infrastructures.

Unlike traditional assessments that primarily evaluate system
design, implementation, or technical compliance before deployment,
Runtime Integrity Assessment™ evaluates how systems actually operate
under real-world conditions.

The objective is not to verify intentions.

The objective is to verify operational reality.

Why This Service Exists

Modern operational systems continuously make decisions, execute
workflows, coordinate autonomous behavior, interact with humans, and
adapt to changing operational environments.

Even well-designed systems may gradually deviate from their intended
governance conditions during operation.

Runtime Integrity Assessment™ exists to determine whether governance
integrity remains preserved after deployment.

Core Question

Does the system remain governable, trustworthy, traceable,
explainable, and operationally aligned while operating under
real-world conditions?

Assessment Scope

Runtime Integrity Assessment™ may be applied to:

  • Digital Business Processes
  • Workflow Automation
  • Enterprise Operational Platforms
  • Critical Infrastructure
  • Intelligent Operational Systems


The methodology is applicable wherever governance integrity must be
preserved throughout runtime operation.

Assessment Boundaries

Runtime Integrity Assessment™ is not a software certification
service.

It does not replace:

  • penetration testing,
  • cybersecurity assessments,
  • software quality assurance,
  • functional software testing,
  • infrastructure monitoring.


Instead, it evaluates whether governance integrity remains preserved
while systems operate under real-world conditions.

Methodology Independence

Runtime Integrity Assessment™ is methodology-driven rather than
technology-dependent.

The assessment may be applied across different vendors, software
platforms, AI models, operational architectures, and technology
environments.

The methodology evaluates governance integrity rather than specific
technologies.
