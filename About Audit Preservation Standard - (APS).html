About Audit Preservation Standard - (APS)

Understanding Audit Preservation StandardWhat Is Audit Preservation Standard?

Audit Preservation Standard is the ninth Parent Standard of ADIT® — Continuous
Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture™. It governs how audit evidence,
execution records, findings, responses, decisions, approvals, and governance
history are preserved throughout their complete lifecycle.

Audit Preservation Standard recognizes that an audit does not end when
corrective actions are completed. The long-term value of an audit depends on
whether its complete history remains preserved, accessible, trustworthy,
traceable, and independently reconstructable whenever future governance,
regulatory, legal, or operational review becomes necessary. Canonical
Definition

Audit Preservation Standard defines the governance conditions required to
preserve audit evidence, execution records, findings, responses, decisions,
approvals, and supporting governance information throughout the complete audit
lifecycle. It establishes preservation methodology, lifecycle controls,
retention requirements, governance mechanisms, preservation validation, and
continuous preservation assurance necessary to ensure that audit history
remains complete, accessible, trustworthy, traceable, and independently
reconstructable over time. Why Audit Preservation Standard Exists

Organizations frequently rely on historical audit information long after
operational events have ended.

Without standardized preservation, audit records may become incomplete,
inaccessible, corrupted, or impossible to reconstruct. This weakens
governance, accountability, regulatory compliance, and organizational
learning.

Audit Preservation Standard exists to ensure that the complete audit history
remains preserved and trustworthy throughout its required lifecycle. What
Audit Preservation Standard Governs

Audit Preservation Standard governs:

  • Audit preservation
  • Audit record retention
  • Audit archives
  • Audit continuity
  • Audit history
  • Audit retrieval
  • Preservation assurance

Audit Preservation Standard establishes the canonical governance framework for
