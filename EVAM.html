Evidence Accuracy

Canonical Definition

Evidence Accuracy Module defines the governance framework for determining
whether governed evidence correctly represents the operational reality it
claims to describe. It establishes accuracy principles, validation
requirements, verification controls, governance mechanisms, and continuous
accuracy assurance necessary to identify and prevent inaccurate, misleading,
or erroneous evidence throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

Evidence Accuracy Module governs the accuracy layer of evidence verification
by ensuring that governed evidence objectively reflects actual operational
events, conditions, activities, and outcomes.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Accuracy Requirements

Identify accuracy criteria, acceptable tolerances, verification objectives,
governance requirements, and operational conditions necessary to evaluate
evidence accuracy.

2. Define Accuracy Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for measuring, validating, documenting, and
continuously maintaining evidence accuracy throughout the verification
process.

3. Define Accuracy Validation Logic

Define how evidence accuracy is evaluated by comparing evidence against
operational reality, authoritative sources, validated records, and established
governance criteria.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for inaccurate evidence, detected discrepancies,
validation failures, conflicting information, and corrective governance
measures.

5. Preserve Accuracy Records

Maintain accuracy assessments, validation history, governance decisions,
supporting evidence, timestamps, and corrective actions throughout the
complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An autonomous AI platform generates operational evidence describing decisions
and actions performed by multiple intelligent agents.

Application

Evidence Accuracy Module verifies that every evidence record faithfully
represents the actual operational events without omission, distortion, or
error.

Result

Only evidence that accurately reflects operational reality proceeds to audit
execution and governance decision-making.

Use Case 2 — Financial Audit

Scenario

Auditors evaluate financial evidence submitted by an organization before
issuing formal audit findings.

Application

Evidence Accuracy Module verifies that transactions, balances, approvals, and
supporting records accurately represent actual financial operations.

Result

Audit conclusions are based on evidence that objectively reflects operational
and financial reality.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Accuracy Module establishes the canonical governance framework for
verifying the accuracy of governed evidence throughout its complete lifecycle.
By governing accuracy validation, objective comparison with operational
reality, governance responses, and continuous accuracy assurance, the module
enables reliable audit, operational reconstruction, accountable governance,
and independent verification across all operational domains.
