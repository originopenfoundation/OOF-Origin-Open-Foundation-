About Evidence Correlation Standard - (ECS)

Understanding Evidence Correlation StandardWhat Is Evidence Correlation Standard?

Evidence Correlation Standard is the fourth Parent Standard of ADIT® —
Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture™. It governs how
independently governed evidence is connected, contextualized, sequenced, and
related to reconstruct operational reality while preserving the integrity of
every individual evidence object.

Evidence Correlation Standard recognizes that trustworthy evidence alone is
often insufficient to explain what actually happened. Operational
understanding emerges when multiple evidence objects are accurately connected
through governed relationships, chronology, dependencies, context, and
causality. Canonical Definition

Evidence Correlation Standard defines the governance conditions required to
establish, validate, manage, and preserve relationships between multiple
governed evidence objects. It establishes evidence correlation principles,
relationship rules, dependency validation, contextual linkage, correlation
governance, and continuous correlation assurance necessary to reconstruct
operational reality from trustworthy evidence throughout the complete
lifecycle of a governed entity. Why Evidence Correlation Standard Exists

Operational events rarely produce only one piece of evidence.

Organizations must combine logs, documents, decisions, transactions,
identities, communications, and system records into a coherent representation
of operational reality. Without governed correlation, evidence remains
fragmented and important relationships may be overlooked or misunderstood.

Evidence Correlation Standard exists to ensure that trustworthy evidence can
be accurately connected without compromising its integrity. What Evidence
Correlation Standard Governs

Evidence Correlation Standard governs:

  • Evidence relationships
  • Evidence linkage
  • Contextual association
  • Chronological correlation
  • Dependency mapping
  • Causal relationships
  • Evidence network integrity
