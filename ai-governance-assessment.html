What Is AI Governance Assessment™?

AI Governance Assessment™ is a professional governance intelligence
service focused on evaluating whether AI systems, autonomous agents,
AI-powered products, and intelligent environments operate under
appropriate governance, accountability, auditability, transparency,
human oversight, and operational control structures.

Organizations increasingly deploy AI systems into:

  • business operations,
  • customer interactions,
  • decision support,
  • autonomous workflows,
  • intelligent products,
  • agent ecosystems.


However, many organizations cannot confidently answer:

  • Who is accountable for AI decisions?
  • How are AI actions governed?
  • Can AI outputs be audited?
  • How is memory managed?
  • How are risks detected and escalated?
  • How is human oversight maintained?
  • Can the AI system be trusted?


AI Governance Assessment™ exists to help organizations answer these
questions through structured governance analysis.

Why This Service Exists

Many organizations focus on:

AI Capability™

while failing to evaluate:

AI Governability™

As AI systems become increasingly autonomous, governance becomes
increasingly important.

Without appropriate governance, organizations may face:

  • accountability gaps,
  • operational risks,
  • regulatory exposure,
  • undocumented decision processes,
  • uncontrolled AI behavior,
  • memory integrity risks,
  • escalation failures,
  • loss of human oversight,
  • reduced transparency,
  • increased trust risks.


AI Governance Assessment™ was created to identify and evaluate these
governance conditions before governance failures occur.

Architecture Foundations

AI Governance Assessment™ utilizes multiple OOF® governance
architectures and standards.

CLIA® — Cognitive Governance Intelligence Architecture™

Used to evaluate cognition, interpretation, reasoning integrity, and
cognitive governance conditions.

Core Question:

How does the AI system think and interpret?

MGIA™ — Memory Governance Intelligence Architecture™

Used to evaluate memory integrity, memory governance, memory
traceability, and memory utilization.

Core Question:

How does the AI system remember?

AGA™ — Accountability Governance Architecture™

Used to evaluate accountability structures, responsibility
allocation, authority relationships, and consequence ownership.

Core Question:

Who is accountable?

AIG™ — AI Governance Architecture™

Used to evaluate behavioral governance, behavioral controls,
monitoring mechanisms, and behavioral risks.

Core Question:

How does the AI system behave?

ASGA™ — Autonomous Systems Governance Architecture™

Used to evaluate autonomous operation, escalation mechanisms,
control structures, and governability of autonomous systems.

Core Question:

Can autonomous operation remain governable?

RIS™ — Runtime Integrity Standard™

Used to evaluate runtime integrity, operational conditions, control
effectiveness, and operational validation.

Core Question:

Does operational reality match governance assumptions?
