Canonical Definition

Autonomous Identity Verification Module (AIVM) defines the
structural conditions under which the identity of an autonomous
operational actor can be verified before autonomous operations are
authorized, executed, or coordinated.

AIVM governs autonomous identity verification.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every autonomous operational actor can be independently
verified before participating in autonomous operations.

Identity claimed is not identity verified.

AIVM governs that verification.

Module Operational Role

AIVM defines the identity verification layer of AIS.

Its role is to verify the authenticity of every autonomous
operational actor before autonomous operation begins.

Module Operational Space

AIVM governs:

  • autonomous identity verification,
  • identity authenticity,
  • operational identity validation,
  • identity confirmation,
  • trusted operational identity,
  • verification governance,
  • operational identity assurance,
  • identity trust.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must ensure that
autonomous operational identities are authentic, verifiable,
trustworthy, and governance-valid before operational authority is
granted.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Identity Verification Object

Identify which autonomous operational identities require
verification.

2. Define Verification Conditions

Establish the governance conditions required to verify operational
identity.

3. Define Verification Failure Logic

Identify conditions under which identity verification fails or
cannot be completed.

4. Define Governance Response

Define the governance actions when verification succeeds, fails, or
requires escalation.

5. Preserve Verification Traceability

Preserve evidence, verification results, governance decisions, and
operational records supporting identity verification.

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Delivery Robot

Scenario

A delivery robot requests authorization to begin autonomous
operations.

Application

AIVM verifies the robot's operational identity before authority is
granted.

Result

Only verified autonomous operational actors may begin governed
operations.

Use Case 2 — Multi-Agent AI Environment

Scenario

Multiple autonomous AI agents cooperate to complete a complex
operational objective.

Application

AIVM verifies the operational identity of each participating
autonomous agent before coordination begins.

Result

The organization establishes trusted autonomous collaboration based
on verified operational identities.

Canonical Closing Statement

Trustworthy autonomy begins with trusted identity. Autonomous
Identity Verification ensures that every autonomous operation starts
with an operational actor whose identity can be confidently verified
before independent action begins.
