ArtData Diagnostics™

Governance Architecture Space

ArtData Diagnostics™ governs the architectural space responsible for
consolidating, evaluating, interpreting, assessing, documenting, and
communicating findings generated throughout the ArtData® lifecycle.

The space exists because qualification, registration, intelligence
generation, value assessment, exchange activities, and governance
mechanisms individually provide valuable insights. However,
organizations ultimately require a unified understanding of what
those insights collectively mean.

ArtData Diagnostics™ exists to transform governed evidence assets
into governance intelligence.

Canonical Definition

ArtData Diagnostics™ is the governable architectural space
responsible for consolidating, evaluating, interpreting, assessing,
documenting, and communicating findings generated through ArtData
Qualification™, ArtData Registry™, ArtData Intelligence™, ArtData
Value™, ArtData Exchange™, and ArtData Governance™ in order to
produce evidence-based governance intelligence.

The Core Problem

Organizations frequently possess:

  • qualified evidence,
  • evidence registries,
  • intelligence outputs,
  • value assessments,
  • governance frameworks,
  • exchange activities,


while lacking a mechanism capable of determining the overall health,
maturity, effectiveness, value, readiness, and governance confidence
of the ArtData® ecosystem.

As a result:

  • governance visibility decreases,
  • evidence value becomes unclear,
  • intelligence utilization becomes inconsistent,
  • strategic understanding weakens.


ArtData Diagnostics™ was created to address this challenge.

Why ArtData Diagnostics™ Exists

ArtData® generates:

  • qualified evidence,
  • evidence intelligence,
  • governance value,
  • evidence ecosystems,
  • evidence governance structures.


However, organizations still require a mechanism capable of
answering a critical question:

  • What does the entire ArtData® ecosystem tell us?


ArtData Diagnostics™ exists to answer this question.
