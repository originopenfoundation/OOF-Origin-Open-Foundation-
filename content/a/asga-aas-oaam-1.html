Canonical Definition

Operational Authority Assignment Module (OAAM) defines the
structural conditions under which autonomous operational authority
is legitimately assigned to an autonomous operational actor before
independent operations may begin.

OAAM governs operational authority assignment.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that autonomous operational authority is explicitly assigned,
governance-approved, and operationally justified before autonomous
execution is authorized.

Identity establishes the operational actor.

Authority assignment establishes operational permission.

OAAM governs that assignment.

Module Operational Role

OAAM defines the authority assignment layer of AAS.

Its role is to establish legitimate operational authority before
autonomous systems perform independent operations.

Module Operational Space

OAAM governs:

  • operational authority assignment,
  • authority allocation,
  • governance-approved authority,
  • operational permissions,
  • authority establishment,
  • authority attribution,
  • operational authorization,
  • governed authority assignment.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must formally assign
operational authority to autonomous operational actors before
autonomous execution begins.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Authority Assignment Object

Identify which autonomous operational actors require assigned
operational authority.

2. Define Assignment Conditions

Establish the governance conditions required before operational
authority may be assigned.

3. Define Assignment Failure Logic

Identify conditions where authority assignment becomes invalid,
incomplete, unauthorized, or governance-noncompliant.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for authority approval, rejection,
reassignment, or escalation.

5. Preserve Assignment Traceability

Preserve authority assignments, governance approvals, operational
records, and supporting evidence throughout the authority lifecycle.

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Warehouse Robot

Scenario

A newly deployed warehouse robot is prepared for autonomous
operation.

Application

OAAM assigns governance-approved operational authority before the
robot performs independent tasks.

Result

The robot begins operation only after legitimate authority has been
formally established.

Use Case 2 — Enterprise AI Agent Platform

Scenario

An organization deploys autonomous AI agents to perform business
operations.

Application

OAAM assigns operational authority according to organizational
governance policies before execution begins.

Result

Every autonomous operational actor performs only those activities
for which legitimate operational authority has been explicitly
assigned.

Canonical Closing Statement

Legitimate autonomy begins with legitimate authority. Operational
Authority Assignment ensures that every autonomous operational actor
receives clearly defined, governance-approved authority before
independent operation begins.
