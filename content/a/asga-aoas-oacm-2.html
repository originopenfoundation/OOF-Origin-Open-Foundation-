Canonical Definition

Operational Accountability Continuity Module (OACM) defines the
structural conditions under which accountability remains continuous,
uninterrupted, and governable throughout the complete autonomous
operational lifecycle.

OACM governs operational accountability continuity.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that accountability is never lost, interrupted, or left undefined
during autonomous operation, operational transitions, or governance
changes.

Accountability begins with attribution.

It must continue until the operation is fully completed.

OACM governs that continuity.

Module Operational Role

OACM defines the accountability continuity layer of AOAS.

Its role is to preserve uninterrupted accountability across every
stage of autonomous operation.

Module Operational Space

OACM governs:

  • operational accountability continuity,
  • continuous accountability,
  • accountability lifecycle,
  • accountability preservation,
  • governance continuity,
  • uninterrupted responsibility,
  • accountable operational transitions,
  • continuous governance responsibility.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must ensure that
accountability remains continuously preserved before, during, and
after autonomous operations.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Accountability Continuity Object

Identify which autonomous operations require continuous
accountability.

2. Define Continuity Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing accountability throughout
the operational lifecycle.

3. Define Continuity Failure Logic

Identify conditions where accountability becomes interrupted,
ambiguous, transferred incorrectly, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for accountability preservation,
reassignment, operational review, escalation, or governance
correction.

5. Preserve Accountability Traceability

Preserve accountability records, governance decisions, operational
evidence, continuity history, and supporting documentation.

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Logistics Network

Scenario

An autonomous shipment passes through multiple autonomous warehouses
and transport systems.

Application

OACM preserves accountability throughout every operational
transition without creating responsibility gaps.

Result

The organization maintains continuous governance accountability
across the complete logistics lifecycle.

Use Case 2 — Enterprise Autonomous AI Platform

Scenario

An autonomous business process is transferred between several
specialized AI agents during execution.

Application

OACM ensures accountability remains continuous despite operational
handovers.

Result

Responsibility is preserved from the beginning of the process until
final completion.

Canonical Closing Statement

Governable autonomy requires continuous accountability. Operational
Accountability Continuity ensures that responsibility is never
interrupted, never abandoned, and remains continuously governed
throughout the complete autonomous operational lifecycle.
