Canonical Definition

Accountability Chain Traceability Integrity Module (ACTIM) defines
the structural conditions under which accountability-chain
relationships, accountability transitions, accountability transfers,
accountability delegations, accountability validations,
accountability records, accountability decisions, accountability
outcomes, and accountability- governance activities remain
traceable, reconstructable, reviewable, auditable, governable,
enforceable, and operationally valid throughout
the accountability-chain lifecycle.

ACTIM governs accountability-chain traceability.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine how accountability traveled through the chain, which
governance actors participated in accountability transitions, how
accountability relationships evolved, and whether the complete
accountability chain can be reconstructed.

Module Operational Role

ACTIM defines the chain-traceability governance layer of ACS-C.

Its role is to preserve visibility and reconstructability of
accountability chains throughout governance environments.

Module Operational Space

ACTIM governs:

  • accountability-chain traceability
  • accountability-chain reconstruction
  • accountability auditability
  • accountability-chain governance
  • accountability-transition traceability
  • accountability-history reconstruction
  • governance-chain traceability
  • multi-layer accountability visibility


The module applies wherever governance requires reconstruction
of accountability-chain history.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • traceable accountability chains
  • reconstructable accountability history
  • auditable accountability records
  • accountable governance transitions
  • governance-valid chain documentation
  • operational accountability transparency


Its function is to ensure that governance can reconstruct how
accountability traveled across multiple governance layers.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Chain Traceability Object

The organization must define which accountability-chain environments
require traceability governance.

This may include:

  • contractor networks
  • subcontractor structures
  • corporate groups
  • holding-company ecosystems
  • regulatory environments
  • AI governance systems
  • Human-AI operational environments
  • multi-layer governance ecosystems


2. Define Chain Traceability Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which
accountability-chain traceability remains valid.

This includes:

  • traceability requirements
  • reconstruction requirements
  • auditability requirements
  • accountability-history requirements
  • governance requirements
  • chain-traceability conditions


3. Define Traceability Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how accountability-chain traceability
failures are identified.

This may include:

  • missing accountability records
  • undocumented accountability transfers
  • incomplete accountability history
  • accountability-chain gaps
  • governance-blind accountability transitions
  • unreconstructable accountability pathways


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
accountability-chain traceability failures.

Governance response may include:

  • chain review
  • audit procedures
  • governance intervention
  • chain reconstruction
  • corrective actions
  • traceability verification
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • accountability transitions
  • accountability transfers
  • accountability validations
  • governance reviews
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting chain states


An accountability-chain environment must not remain
traceability-valid if materially significant accountability-chain
activities cannot be reconstructed, reviewed, audited, validated,
enforced, preserved, or governed.

Investigation

Scenario

structure involving several organizations.

Application

ACTIM reconstructs accountability transfers, governance decisions,
accountability transitions, and accountability-history pathways
throughout the contractor chain.

Result

accountability-chain pathway.

Result
