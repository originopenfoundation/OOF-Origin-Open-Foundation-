Canonical Definition

Operational Conflict Resolution Module (OCRM) defines the structural
conditions under which operational conflicts between autonomous
operational actors are identified, evaluated, resolved, and governed
while preserving operational consistency, governance integrity, and
coordinated autonomous operation.

OCRM governs operational conflict resolution.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that conflicts between autonomous operational actors are resolved in
a controlled, traceable, and governance-approved manner.

Coordination may create conflicts.

Governance must resolve them.

OCRM governs that resolution.

Module Operational Role

OCRM defines the operational conflict resolution layer of ACOS.

Its role is to preserve coordinated autonomous operation by
governing how operational conflicts are detected and resolved.

Module Operational Space

OCRM governs:

  • operational conflict resolution,
  • autonomous operational conflicts,
  • coordination conflict management,
  • conflict evaluation,
  • governed conflict resolution,
  • operational decision conflicts,
  • collaborative conflict handling,
  • operational consistency restoration.


Module Function

The module applies wherever autonomous operational actors may
generate conflicting actions, priorities, objectives, or operational
decisions requiring governance intervention.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Conflict Object

Identify which operational conflicts require governed resolution.

2. Define Conflict Resolution Conditions Establish governance
conditions governing conflict identification, evaluation,
prioritization, and resolution.

3. Define Conflict Failure Logic

Identify conditions where conflicts remain unresolved, escalate
unnecessarily, recur, or become governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for conflict resolution, operational
reprioritization, coordination adjustment, escalation, or human
intervention.

5. Preserve Conflict Traceability

Preserve conflict records, governance decisions, operational
evidence, resolution history, and supporting documentation.

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Warehouse Fleet

Scenario

Two autonomous transport robots simultaneously attempt to access the
same loading zone.

Application

OCRM governs conflict resolution by assigning operational priority
and coordinating safe task execution.

Result

The conflict is resolved without disrupting warehouse operations or
governance integrity.

Use Case 2 — Multi-Agent AI Operations Platform

Scenario

Multiple autonomous AI agents generate conflicting operational
recommendations for the same business process.

Application

OCRM evaluates the conflict and applies governance-approved
resolution rules before execution continues.

Result

Enterprise operations remain coordinated, consistent, and
operationally trustworthy.

Canonical Closing Statement

Governable coordination requires governable conflict resolution.
Operational Conflict Resolution ensures that conflicts between
autonomous operational actors are resolved consistently,
transparently, and under continuous governance, preserving reliable
autonomous collaboration.
