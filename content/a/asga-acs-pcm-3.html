Canonical Definition

Policy Constraint Module (PCM) defines the structural conditions
under which organizational, regulatory, legal, ethical, and
operational policies are transformed into enforceable autonomous
operational constraints.

PCM governs policy constraints.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that autonomous systems continuously operate in accordance with
approved organizational and governance policies.

Policies define expectations.

Policy constraints define operational limits.

PCM governs that translation.

Module Operational Role

PCM defines the policy constraint layer of ACS.

Its role is to ensure that governance policies are consistently
reflected in autonomous operational behavior.

Module Operational Space

PCM governs:

  • policy constraints,
  • governance policy implementation,
  • operational policy enforcement,
  • organizational policy constraints,
  • regulatory operational constraints,
  • policy compliance,
  • policy-based operational limits,
  • governed policy execution.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must convert governance
policies into enforceable operational constraints for autonomous
systems.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Policy Constraint Object

Identify which governance policies require operational
implementation.

2. Define Policy Constraint Conditions

Establish governance conditions translating approved policies into
operational constraints.

3. Define Policy Constraint Failure Logic

Identify conditions where policy constraints become incomplete,
inconsistent, outdated, bypassed, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for policy updates, operational
restriction, policy enforcement, escalation, or review.

5. Preserve Policy Traceability

Preserve policy definitions, governance approvals, implementation
records, operational evidence, and policy revision history.

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Healthcare Assistant

Scenario

An autonomous healthcare assistant processes patient information
within a hospital.

Application

PCM converts healthcare privacy and organizational policies into
operational constraints governing autonomous behavior.

Result The system operates consistently with organizational
governance and regulatory requirements.

Use Case 2 — Autonomous Financial Compliance Agent

Scenario

An autonomous AI agent performs financial compliance reviews for a
multinational organization.

Application

PCM translates internal governance policies and regulatory
obligations into enforceable operational constraints.

Result

Autonomous operations remain aligned with governance policies across
all compliance activities.

Canonical Closing Statement

Governed autonomy requires governed policies. Policy Constraint
ensures that organizational and regulatory policies become
enforceable operational limits, preserving compliant, trustworthy,
and continuously governed autonomous operation.
