Canonical Definition

Authority Delegation Continuity Integrity Module (ADCIM) defines the
structural conditions under which delegated authority remains
continuously traceable, reconstructable, governable, accountable,
and operationally valid throughout transfer, succession, escalation,
inheritance, reassignment, organizational change, and
operational transition.

ADCIM governs delegation continuity.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine whether delegated authority remained preserved,
transferred, inherited, reassigned, suspended, revoked, or
terminated throughout its lifecycle.

Module Operational Role

ADCIM defines the delegation-continuity governance layer of ADS.

Its role is to preserve visibility of delegated authority throughout
operational change and governance transitions.

Module Operational Space

ADCIM governs:

  • delegation continuity
  • delegated-authority transfer
  • delegation succession
  • delegation reassignment
  • delegation lifecycle governance
  • continuity accountability
  • delegation traceability
  • authority-transition governance


The module applies wherever delegated authority changes over time.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • continuous delegation visibility
  • reconstructable delegation history
  • accountable authority transitions
  • governance-valid delegation continuity
  • traceable authority movement
  • operational delegation preservation


Its function is to ensure that governance can determine what
happened to delegated authority throughout its lifecycle.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Delegation Continuity Object

The organization must define which delegated-authority environments
require continuity governance.

This may include:

  • management delegation structures
  • supervisory delegation chains
  • contractor governance systems
  • regulatory delegation environments
  • AI orchestration systems
  • autonomous governance structures
  • Human-AI governance environments
  • distributed authority systems


2. Define Delegation Continuity Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which delegation
continuity remains valid.

This includes:

  • continuity requirements
  • transfer requirements
  • succession requirements
  • traceability requirements
  • accountability requirements
  • governance-valid continuity conditions


3. Define Continuity Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how delegation-continuity failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • undocumented delegation transfers
  • delegation gaps
  • continuity breaks
  • hidden reassignment events
  • governance discontinuities
  • delegation lifecycle ambiguity


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
delegation-continuity failures.

Governance response may include:

  • continuity review
  • delegation reconstruction
  • governance intervention
  • escalation
  • corrective actions
  • delegation verification
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • delegation transfers
  • reassignment events
  • succession activities
  • governance reviews
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting delegation structures


A delegation-governance environment must not remain continuity-valid
if materially significant delegation transitions cannot be
reconstructed, reviewed, validated, or governed.

Use Case 1 — Organizational Delegation Chain

Scenario

A department manager delegates authority to a supervisor. The
supervisor is later replaced and authority is reassigned to
a successor.

Application

ADCIM reconstructs continuity of delegated authority throughout
the transition process.

Result

Governance gains visibility into how delegated authority evolved and
whether continuity remained preserved.

Use Case 2 — Multi-Agent Governance

Environment

Scenario

An orchestration system reassigns delegated authority between
supervisory agents during operational restructuring.

Application

ADCIM governs continuity and traceability of delegated authority
throughout the reassignment lifecycle.

Result

Governance gains visibility into delegated-authority evolution
across intelligent operational systems.

Canonical Closing Statement
