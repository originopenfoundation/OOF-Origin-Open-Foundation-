Canonical Definition

Authority Escalation Traceability Integrity Module (AETIM) defines
the structural conditions under which authority escalation
activities remain traceable, reconstructable, reviewable, auditable,
attributable, governable, and operationally valid throughout
the escalation lifecycle.

AETIM governs escalation traceability.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine how escalation occurred, when escalation occurred, who
initiated escalation, who received escalation, what evidence
supported escalation, and how escalation was resolved.

Module Operational Role

AETIM defines the escalation-traceability governance layer of AES.

Its role is to preserve visibility and reconstructability of
escalation pathways throughout governance environments.

Module Operational Space

AETIM governs:

  • escalation traceability
  • escalation reconstruction
  • escalation auditability
  • escalation-path visibility
  • escalation history
  • escalation evidence linkage
  • escalation decision traceability
  • governance traceability


The module applies wherever governance requires reconstruction
of escalation activities.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • traceable escalation activities
  • reconstructable escalation history
  • auditable escalation decisions
  • accountable escalation processes
  • governance-valid escalation records
  • operational escalation transparency


Its function is to ensure that governance can reconstruct and review
escalation activities at any point in time.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Escalation Traceability Object

The organization must define which escalation environments
require traceability governance.

This may include:

  • management escalation systems
  • compliance escalation environments
  • incident-management systems
  • contractor governance structures
  • safety escalation frameworks
  • AI governance systems
  • autonomous-agent environments
  • Human-AI governance systems


2. Define Escalation Traceability Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which escalation
traceability remains valid.

This includes:

  • traceability requirements
  • reconstruction requirements
  • evidence requirements
  • accountability requirements
  • auditability requirements
  • governance-valid traceability conditions


3. Define Traceability Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how escalation-traceability failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • missing escalation records
  • undocumented escalation events
  • incomplete escalation history
  • evidence gaps
  • governance-blind escalation activities
  • unreconstructable escalation paths


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
escalation-traceability failures.

Governance response may include:

  • escalation review
  • audit procedures
  • governance intervention
  • escalation reconstruction
  • corrective actions
  • pathway verification
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • escalation activities
  • escalation decisions
  • escalation evidence
  • governance reviews
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting escalation states


An authority-escalation environment must not remain
traceability-valid if materially significant escalation activities
cannot be reconstructed, reviewed, audited, validated, or governed.

Use Case 1 — Safety Incident Escalation

Environment

Scenario

A serious safety incident is escalated through multiple management
levels before a final resolution is reached.

Application

AETIM reconstructs the escalation path, escalation decisions,
evidence chain, and final resolution process.

Result

Governance gains visibility into the complete escalation lifecycle.

Use Case 2 — AI Escalation Environment

Scenario

An autonomous agent escalates a high-risk decision through multiple
supervisory layers before reaching a human authority.

Application

AETIM preserves traceability of escalation events, escalation
decisions, evidence, and governance actions.

Result

Governance gains visibility into escalation pathways across
intelligent operational systems.

Canonical Closing Statement

Authority Escalation Traceability Integrity Module (AETIM) defines
the structural conditions under which authority escalation
activities remain traceable, reconstructable, reviewable, auditable,
attributable, governable, and operationally valid throughout
the escalation lifecycle.

Escalation that cannot be reconstructed cannot be trusted. Authority
escalation traceability integrity therefore becomes a foundational
condition of trustworthy escalation governance.
