Canonical Definition

Accountability Continuity Origin Integrity Module (ACOIM) defines
the structural conditions under which accountability-continuity
relationships remain attributable to identifiable, legitimate,
traceable, governable, reconstructable, reviewable, accountable, and
operationally valid accountability sources throughout
the accountability lifecycle.

ACOIM governs accountability-continuity origin.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine where accountability continuity originated, which
accountability relationship is being preserved, and whether the
continuity relationship remains connected to a
legitimate accountability source.

Module Operational Role

ACOIM defines the continuity-origin governance layer of ACS.

Its role is to preserve visibility into the original accountability
relationship from which continuity emerges.

Module Operational Space

ACOIM governs:

  • accountability continuity origin
  • accountability-source continuity
  • continuity attribution
  • accountability provenance
  • preserved accountability origin
  • continuity legitimacy
  • accountability-preservation origin
  • continuity governance


The module applies wherever governance requires reconstruction
of accountability-continuity origins.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • identifiable accountability sources
  • traceable accountability origins
  • reconstructable accountability history
  • accountable continuity relationships
  • governance-valid continuity attribution
  • operationally valid accountability provenance


Its function is to ensure that governance can determine which
original accountability relationship remains preserved
throughout operational reality.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Continuity Origin Object

The organization must define which accountability environments
require origin governance.

This may include:

  • organizational structures
  • executive environments
  • governance frameworks
  • contractor networks
  • regulatory systems
  • AI governance systems
  • Human-AI operational environments
  • accountability programs


2. Define Continuity Origin Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which continuity
origin remains valid.

This includes:

  • continuity-source requirements
  • attribution requirements
  • legitimacy requirements
  • preservation requirements
  • governance requirements
  • continuity-origin conditions


3. Define Origin Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how continuity-origin failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • unidentified accountability origins
  • undocumented continuity relationships
  • illegitimate accountability structures
  • governance-blind accountability preservation
  • continuity-source ambiguity
  • unverifiable continuity origins


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
continuity-origin failures.

Governance response may include:

  • continuity review
  • source verification
  • governance intervention
  • continuity reconstruction
  • corrective actions
  • continuity reassessment
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • accountability origins
  • continuity relationships
  • governance reviews
  • validation activities
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting continuity states


An accountability-continuity environment must not remain
origin-valid if materially significant continuity relationships
cannot be attributed, reconstructed, reviewed, validated,
or governed.

Use Case 1 — Executive Accountability

Environment

Scenario

A decision creates long-term consequences across multiple management
periods and governance structures.

ACOIM reconstructs the original accountability relationship and
verifies whether accountability continuity remained connected
to that origin.

Result

of accountability continuity.

Result

Governance gains visibility into accountability continuity origins
across Human-AI ecosystems.

Canonical Closing Statement

Accountability Continuity Origin Integrity Module (ACOIM) defines
the structural conditions under which accountability-continuity
relationships remain attributable to identifiable, legitimate,
traceable, governable, reconstructable, reviewable, accountable, and

Accountability continuity cannot be preserved if the original
