Canonical Definition

Governance Compatibility Module (GCM) defines the structural
conditions under which autonomous operational systems remain
compatible across different governance frameworks, governance
policies, operational rules, and decision structures while
preserving operational integrity and trustworthy autonomous
cooperation.

GCM governs governance compatibility.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that autonomous operational actors can cooperate even when operating
under different governance environments.

Operational compatibility alone is insufficient.

Governance compatibility is also required.

GCM governs that compatibility.

Module Operational Role

GCM defines the governance compatibility layer of ACPS.

Its role is to preserve compatibility between autonomous systems
operating under different governance models without compromising
governance integrity.

Module Operational Space

GCM governs:

  • governance compatibility,
  • governance alignment,
  • policy compatibility,
  • operational rule compatibility,
  • governance interoperability,
  • cross-governance cooperation,
  • governed operational alignment,
  • governance consistency.


Module Function

The module applies wherever autonomous operational systems interact
across organizations, jurisdictions, governance models, or
operational policies.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Governance Compatibility Object

Identify which governance environments require compatibility.

2. Define Compatibility Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing policy alignment,
operational rule compatibility, and governance interoperability.

3. Define Compatibility Failure Logic

Identify conditions where governance rules become conflicting,
incompatible, inconsistent, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for policy alignment, governance
adaptation, compatibility validation, escalation, or operational
restriction.

5. Preserve Compatibility Traceability

Preserve governance mappings, compatibility assessments, operational
evidence, governance decisions, and supporting documentation.

Use Case 1 — Cross-Border Autonomous Freight Network

Scenario

Autonomous freight vehicles operate across multiple countries with
different regulatory and governance requirements.

Application

GCM governs compatibility between different governance environments
before autonomous operations continue across jurisdictions.

Result

Cross-border autonomous operations remain compliant, coordinated,
and operationally trustworthy.

Use Case 2 — Multi-Organization Industrial Ecosystem

Scenario

Several organizations deploy autonomous systems using different
internal governance policies while collaborating on a shared
industrial operation.

Application

GCM aligns governance requirements to enable safe and governed
autonomous cooperation.

Result

The participating organizations maintain compatible governance while
preserving operational integrity and collaborative efficiency.

Canonical Closing Statement

Governable interoperability requires governable governance
compatibility. Governance Compatibility ensures that autonomous
operational systems cooperate safely and consistently across
different governance environments while preserving operational
integrity and trustworthy autonomous collaboration.
