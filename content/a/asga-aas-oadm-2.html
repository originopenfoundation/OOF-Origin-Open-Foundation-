Canonical Definition

Operational Authority Delegation Module (OADM) defines the
structural conditions under which autonomous operational authority
may be legitimately delegated while preserving governance control,
operational accountability, and continuous authority oversight.

OADM governs operational authority delegation.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that delegated operational authority remains authorized, traceable,
limited, and continuously governable throughout autonomous
operation.

Authority may be delegated.

Responsibility for governance must remain preserved.

OADM governs that delegation.

Module Operational Role

OADM defines the authority delegation layer of AAS.

Its role is to govern how autonomous operational authority is
transferred between authorized operational actors without
compromising governance integrity.

Module Operational Space

OADM governs:

  • operational authority delegation,
  • delegated authority,
  • authority transfer,
  • delegated operational permissions,
  • authority governance,
  • delegation traceability,
  • controlled authority transfer,
  • governance-approved delegation.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must delegate autonomous
operational authority while preserving governance legitimacy,
operational control, and accountability.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Authority Delegation Object

Identify which operational authorities may be delegated and to whom.

2. Define Delegation Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing the delegation of
operational authority.

3. Define Delegation Failure Logic

Identify conditions where delegated authority becomes unauthorized,
excessive, expired, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for delegation approval, restriction,
suspension, revocation, or escalation.

5. Preserve Delegation Traceability

Preserve delegation records, governance approvals, authority
relationships, operational evidence, and supporting documentation.

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Drone Fleet

Scenario

A supervisory autonomous controller delegates operational authority
to regional autonomous drones during a coordinated inspection
mission.

Application

OADM governs the delegation of operational authority while
preserving governance oversight and operational control.

Result

Delegated autonomous operations remain legitimate, traceable, and
continuously governed.

Use Case 2 — Multi-Agent Manufacturing Environment

Scenario

An autonomous production coordinator delegates specific operational
responsibilities to specialized manufacturing agents.

Application

OADM governs the delegation process and preserves authority
traceability across all participating autonomous actors.

Result The manufacturing environment maintains controlled delegation
without compromising governance integrity or operational
accountability.

Canonical Closing Statement

Delegated authority must remain governed authority. Operational
Authority Delegation ensures that autonomous operational authority
may be transferred without losing legitimacy, accountability, or
continuous governance oversight.
