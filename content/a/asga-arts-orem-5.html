Canonical Definition

Operational Risk Escalation Module (OREM) defines the structural
conditions under which increasing operational risk triggers governed
escalation to preserve operational safety, governance integrity, and
justified operational trust.

OREM governs operational risk escalation.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that unacceptable operational risk automatically initiates
appropriate governance responses before autonomous operation becomes
unsafe or no longer justifiable.

Risk must not simply be observed.

Risk must trigger action.

OREM governs that escalation.

Module Operational Role

OREM defines the operational risk escalation layer of ARTS.

Its role is to ensure that increasing operational risk results in
timely, proportionate, and governance-approved escalation.

Module Operational Space

OREM governs:

  • operational risk escalation,
  • risk-triggered governance,
  • escalation thresholds,
  • operational risk response,
  • governance intervention,
  • escalation decision logic,
  • risk-based operational control,
  • governed escalation.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must initiate governance
actions when autonomous operational risk exceeds acceptable
thresholds.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Risk Escalation Object

Identify which operational risks require governed escalation.

2. Define Escalation Conditions

Establish governance conditions defining when operational risk must
trigger escalation.

3. Define Escalation Failure Logic

Identify conditions where escalation is delayed, omitted,
inappropriate, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for operational restriction, human
intervention, emergency procedures, execution suspension, or further
governance review.

5. Preserve Escalation Traceability

Preserve escalation triggers, governance decisions, operational
evidence, response history, and supporting documentation.

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Chemical Processing System

Scenario

An autonomous industrial control system detects rapidly increasing
operational risk within a chemical production process.

Application

OREM triggers governance escalation before operational safety limits
are exceeded.

Result

The organization prevents unsafe autonomous operation through timely
governance intervention.

Use Case 2 — Autonomous Financial Trading Platform

Scenario

An autonomous trading platform identifies abnormal market conditions
causing unacceptable operational risk.

Application

OREM initiates governance escalation, restricting autonomous trading
until operational risk returns to acceptable levels.

Result

The organization preserves financial stability, governance control,
and justified operational trust.

Canonical Closing Statement

Governable autonomy requires timely governance when risk becomes
unacceptable. Operational Risk Escalation ensures that increasing
operational risk triggers controlled, accountable, and
governance-approved intervention before autonomous operations
compromise safety, trust, or operational integrity.
