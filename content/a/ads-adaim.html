Canonical Definition

Authority Delegation Accountability Integrity Module (ADAIM) defines
the structural conditions under which delegated authority remains
attributable, accountable, traceable, evidence-supported,
governable, reconstructable, and operationally valid throughout
the delegation lifecycle.

ADAIM governs delegation accountability.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine who delegated authority, who accepted delegated authority,
who exercised delegated authority, who supervised delegated
authority, and who remains accountable for outcomes arising
from delegated authority.

Module Operational Role

ADAIM defines the delegation-accountability governance layer of ADS.

Its role is to ensure that accountability remains attached to
delegated authority throughout governance analysis.

Module Operational Space

ADAIM governs:

  • delegation accountability
  • delegated-authority attribution
  • delegation ownership
  • delegation consequence accountability
  • delegated decision accountability
  • delegation supervision accountability
  • delegation traceability
  • accountable delegation governance


The module applies wherever governance must determine accountability
connected to delegated authority.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • accountable delegation structures
  • attributable authority transfers
  • reconstructable delegation history
  • traceable delegated decisions
  • evidence-supported delegation governance
  • governance-valid consequence attribution


Its function is to ensure that accountability remains visible
throughout the delegation chain.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Delegation Accountability Object

The organization must define which delegated-authority environments
require accountability governance.

This may include:

  • management delegation structures
  • supervisory delegation chains
  • contractor authority environments
  • regulatory delegation systems
  • Human-AI governance environments
  • AI orchestration systems
  • autonomous operational systems
  • distributed governance architectures


2. Define Delegation Accountability Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which delegation
accountability remains valid.

This includes:

  • attribution requirements
  • accountability requirements
  • traceability requirements
  • evidence requirements
  • governance requirements
  • accountability-valid delegation conditions


3. Define Accountability Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how delegation-accountability failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • anonymous delegation
  • undocumented authority transfer
  • accountability gaps
  • hidden delegation chains
  • unverifiable delegated decisions
  • governance-blind delegation structures


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic
for delegation-accountability failures.

Governance response may include:

  • accountability review
  • attribution verification
  • governance intervention
  • escalation
  • corrective actions
  • delegation restrictions
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • delegation decisions
  • authority-transfer events
  • accountability reviews
  • governance interventions
  • evidence records
  • resulting accountability structures


A delegation-governance environment must not remain
accountability-valid if materially significant delegation activities
cannot be attributed, reconstructed, reviewed, validated,
or governed.

Use Case 1 — Multi-Layer Organizational

Delegation

Scenario

An executive delegates authority to a director, the director
delegates authority to a manager, and the manager delegates
authority to a supervisor who executes operational decisions.

Application

ADAIM reconstructs accountability throughout the delegation chain
and determines where accountability remained, transferred, or
was retained.

Result

Governance gains visibility into accountability across the
entire authority-delegation structure.

Use Case 2 — Human-AI Delegation Environment

Scenario

A human operator delegates authority to an orchestration platform,
which distributes delegated authority to multiple AI agents
and execution systems.

Application

ADAIM reconstructs accountability throughout the delegation
lifecycle and identifies accountable actors across the
intelligent operational environment.

Result

Governance gains visibility into who remains accountable for
decisions and outcomes generated through delegated authority.

Canonical Closing Statement

Authority Delegation Accountability Integrity Module (ADAIM) defines
the structural conditions under which delegated authority remains
attributable, accountable, traceable, evidence-supported,
governable, reconstructable, and operationally valid throughout
the delegation lifecycle.

Authority may be delegated, but accountability must remain
reconstructable. Authority delegation accountability integrity
therefore becomes a foundational condition of trustworthy
delegation governance.
