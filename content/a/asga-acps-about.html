About Autonomous Compatibility Standard (ACPS)

Governing Autonomous Operational Compatibility

Autonomous Compatibility Standard (ACPS) defines the governance
conditions under which autonomous operational compatibility remains
interoperable, aligned, trustworthy, and governable throughout the
complete autonomous operational lifecycle.

Autonomous systems increasingly operate as part of larger
operational ecosystems.

Operational compatibility enables independently developed autonomous
systems to work together under shared governance conditions.

ACPS governs this operational governance space.

What Is Autonomous Operational Compatibility?

Autonomous operational compatibility is the governed ability of
autonomous operational actors to operate together across different
platforms, technologies, organizations, and operational environments
while preserving governance integrity and operational consistency.

It enables organizations to establish interoperable autonomous
operations, maintain governance alignment, preserve operational
trust, validate compatibility, and support scalable autonomous
ecosystems.

Canonical Definition

Autonomous Compatibility Standard (ACPS) defines the structural
conditions under which autonomous operational systems remain
compatible, interoperable, governable, and operationally aligned
while interacting across heterogeneous autonomous operational
environments throughout the complete autonomous operational
lifecycle.

Why ACPS Exists

Future autonomous ecosystems will consist of autonomous systems
developed by different organizations using different technologies
and operational models.

Without governed compatibility, autonomous operations become
fragmented, difficult to coordinate, and increasingly unreliable.

ACPS exists because autonomous operational compatibility represents
the ninth governable space of Autonomous Systems Governance
Architecture (ASGA™).

What ACPS Governs

ACPS governs the structural conditions under which autonomous
operational compatibility remains:

  • interoperable,
  • aligned,
  • trustworthy,
  • validated,
  • resilient,
  • governable.


Rather than governing communication technologies or technical
integration protocols, ACPS governs the governance conditions
required for trustworthy autonomous operational compatibility.

Architectural Position

Autonomous Compatibility Standard serves as the ninth governance
space of Autonomous Systems Governance Architecture (ASGA™).

It establishes the governance foundation that enables autonomous
operational actors to cooperate reliably across diverse autonomous
ecosystems.

Without governed compatibility, scalable autonomous ecosystems
cannot operate consistently.

Canonical Closing Statement

Governable autonomy requires governable compatibility. Autonomous
Compatibility establishes the governance foundation that enables
autonomous operational ecosystems to remain interoperable,
coordinated, trustworthy, and continuously governable throughout the
complete autonomous operational lifecycle.
