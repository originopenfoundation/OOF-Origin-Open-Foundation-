Canonical Definition

Trust Degradation Module (TDM) defines the structural conditions
under which reductions in operational trust are identified,
evaluated, monitored, and governed throughout autonomous operation.

TDM governs trust degradation.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that declining operational trust is detected early and addressed
before it compromises autonomous operation.

Trust is not permanent.

Trust may increase or decrease over time.

TDM governs that degradation.

Module Operational Role

TDM defines the trust degradation layer of ARTS.

Its role is to detect, evaluate, and govern declining operational
trust before it creates unacceptable operational risk.

Module Operational Space

TDM governs:

  • trust degradation,
  • declining operational trust,
  • trust deterioration,
  • trust monitoring,
  • operational confidence reduction,
  • governance trust degradation,
  • trust status assessment,
  • continuous trust observation.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must detect and govern
reductions in operational trust throughout autonomous execution.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Trust Degradation Object

Identify which autonomous operational activities require monitoring
for declining trust.

2. Define Trust Degradation Conditions

Establish governance conditions for identifying and evaluating
reductions in operational trust.

3. Define Trust Degradation Failure Logic

Identify conditions where declining trust is not detected,
incorrectly evaluated, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for increased monitoring, operational
restriction, trust review, escalation, or corrective action.

5. Preserve Trust Traceability

Preserve trust assessments, governance decisions, operational
evidence, degradation history, and supporting documentation.

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Inspection Drone

Scenario

An autonomous inspection drone begins producing inconsistent
inspection results during routine operations.

Application

TDM detects the reduction in operational trust and initiates
additional governance review.

Result

Potential operational issues are identified before trust
deteriorates to unacceptable levels.

Use Case 2 — Enterprise Autonomous AI Agent

Scenario

An autonomous AI agent gradually produces recommendations with
decreasing reliability.

Application

TDM monitors trust degradation and triggers governance actions
before operational confidence is lost.

Result

The organization preserves trustworthy autonomous operation through
early governance intervention.

Canonical Closing Statement

Trustworthy autonomy requires continuous awareness of declining
trust. Trust Degradation ensures that reductions in operational
confidence are identified, governed, and addressed before they
undermine safe and reliable autonomous operation.
