ArtData Governance™

Governance Architecture Space

ArtData Governance™ governs the architectural space responsible for
establishing, maintaining, enforcing, validating, and evolving the
rules, policies, authority structures, stewardship responsibilities,
access conditions, lifecycle requirements, and governance frameworks
governing ArtData® assets.

The space exists because valuable evidence assets
require governance.

Without governance:

  • confidence may decline,
  • stewardship may become unclear,
  • authority may become disputed,
  • access may become uncontrolled,
  • integrity may become compromised,
  • long-term value may decrease.


ArtData Governance™ exists to ensure that qualified evidence remains
governed throughout its lifecycle.

Canonical Definition

ArtData Governance™ is the governable architectural space
responsible for establishing, maintaining, enforcing, validating,
and evolving the governance structures, authority frameworks,
stewardship mechanisms, lifecycle controls, access conditions,
integrity protections, and operational rules governing
ArtData® assets.

The Core Problem

Organizations frequently collect, preserve, and utilize evidence
without establishing clear governance conditions.

As a result:

  • ownership becomes unclear,
  • stewardship becomes inconsistent,
  • authority conflicts emerge,
  • accountability weakens,
  • governance confidence decreases.


Evidence preservation alone is insufficient.

Evidence governance is also required.

ArtData Governance™ was created to address this challenge.

Why ArtData Governance™ Exists

ArtData Qualification™ determines admission.

ArtData Registry™ preserves assets.

ArtData Intelligence™ extracts understanding.

ArtData Value™ evaluates significance.

ArtData Exchange™ enables utilization.

However, organizations still require a mechanism capable of
answering a critical question:

  • Who governs the evidence and under which rules?


ArtData Governance™ exists to answer this question.

The Governance Principle

A fundamental principle of ArtData Governance™ is:

Trusted evidence should remain governed throughout its lifecycle.

The objective is not merely preserving evidence.

The objective is preserving confidence in evidence governance.
