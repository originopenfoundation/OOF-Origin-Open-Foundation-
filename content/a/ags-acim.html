Canonical Definition

Authority Continuity Integrity Module (ACIM) defines the structural
conditions under which authority remains continuously traceable,
reconstructable, governable, accountable, and operationally valid
throughout delegation, succession, transfer, escalation,
organizational change, system evolution, and operational transition.

ACIM governs authority continuity.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine whether authority remained preserved, transferred,
inherited, escalated, suspended, revoked, or terminated
throughout its lifecycle.

Module Operational Role

ACIM defines the authority-continuity governance layer of AGS.

Its role is to preserve visibility of authority throughout
operational change and governance transitions.

Module Operational Space

ACIM governs:

  • authority continuity
  • authority transfer
  • authority succession
  • authority delegation continuity
  • authority escalation continuity
  • authority lifecycle governance
  • continuity accountability
  • authority traceability


The module applies wherever authority changes over time.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • continuous authority visibility
  • reconstructable authority history
  • accountable authority transitions
  • governance-valid authority continuity
  • traceable authority movement
  • operational authority preservation


Its function is to ensure that governance can determine what
happened to authority throughout its lifecycle.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Authority Continuity Object

The organization must define which authority environments
require continuity governance.

This may include:

  • organizational authority structures
  • supervisory authority chains
  • delegated authority environments
  • regulatory authority environments
  • AI orchestration authority
  • autonomous governance systems
  • Human-AI governance environments
  • multi-layer governance structures


2. Define Authority Continuity Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which authority
continuity remains valid.

This includes:

  • continuity requirements
  • transfer requirements
  • succession requirements
  • traceability requirements
  • accountability requirements
  • governance-valid continuity conditions


3. Define Continuity Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how authority-continuity failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • undocumented authority transfers
  • authority gaps
  • continuity breaks
  • hidden succession events
  • governance discontinuities
  • authority lifecycle ambiguity


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
continuity-integrity failures.

Governance response may include:

  • continuity review
  • authority reconstruction
  • governance intervention
  • escalation
  • corrective actions
  • authority verification
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • authority transfers
  • succession events
  • delegation events
  • governance reviews
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting authority structures


An authority-governance environment must not remain continuity-valid
if materially significant authority transitions cannot be
reconstructed, reviewed, validated, or governed.

Use Case 1 — Organizational Leadership

Transition

Scenario

A department manager leaves an organization and authority is
transferred to a successor.

Application

ACIM governs traceability and continuity of authority throughout
the transition process.

Result

Governance gains visibility into how authority moved and whether
authority continuity remained preserved.

Use Case 2 — Autonomous Governance

Environment

Scenario

A supervisory AI agent transfers governance authority to another
supervisory agent following operational restructuring.

Application

ACIM governs continuity and traceability of authority throughout
the transfer process.

Result

Governance gains visibility into authority evolution across
intelligent operational systems.
