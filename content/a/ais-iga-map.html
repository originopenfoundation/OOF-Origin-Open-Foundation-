IGA™ Architecture Map

Intelligence Governance Architecture

Official Intelligence Governance Master Reference Map for
Intelligent Systems

IGA™ (Intelligence Governance Architecture) serves as the official
OOF® master governance reference map used to identify, classify,
navigate, diagnose, validate, audit, and understand the primary
governance spaces of intelligent systems, autonomous agents,
robotics, Human-AI environments, and future machine intelligence
ecosystems.

IGA™ does not define a specific technology, model, vendor, platform,
implementation, or intelligence architecture.

IGA™ defines the primary governance spaces of intelligence itself.

The purpose of IGA™ is not to govern intelligence directly.

The purpose of IGA™ is to identify where governance belongs before
governance begins.

IGA™ serves as the master governance reference map for:

  • Artificial Intelligence Systems
  • Autonomous Agents
  • Multi-Agent Systems
  • Robotics
  • Human-AI Environments
  • Cognitive Automation Systems
  • Intelligent Infrastructure
  • Autonomous Organizations
  • Future Machine Intelligence Systems
  • Future Intelligence Ecosystems


Core Architectural Principle

Every governance space answers one primary question.

No governance space duplicates the purpose of another governance
space.

Every governance space begins where the previous governance space
ends.

Together these governance spaces form the official intelligence
governance reference map of IGA™.
