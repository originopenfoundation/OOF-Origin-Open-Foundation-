Evidence Collection™

Governance Architecture Space

Evidence Collection™ governs the architectural space responsible for
capturing, gathering, recording, and preserving operational evidence
while events, actions, conditions, decisions, processes, and
outcomes are occurring.

The space exists because evidence that is not collected at the right
time may be lost, altered, fragmented, disputed, or disconnected
from operational reality.

Evidence Collection™ exists to ensure that relevant operational
evidence is captured close to the moment of reality.

Canonical Definition

Evidence Collection™ is the governable architectural space
responsible for capturing, gathering, recording, and preserving
governance-relevant operational evidence during or near the
occurrence of events, actions, decisions, conditions, processes, and
outcomes.

The Core Problem

Many audits begin too late.

By the time evidence is requested:

  • operational context may be gone,
  • records may be incomplete,
  • memories may be unreliable,
  • conditions may have changed,
  • responsibility may become disputed.


Evidence Collection™ was created to reduce this gap.

Why Evidence Collection™ Exists

ART™ depends on evidence.

Without evidence, auditability becomes weak.

Evidence Collection™ exists to create the first evidentiary layer of
Audit in Real Time™.

Its purpose is not interpretation.

Its purpose is capture.

The Evidence Collection Principle

A fundamental principle of Evidence Collection™ is:

Evidence should be captured as close as possible to the operational
reality it represents.

The objective is preserving reality before reality disappears,
changes, or becomes disputed.

Core Questions

Evidence Collection™ seeks to answer:

What evidence exists?

When was it captured?

Where did it originate?

What operational condition does it represent?

Is the evidence connected to the event?

Is the evidence complete enough for audit?

Can the evidence be preserved?

Can the evidence support future validation?

Evidence Types

Evidence Collection™ may include:

Operational Records

System Logs

Sensor Outputs

Decision Records

Human Observations

Process Records

Image or Video Evidence

Communication Records

AI Agent Action Records

Robotics Operation Records

Evidence Collection for AI Systems

Future AI systems may require evidence collection for:

  • decisions,
  • tool usage,
  • memory changes,
  • self-correction actions,
  • escalations,
  • autonomous behavior.


The objective is not merely observing AI activity.

The objective is preserving evidence of AI activity.

Evidence Collection for Robotics

Robotics environments may require evidence collection for:

  • movement,
  • interaction,
  • safety conditions,
  • operational actions,
  • sensor states,
  • intervention events.


The objective is preserving operational evidence for later
verification.

Relationship to ART™

Evidence Collection™ is the first operational layer of ART™.

ART™ cannot validate, trace, audit, or preserve what was never
collected.

Evidence Collection™ therefore creates the foundation for real-time
auditability.

Relationship to Evidence Validation™

Evidence Collection™ captures evidence.

Evidence Validation™ evaluates whether the collected evidence is
reliable, relevant, complete, and suitable for audit use.

Collection creates the record.

Validation evaluates the record.

Relationship to ArtData®

Evidence Collection™ produces raw or preliminary evidence.

ArtData® requires validated evidence.

The path from operational reality to ArtData® begins with evidence
collection but must pass through validation, traceability,
integrity, and audit logic before becoming preserved evidence
intelligence.

Why This Space Matters

Future governance systems will increasingly require evidence-based
decisions.

Evidence cannot support governance if it is collected too late,
collected incompletely, or disconnected from operational reality.

Evidence Collection™ exists to preserve the first connection between
reality and auditability.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Collection™ transforms operational events into preserved
evidentiary records. By capturing governance-relevant evidence close
to the moment of operational reality, it enables ART™ to validate,
trace, audit, and preserve what actually occurred before evidence is
lost, altered, fragmented, or disputed.
