Canonical Definition

Operational Authority Restriction Module (OARM) defines the
structural conditions under which autonomous operational authority
may be limited, constrained, or partially restricted while
preserving governance control, operational safety, and
accountability.

OARM governs operational authority restriction.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that autonomous operational authority remains proportional to
operational conditions, governance requirements, and acceptable
levels of operational risk.

Authority does not always require revocation.

Sometimes authority requires restriction.

OARM governs that restriction.

Module Operational Role

OARM defines the authority restriction layer of AAS.

Its role is to govern how operational authority may be safely
limited without unnecessarily terminating autonomous operation.

Module Operational Space

OARM governs:

  • operational authority restriction,
  • authority limitation,
  • conditional authority,
  • restricted operational permissions,
  • governance-controlled authority,
  • authority containment,
  • operational authority constraints,
  • proportional authority control.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must temporarily or
permanently restrict autonomous operational authority while
preserving governance integrity and operational control.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Authority Restriction Object

Identify which operational authorities may be restricted under
governed conditions.

2. Define Restriction Conditions

Establish governance conditions requiring operational authority
restriction.

3. Define Restriction Failure Logic

Identify conditions where restricted authority becomes excessive,
insufficient, ineffective, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for authority restriction, adjustment,
monitoring, escalation, or restoration.

5. Preserve Restriction Traceability

Preserve restriction decisions, governance approvals, operational
evidence, authority changes, and supporting documentation.

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Industrial Robot

Scenario

An autonomous production robot detects an abnormal operating
condition during manufacturing.

Application

OARM restricts the robot's operational authority to predefined safe
functions until the condition is resolved.

Result

Operations continue under controlled authority while governance and
operational safety remain preserved.

Use Case 2 — Autonomous Financial Trading Agent

Scenario

An autonomous trading system exceeds predefined operational risk
thresholds.

Application OARM limits the agent's operational authority by
restricting high-risk trading activities while allowing approved
monitoring functions.

Result

The organization reduces operational risk without requiring a
complete operational shutdown.

Canonical Closing Statement

Governable authority includes the ability to limit authority when
conditions require it. Operational Authority Restriction preserves
safe, controlled, and accountable autonomy by ensuring that
operational permissions remain proportionate to governance
requirements.
