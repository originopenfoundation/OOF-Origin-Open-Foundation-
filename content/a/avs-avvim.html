Canonical Definition

Authority Validation Validity Integrity Module (AVVIM) defines the
structural conditions under which authority validation remains
legitimate, recognized, enforceable, governable, accountable,
reconstructable, and operationally valid throughout the
validation lifecycle.

AVVIM governs validation validity.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine whether a validation outcome itself remains valid,
recognized, current, enforceable, and operationally reliable.

Module Operational Role

AVVIM defines the validation-validity governance layer of AVS.

Its role is to ensure that validation outcomes remain trustworthy
after validation has occurred.

Module Operational Space

AVVIM governs:

  • validation validity
  • validation legitimacy
  • validation recognition
  • validation enforceability
  • validation lifecycle governance
  • validation status management
  • authority-recognition continuity
  • validation accountability


The module applies wherever governance requires confirmation that
validation outcomes remain valid over time.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • legitimate validation outcomes
  • recognized authority status
  • reconstructable validation history
  • accountable validation decisions
  • governance-valid authority recognition
  • operationally reliable validation states


Its function is to ensure that governance can determine whether
previously validated authority remains valid today.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Validation Validity Object

The organization must define which validation environments
require validity governance.

This may include:

  • certification systems
  • licensing environments
  • governance appointments
  • supervisory authorizations
  • contractor qualification systems
  • AI authorization frameworks
  • agent-permission environments
  • Human-AI governance systems


2. Define Validation Validity Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which validation
remains valid.

This includes:

  • validity requirements
  • renewal requirements
  • recognition requirements
  • accountability requirements
  • governance requirements
  • validation-maintenance conditions


3. Define Validity Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how validation-validity failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • expired validations
  • revoked authority
  • suspended authority
  • invalid certifications
  • governance-invalid approvals
  • obsolete authority recognition


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
validation-validity failures.

Governance response may include:

  • validation review
  • revalidation procedures
  • governance intervention
  • escalation
  • suspension of authority
  • corrective actions
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • validation outcomes
  • renewal activities
  • revalidation decisions
  • governance reviews
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting validation states


An authority-validation environment must not remain validity-valid
if materially significant validation outcomes cannot be
reconstructed, reviewed, renewed, validated, or governed.

Use Case 1 — Professional Certification

Environment

Scenario

A worker possesses a certification granting authority to perform
specialized operational activities.

Application

AVVIM governs whether the certification remains valid, current,
recognized, and enforceable.

Result

Governance gains visibility into the continuing validity
of authority.

Use Case 2 — AI Authority Environment

Scenario

An AI agent receives validated authority to access tools, workflows,
operational privileges, or execution systems.

Application

AVVIM governs whether the validation remains current and
operationally valid throughout the authorization lifecycle.

Result

Governance gains visibility into whether authority remains valid
after initial approval.
