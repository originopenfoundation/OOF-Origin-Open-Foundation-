Canonical Definition

Mineral Infused Water Module (MIW™) defines the product-specific
conditions under which water may be naturally enriched through
contact with valid mineral source material while remaining
structurally identifiable as water and suitable for continuous
hydration.

Mineral infused water is valid only when mineral enrichment occurs
through natural contact-based release and does not transform water
into a fortified, additive-based, or chemically modified product.

Module Position

This module operates under the Infused Water Standard (IWS™).

IWS™ defines the parent hydration boundary.
MIW™ defines the mineral application of that boundary.

The module specifies when mineral source material may enrich water
without replacing its identity as water.

Base

Water remains the primary medium.
Mineral source material enables natural release through contact.
The output remains water.

The process does not create a supplement or chemically fortified
liquid.
It preserves hydration while allowing controlled mineral enrichment.

Core Principle

Mineral contact may enrich water.
It must not redefine it.

A valid mineral infused water system depends on passive release,
structural restraint, and preservation of hydration identity.

Product Conditions

For mineral infused water to remain valid within this module,
it must:
  • use water as the dominant medium
  • use natural mineral source material suitable for water
    contact
  • rely on passive mineral release through contact
  • avoid direct mineral addition or artificial fortification
  • remain suitable for repeated hydration use
Valid mineral source materials may include natural mineral-bearing
materials suitable for controlled water contact under safe
conditions.

All source materials must remain:
  • safe
  • clean
  • suitable for human use
  • free from contamination and unsafe release behavior


Integrity Principle

Mineral infused water remains valid only when:
  • enrichment occurs through real contact-based release
  • no direct additive logic replaces mineral infusion
  • no forced mineralization or chemical alteration is used
  • output remains structurally water
If mineral loading redefines the function or identity of water,
the product exits this module.

Minimum Implementation Framework (MIF)

Step 1 — Water Base
Use clean water suitable for repeated hydration.

Minimum requirement:
  • water must remain the dominant medium
  • no additive-based mineral fortification may replace
    infusion
 Step 2 — Mineral Source Selection
Use mineral source material suitable for water contact and safe
human use.

Minimum requirement:
  • source material must be naturally suitable
  • source material must be clean and stable
  • no unsafe contamination or uncontrolled release may be
    present
 Step 3 — Contact-Based Infusion
Apply mineral enrichment through passive contact between water and
mineral source material.

Minimum requirement:
  • release must occur through contact
  • no direct addition of mineral substances may simulate
    infusion
  • no artificial acceleration may replace natural release logic
 Step 4 — Intensity Control
Control time, exposure, and contact conditions.

Minimum requirement:
  • enrichment must remain within hydration identity
  • output must not become a supplement-like or chemically
    dominant liquid
  • mineral presence must not override structural water identity
 Step 5 — Output Validation
Confirm final state.

Minimum requirement:
  • water remains dominant
  • output is suitable for continuous hydration
  • product is not structurally classified as fortified water,
    supplement liquid, or chemically modified drink

Output Definition

Mineral Infused Water (MIW™ compliant) is water that:
  • contains naturally released mineral compounds through
    contact
  • remains structurally water
  • supports hydration through controlled natural mineral
    enrichment
  • is suitable for repeated consumption within hydration
    context

Structural Limitation

This module excludes:
  • direct mineral fortification
  • forced mineralization
  • additive-based mineral drinks
  • supplement-style hydration products
  • products whose mineral intensity exceeds water identity
These may be valid products in other categories.
They are not valid within Mineral Infused Water under MIW™.

Use Case 1 — Daily Mineral Hydration

Scenario
An individual uses naturally mineral-infused water as part of
regular hydration.

Application
  • water is exposed to valid mineral source material under
    controlled conditions
  • no direct additives are used
  • the final product remains suitable for repeated daily
    hydration
 Result
  • hydration is naturally enriched
  • water remains structurally simple and clean
  • mineral presence is achieved without conversion into
    supplement logic


Use Case 2 — Natural Mineral Hydration Product

Scenario
A brand develops a hydration product based on natural mineral

Application
  • mineral release occurs through contact-based process
  • source material remains transparent
  • output remains compatible with MIW™ and IWS™ conditions
 Result

  • clearer distinction from fortified drinks
  • stronger category credibility through structural restraint
