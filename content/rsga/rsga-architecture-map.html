RSGA™ Architecture Map

Resource Sustainability Governance Architecture — Resource Sustainability Governance Layer

Official Resource Sustainability Governance reference map for finite, regenerative, recoverable, flow-constrained, capacity-constrained, strategically concentrated and interdependent Resource Systems.

Official Governance Flow

Resource Characterization → Resource Availability & Capacity → Resource Demand → Resource Dependency & Criticality → Resource Access → Resource Prioritization & Allocation → Resource Consumption & Utilization → Resource Scarcity & Constraint → Resource Recovery & Circularity → Resource Substitution & Flexibility → Resource Interdependency & Resilience → Resource Continuity & Sustainability

Governed Spaces

  2. RACS — Resource Availability & Capacity
  3. RDGS — Resource Demand Governance
  4. RDCS — Resource Dependency & Criticality
  5. RAGS — Resource Access Governance
  6. RPAS — Resource Prioritization & Allocation
  7. RCUS — Resource Consumption & Utilization
  8. RSCS — Resource Scarcity & Constraint
  9. RRCS — Resource Recovery & Circularity
  10. RSFS — Resource Substitution & Flexibility
  11. RIRS — Resource Interdependency & Resilience
  12. RCSS — Resource Continuity & Sustainability

Resource scarcity is a governed condition, not a prerequisite for activating the architecture. Recovery, regeneration, substitution and changing dependencies can require reassessment of earlier spaces.
