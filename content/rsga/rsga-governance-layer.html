RSGA™ Resource Sustainability Governance Layer

RSGA™ is the OOF® reference architecture for governing materially constrained Resources and Resource capacities across competing operational and societal dependencies.

Architectural Boundary

RSGA™ governs common Resource Sustainability conditions. It does not replace resource-specific physical, ecological, technical or infrastructural mechanisms; independently establish Governance Authority; determine Accountability; govern general Evidence or Validation; or determine intelligence execution Value.

Interoperability

GOA™ governs Governance Authority; ORA™ governs Operational Reality; AGA™ governs Accountability; ADIT® governs Evidence and Auditability; VALIDOS™ governs Validation; PGA™ governs Prediction; VFM® governs Value lifecycle mechanisms; VEGA™ governs intelligence execution value and Resource justification. RSGA™ governs Resource Sustainability without acquiring those independent domains.

Canonical Principle

Resources do not become sustainable merely because they exist, demand is legitimate, or consumption creates Value. Their sustainability depends on availability, dependency, access, allocation, use, recovery, resilience and future continuity remaining governable together.
