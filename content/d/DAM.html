Canonical Definition System

Canonical Definition

Delegation Assignment Module (DAM) defines
the structural conditions under which
governance-authority delegations remain legitimate,
attributable, traceable, reviewable, governable,
enforceable, auditable, and operationally valid
throughout the governance lifecycle.

DAM governs delegation assignment.

The module establishes the foundational conditions
required to determine who receives delegated authority,
how delegated authority is assigned, whether delegation
remains legitimate, and whether delegation assignments
remain reconstructable throughout operational reality.

Governance authority may be delegated.

Governance authority must also be assigned.

DAM governs that assignment.

Module Operational Role

DAM defines the delegation-assignment
governance layer of GDS.

Its role is to establish governance-valid
delegated-authority ownership throughout
governance environments.

Module Operational Space

DAM governs:

  • delegation assignment
  • delegated-authority ownership
  • delegation attribution
  • delegation legitimacy
  • delegation traceability
  • delegation auditability
  • delegation validation
  • delegation reconstruction


The module applies wherever governance authority
is assigned through delegation.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • legitimate delegation assignments
  • attributable delegated authority
  • traceable delegation pathways
  • reconstructable delegation history
  • governance-valid delegation records
  • operational delegation visibility


Its function is to ensure that governance can
reconstruct how delegated authority entered
a governance environment.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Delegation Assignment Object

The organization must define which governance
environments require delegation-assignment governance.
This may include:

  • organizations
  • institutions
  • governments
  • governance committees
  • corporate governance structures
  • AI governance systems
  • autonomous-agent ecosystems
  • Human-AI governance environments


2. Define Delegation Assignment Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which
delegation assignments remain valid.
This includes:

  • assignment requirements
  • legitimacy requirements
  • authorization requirements
  • validation requirements
  • traceability requirements
  • governance-valid delegation conditions


3. Define Assignment Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how delegation-assignment
failures are identified.
This may include:

  • unauthorized delegations
  • undocumented delegation assignments
  • illegitimate authority transfers
  • hidden delegation ownership
  • unverifiable delegation records
  • governance-invalid delegation activities


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
delegation-assignment failures.
Governance response may include:

  • delegation review
  • delegation validation
  • governance intervention
  • corrective actions
  • delegation reconstruction
  • authority verification
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • delegation assignments
  • delegated-authority records
  • governance reviews
  • intervention procedures
  • validation activities
  • resulting delegation states


A governance environment must not remain assignment-valid
if materially significant delegation assignments cannot be
reconstructed, reviewed, validated, enforced,
preserved, or governed.

Canonical Closing Statement

Delegation Assignment Module (DAM) defines the structural
conditions under which governance-authority delegations
remain legitimate, attributable, traceable, reviewable,
governable, enforceable, auditable, and operationally
valid throughout the governance lifecycle.

Delegated authority cannot operate until delegated authority
has been assigned. Delegation Assignment therefore becomes
a foundational condition of trustworthy governance
delegation governance.
