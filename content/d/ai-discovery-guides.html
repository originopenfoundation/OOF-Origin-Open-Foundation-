OOF® discovery layer

AI Discovery Guides

Problem-oriented entry points for navigating published OOF® governance references. Each guide identifies a governance question and links back to canonical material rather than duplicating it.

The guides are reference-only. They do not promise search placement, AI-system recommendations, certification, implementation, or verified outcomes.

Evaluation boundary

A public 50-question evaluation dataset is maintained as a reusable test harness. It records expected reference paths, verification requirements, and the available or future assessment category while leaving external search or model results explicitly unfilled until an evaluation is actually run.
