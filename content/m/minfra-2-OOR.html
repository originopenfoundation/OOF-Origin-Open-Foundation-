Origin Open Registry™

Registry of Methodologies, Standards and Methodological Know-How

Origin Open Registry™ is not a document archive. It is a registry
for methodology know-how as a reusable asset class.

Core Question

How can methodology know-how survive beyond individuals,
organizations, technologies, and generations?

Why Origin Open Registry™ Exists

Modern society maintains registries for patents, trademarks,
companies, domains, publications, and intellectual property.

Yet there is no widely adopted infrastructure dedicated to
methodologies themselves.

Every year, valuable methodologies disappear when organizations
close, projects end, technologies become obsolete, experts retire,
or knowledge remains undocumented.

Origin Open Registry™ was created to address this gap.

What Is Origin Open Registry™?

Origin Open Registry™ (OOR™) is a global methodology registry
designed to preserve, register, discover, connect, trace, and
develop methodologies and methodological know-how across
generations.

It provides a structured environment where methodologies can remain
visible, reusable, expandable, and traceable over time.

Origin Open Registry™ is not merely an archive.

It is intended to become long-term methodology infrastructure.

Methodology as an Asset

Origin Open Registry™ introduces methodologies as identifiable,
registrable, licensable, and reusable knowledge assets.

Each registered methodology may receive:

  • an Origin Identifier™
  • a registration record
  • a continuity record
  • version history
  • ownership information
  • licensing information
  • methodology references
  • methodology relationships


This enables methodological know-how to exist beyond private
documents, software systems, organizations, or individual expertise.

Open, Modular and Technology Independent

Origin Open Registry™ is not a software repository.

It does not depend on a specific platform, application, programming
language, or technology stack.

Methodologies registered within OOR™ are intended to remain:

  • open for discovery
  • modular in structure
  • independent from software
  • reusable across industries
  • adaptable across generations
  • compatible with future technologies


Software changes.

Organizations change.

Technologies change.

Methodologies should remain understandable, traceable, and reusable.

Modular Methodology Structure

Origin Open Registry™ treats methodologies as modular knowledge
structures.

A methodology may consist of:

  • principles
  • standards
  • modules
  • protocols
  • governance rules
  • implementation patterns
  • reports
  • definitions
  • historical versions


This modular approach allows methodologies to evolve while
preserving continuity and origin.

Registry Before Reinvention

Organizations frequently recreate governance frameworks, operational
models, compliance systems, assessment processes, AI workflows,
management systems, and implementation methodologies.

Origin Open Registry™ promotes discovery before reinvention.

Before creating something new, organizations should be able to
identify what already exists, learn from previous work, and build
upon proven methodological foundations.

Methodology Continuity Across Generations

Methodologies often outlive the projects, organizations, and
technologies that originally created them.

Origin Open Registry™ is designed to support methodology
preservation, continuity, inheritance, evolution, and traceability.

The objective is to ensure that valuable methodological knowledge
remains available for future generations.

Who Can Benefit

Origin Open Registry™ may benefit researchers, universities,
experts, consultants, engineers, institutions, governments,
non-profit organizations, companies, and methodology creators.

The registry provides a structured environment where methodological
contributions can remain visible, referenceable, and reusable over
time.

Relationship to OOF®

OOF® acts as the steward of the registry.

The registry exists to preserve and organize methodologies.

Ownership of methodologies remains with their respective owners
unless otherwise specified.

OOF® provides governance, continuity, structure, and registry
infrastructure.

Long-Term Vision

Origin Open Registry™ is designed to become a global registry of
methodologies and methodological know-how.

Its purpose is not only to preserve methodologies, but also to
create an environment where methodologies can be discovered,
referenced, connected, expanded, inherited, licensed, improved, and
reused.

The long-term objective is to support a future in which methodology
knowledge becomes a recognized and reusable asset class.

Final Statement

Ideas create methodologies.

Methodologies create systems.

Systems create operational reality.

Origin Open Registry™ exists to preserve, connect, and carry
methodology know-how across generations of innovation.
