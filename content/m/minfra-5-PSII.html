OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™)

Understanding Problems Before Searching for Solutions

Core Question

What problem are you actually trying to solve?

Why the OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ Exists

Most organizations begin by searching for solutions.

Few begin by understanding the problem.

As a result, significant resources are often invested into solving
symptoms while the underlying causes remain unidentified.

Organizations frequently implement:

  • new software,
  • new processes,
  • new controls,
  • new policies,
  • new technologies,
  • new consultants,


without first establishing whether they correctly understand the
problem itself.

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ was created to address
this challenge.

Its purpose is simple:

Help organizations understand where a problem belongs before
deciding how to solve it.

What Is the OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™?

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ is a structured
intelligence system designed to identify, classify, map, and
organize problems across governance, operations, infrastructure,
technology, responsibility, methodology, identity, certification,
and emerging reality environments.

The Index serves as a growing repository of:

  • problems,
  • risks,
  • failures,
  • conflicts,
  • governance gaps,
  • responsibility gaps,
  • operational weaknesses,
  • emerging challenges.


Unlike traditional service catalogs, the Index begins with the
problem rather than the solution.

Why Problems Matter

Every audit begins with a problem.

Every investigation begins with a problem.

Every accident begins with a problem.

Every dispute begins with a problem.

Every governance failure begins with a problem.

Every innovation begins with a problem.

Problems are often the first visible signal that a system, process,
methodology, organization, infrastructure, or governance environment
requires attention.

Understanding the problem often determines whether the solution
succeeds or fails.

The Problem-First Approach

Most organizations navigate reality through solutions.

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ navigates reality through
problems.

Instead of asking:

What service do I need?

The Index asks:

What problem am I facing?

This distinction is important.

Solutions may vary.

Technologies may change.

Problems often remain.

How the Index Works

The Index begins with a problem.

Each problem is then connected to:

  • related problem spaces,
  • governance spaces,
  • responsibility conditions,
  • methodologies,
  • standards,
  • reports,
  • intelligence services,
  • potential solution pathways.


This allows organizations to move from confusion to clarity through
structured analysis.

Questions Before Solutions

The Index is built around questions.

Questions often reveal more than assumptions.

Examples include:

  • Who is responsible?
  • Why did this failure occur?
  • Where does the risk originate?
  • Is the certification trustworthy?
  • Can this methodology be trusted?
  • Is there a governance gap?
  • What is the root cause?
  • What problem are we actually trying to solve?


The purpose of the Index is not to immediately answer every
question.

The purpose is to ensure the right question is being asked.

Long-Term Vision

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ is designed to become a
continuously expanding intelligence map of problems across
governance, infrastructure, technology, AI, society, industry, and
future operational environments.

Its purpose is to help organizations understand not only what is
happening, but why it is happening and where it belongs.

The Index does not begin with products.

It does not begin with services.

It begins with problems.

Final Statement

Every problem exists within a space.

Every meaningful decision begins with understanding both.

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ exists to help identify,
map, and understand problems before searching for solutions.
