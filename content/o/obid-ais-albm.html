Canonical Definition

Authority Limitation & Boundary Module (ALBM) defines the structural
conditions under which authority over a governed identity remains
limited, bounded, enforceable, and continuously governable throughout
the complete identity lifecycle.

ALBM governs authority limitations.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every authority operates only within its legitimate scope and
never exceeds its authorized governance boundaries.

Authority requires limits.

Governance enforces those limits.

ALBM governs those boundaries.

Module Operational Role

ALBM defines the authority limitation layer of AIS.

Its role is to establish and preserve governance boundaries that
prevent unauthorized or excessive use of authority.

Module Operational Space

ALBM governs:

  • authority boundaries,
  • authority limitations,
  • governance constraints,
  • scope of authority,
  • boundary enforcement,
  • authorized governance actions,
  • authority restrictions,
  • governance boundary control.


Module Function

The module applies wherever governance authority must operate within
predefined limits and governance boundaries.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Authority Boundary Object

Identify the authority relationship requiring governance boundaries.

2. Define Boundary Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing the scope, limits,
restrictions, and permitted authority actions.

3. Define Boundary Failure Logic

Identify conditions where authority exceeds its scope, violates
governance boundaries, becomes uncontrolled, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for restriction, suspension, escalation,
corrective action, or authority revocation.

5. Preserve Boundary Records

Preserve authority limitations, governance decisions, boundary
definitions, supporting evidence, and enforcement history.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Identity Governance

Scenario

An identity administrator is authorized to manage user identities but
not ownership transfers.

Application

ALBM defines and enforces the governance boundaries of the
administrator's authority.

Result

Authority remains limited to its legitimate governance scope.

Use Case 2 — AI Identity Administration

Scenario

A service provider manages operational AI identities but is prohibited
from changing ownership or governance policies.

Application

ALBM establishes the authority boundaries governing permitted actions.

Result

Operational authority remains controlled, enforceable, and
continuously governable.

Canonical Closing Statement

Authority earns trust not only through legitimacy, but through
restraint. Authority Limitation & Boundary preserves governance
integrity by ensuring that every authority remains within its
authorized boundaries throughout the complete identity lifecycle.
