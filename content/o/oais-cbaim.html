Canonical Definition

Consequence-Bearing Authority Integrity Module (CBAIM) defines the structural conditions
under which operational authority affecting real operational outcomes,
consequence-bearing delegation states, escalation-sensitive authority conditions, and
distributed authority legitimacy remain materially stable, traceable, and operationally
governable across runtime operational environments.

A system satisfies CBAIM only if:
  • consequence-bearing operational authority remains materially legitimate
  • runtime authority continuity preserves governance-valid alignment
  • escalation-sensitive authority remains operationally traceable
  • distributed authority legitimacy remains coherent
  • consequence-bearing authority corruption does not destabilize
  • operational legitimacy


A system that preserves runtime execution while authority affecting real operational
outcomes materially destabilizes does

not satisfy CBAIM.

Module Operational Role

CBAIM defines the consequence-bearing authority governance layer of OAIS by preserving
governance-valid authority legitimacy across runtime systems affecting real operational
outcomes.

Module Operational Space

CBAIM governs the consequence-bearing authority space of OAIS by preserving runtime
authority legitimacy, escalation- sensitive authority continuity, distributed
operational coordination, delegated authority governance, and consequence- bearing
authority stability across runtime systems.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:
  • consequence-bearing operational authority
  • runtime authority legitimacy
  • escalation-sensitive authority continuity
  • distributed authority coordination
  • delegated authority governance
  • operational legitimacy continuity


Its function is to ensure that operational authority affecting real operational outcomes
remains materially legitimate strongly enough to preserve governance-valid runtime execution
across operational environments.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Consequence-Bearing Authority Object

The organization must define which authority structures affecting real operational outcomes
require governance preservation.

This may include:
  • delegated runtime authority
  • escalation-sensitive authority pathways
  • autonomous operational authorization
  • distributed authority hierarchies
  • runtime override authority
  • consequence-bearing operational delegation
  • operational-critical authority chains


2. Define Consequence-Bearing Authority Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which operational authority affecting real
operational outcomes remains materially stable and operationally aligned.

This includes:
  • runtime authority legitimacy
  • escalation continuity
  • distributed authority coherence
  • delegated authority stability
  • governance-valid operational alignment


3. Define Consequence-Bearing Authority Corruption Detection Logic

The system must define how materially unstable authority conditions or authority corruption
is identified.

This may include:
  • unauthorized operational execution
  • escalation-authority instability
  • distributed legitimacy fragmentation
  • delegated authority divergence
  • consequence-bearing authority misalignment


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for materially unstable consequence-bearing
authority conditions.

Governance response may include:
  • authority synchronization
  • escalation review activation
  • delegation restriction
  • operational legitimacy reconstruction
  • distributed coordination stabilization
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of consequence-bearing authority
continuity and authority- corruption states. A system must not remain authority-valid if
operational authority affecting real operational outcomes materially destabilizes while
systems continue assuming governance-valid runtime legitimacy remains preserved.
