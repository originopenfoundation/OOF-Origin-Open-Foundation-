Canonical Definition

Ownership Evidence Module (OOEM) defines the structural conditions
under which evidence supporting ownership of a governed identity
remains authentic, attributable, verifiable, preserved, and
continuously governable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

OOEM governs ownership evidence.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every ownership relationship is supported by sufficient,
trustworthy, and independently verifiable evidence.

Ownership claims require evidence.

Governance preserves that evidence.

OOEM governs that evidence.

Module Operational Role

OOEM defines the ownership evidence layer of OWIS.

Its role is to preserve trustworthy evidence supporting the legitimacy
of ownership throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Module Operational Space

OOEM governs:

  • ownership evidence,
  • ownership proof,
  • evidence preservation,
  • ownership verification,
  • evidence integrity,
  • governance evidence,
  • ownership documentation,
  • trustworthy ownership records.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must preserve evidence
demonstrating legitimate ownership of a governed identity.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Ownership Evidence Object

Identify the evidence required to support ownership of the
governed identity.

2. Define Evidence Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing ownership evidence
authenticity, preservation, attribution, and verification.

3. Define Evidence Failure Logic

Identify conditions where ownership evidence becomes incomplete,
manipulated, unverifiable, inconsistent, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for evidence verification, additional
evidence requests, investigation, correction, or
governance escalation.

5. Preserve Ownership Evidence Records

Preserve ownership evidence, governance decisions, verification
history, supporting documentation, and audit records.

Use Case 1 — AI Ownership Verification

Scenario

An organization must demonstrate legitimate ownership of a governed AI
identity during an external governance review.

Application

OOEM provides the preserved evidence supporting the
ownership relationship.

Result

Ownership is independently verified using governance-valid evidence.

Use Case 2 — Digital Asset Ownership Audit

Scenario

A governed digital asset undergoes a compliance audit requiring proof
of ownership.

Application

Result

continuously governance-valid.

Canonical Closing Statement

Ownership may be claimed by many, but it is proven through evidence.
Ownership Evidence preserves the verifiable foundation that allows
legitimate ownership to withstand time, scrutiny, and
governance review.
