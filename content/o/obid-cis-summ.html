Canonical Definition

Succession Module (SUMM) defines the structural conditions under which
governance succession associated with a governed identity is
established, transferred, preserved, and continuously governable
throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

SUMM governs identity succession.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every legitimate successor preserves the continuity, legitimacy,
accountability, and governance relationships of the governed identity.

Participants may change.

Governed identity must continue.

SUMM governs that succession.

Module Operational Role

SUMM defines the succession layer of CIS.

Its role is to preserve uninterrupted governance continuity whenever
responsibility for a governed identity passes to a
legitimate successor.

Module Operational Space

SUMM governs:

  • identity succession,
  • governance succession,
  • successor assignment,
  • succession continuity,
  • governance transition,
  • succession authorization,
  • long-term governance,
  • identity continuity.


Module Function

The module applies wherever governance responsibilities associated
with a governed identity must continue through legitimate succession.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Succession Object

Identify the governed identity and the governance responsibilities
requiring succession.

2. Define Succession Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing successor eligibility,
authorization, continuity, provenance preservation, and
governance approval.

3. Define Succession Failure Logic

Identify conditions where succession becomes unauthorized,
interrupted, ambiguous, incomplete, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for succession approval, rejection,
reassignment, investigation, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Succession Records

Preserve succession decisions, governance approvals, supporting
evidence, continuity records, and succession history.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Identity Governance

Scenario

The governance responsibility for a strategic enterprise identity is
transferred to a newly appointed executive.

Application

SUMM governs the succession while preserving complete
governance continuity.

Result

The identity remains continuously governed without interrupting
accountability or governance integrity.

Use Case 2 — AI Identity Governance

Scenario

Responsibility for a governed AI identity is transferred from one
operational team to another following organizational restructuring.

Application

SUMM preserves governance continuity and validates the
legitimate successor.

Result

The governed identity continues without breaking its governance
relationships or lifecycle continuity.

Canonical Closing Statement

Continuity begins with legitimate succession. Succession preserves
governance trust by ensuring that every governed identity continues
through authorized successors without losing its integrity, history,
or governance relationships throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.
