Canonical Definition

Ownership Assignment Module (OOAM) defines the structural conditions
under which ownership of a governed identity is established, assigned,
recorded, and continuously governable throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.

OOAM governs ownership assignment.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every governed identity is assigned to a legitimate owner through
an authorized, attributable, and verifiable governance process.

Ownership does not appear automatically.

Ownership must be legitimately assigned.

OOAM governs that assignment.

Module Operational Role

OOAM defines the ownership assignment layer of OWIS.

Its role is to establish legitimate ownership before governance
authority, permissions, and responsibilities are exercised.

Module Operational Space

OOAM governs:

  • ownership assignment,
  • owner identification,
  • ownership establishment,
  • ownership registration,
  • governance assignment,
  • ownership attribution,
  • initial ownership governance,
  • legitimate ownership creation.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must establish and govern
the initial ownership of a governed identity.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Ownership Object

Identify the governed identity requiring legitimate ownership.

2. Define Ownership Assignment Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing how legitimate ownership is
assigned and recorded.

3. Define Assignment Failure Logic

Identify conditions where ownership assignment becomes unauthorized,
ambiguous, duplicated, invalid, or governance- invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for ownership approval, rejection,
correction, reassignment, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Ownership Assignment Records

Preserve ownership assignments, governance decisions, attribution
records, supporting evidence, and ownership history.

Use Case 1 — AI Identity Registration

Scenario

An organization creates a new governed AI identity for
enterprise operations.

Application

OOAM assigns the governed identity to its legitimate organizational
owner before activation.

Result

Ownership is established through a documented and governance-approved
assignment process.

Use Case 2 — Digital Asset Identity

Scenario

A governed digital asset is registered within an enterprise
identity ecosystem.

Application

OOAM establishes the legitimate owner before the asset enters
operational use.

Result

The governed identity begins its lifecycle with clear, attributable,
and governance-valid ownership.

Canonical Closing Statement

Ownership becomes trustworthy only when its beginning is trustworthy.
Ownership Assignment establishes the first legitimate governance
relationship between a governed identity and its rightful owner.
