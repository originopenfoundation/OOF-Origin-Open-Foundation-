About Continuity Integrity Standard (CIS)

Governing Continuity Integrity

Continuity Integrity Standard (CIS) defines the governance conditions
under which a governed identity preserves its continuity, legitimacy,
traceability, provenance, governance relationships, and continuous
governability throughout the complete identity lifecycle despite
legitimate lifecycle changes.

Governed identities may evolve.

They may be inherited, merged, replaced, or transferred.

CIS governs this identity governance space.

What Is Continuity Integrity?

Continuity integrity is the governed condition in which a governed
identity remains continuously recognizable, attributable, traceable,
and governance-valid despite legitimate lifecycle transitions.

It enables organizations to preserve identity continuity across
succession, inheritance, mergers, replacements, platform changes, and
other governance-approved lifecycle events without compromising
governance trust.

Canonical Definition

Continuity Integrity Standard (CIS) defines the structural conditions
under which a governed identity preserves its continuity, legitimacy,
traceability, provenance, governance relationships, and continuous
governability throughout the complete identity lifecycle despite
legitimate lifecycle changes.

Why CIS Exists

Governed identities are expected to exist beyond individual owners,
organizations, technologies, and operational environments.

Without governed continuity, governance relationships become
fragmented, provenance loses meaning, long-term accountability
weakens, and identity trust cannot be sustained.

CIS exists because Continuity Integrity represents the ninth
governable space of OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity
Governance Architecture.

What CIS Governs

CIS governs the structural conditions under which identity
continuity remains:

  • continuous,
  • legitimate,
  • traceable,
  • attributable,
  • verifiable,
  • governable.


Rather than governing backup systems, synchronization technologies, or
technical migration processes, CIS governs the governance conditions
required for trustworthy continuity integrity.

Architectural Position

Continuity Integrity Standard serves as the ninth governance space of
OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture.

It establishes the governance foundation that preserves the identity
itself across legitimate lifecycle transitions before complete
governance auditability is established.

Without governed continuity, trustworthy identity governance cannot
survive the passage of time.

Canonical Closing Statement

Time changes identities, but governance must preserve their
continuity. Continuity Integrity ensures that every governed identity
remains continuously recognizable, trustworthy, and governable
throughout its complete identity lifecycle.
