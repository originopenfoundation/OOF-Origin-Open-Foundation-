About Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture (OBIDENITY®)

Governing Identity

Identity exists everywhere.

Every person, organization, AI system, autonomous agent, digital
asset, robotic system, and governed object depends upon identity.

Technology evolves.

Platforms change.

Identity providers change.

Implementation models change.

Identity governance must remain stable.

Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture (OBIDENITY®) was created
to provide a stable identity governance architecture that remains
applicable regardless of how identity technologies continue to evolve.

OBIDENITY® does not govern identity providers, authentication systems,
credentials, registries, software platforms, or
implementation technologies.

OBIDENITY® governs identity.

Why OBIDENITY® Exists

Most identity initiatives focus on authentication, authorization,
access management, digital credentials, identity providers, security,
or technical implementation.

These perspectives are essential, but they do not fully answer a more
fundamental question:

How should identity itself be governed?

OBIDENITY® addresses this question by defining the governable identity
spaces that exist throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Rather than standardizing identity technology, OBIDENITY® standardizes
the governance conditions that enable identity to remain uniquely
identifiable, attributable, ownable, governable, transferable,
continuous, and independently verifiable.

Identity Before Technology

The objective of OBIDENITY® is not to define how identities
are implemented.

The objective is to define how identity remains governable.

Every identifiable entity possesses identity.

Every identity creates governance relationships.

Every governance relationship requires trustworthy identity.

OBIDENITY® therefore treats identity as an independent
governance domain.

The Identity Governance Architecture

OBIDENITY® consists of ten independent identity governance spaces.

Each space answers one architectural question.

Each space governs one identity condition.

Each space begins where the previous space ends.

Together these spaces form the complete identity
governance architecture.

The architecture progresses through:

Origin → Identity → Ownership → Authority → Permission →
Responsibility → Provenance → Transfer → Continuity → Auditability

This progression represents the complete governance journey of
identity—from establishing legitimate origin to independently
verifying the complete identity lifecycle.

Identity Governance Spaces Rather Than Technical Components

OBIDENITY® does not organize identity governance around
software components.

Instead, it organizes governance around identity spaces.

Each identity governance space represents a distinct governance
responsibility that exists independently of any specific identity
platform, authentication mechanism, identity provider, registry,
vendor, or implementation technology.

Because identity governance spaces are technology-neutral, the
architecture remains applicable even as identity technologies continue
to evolve.

A Methodology Rather Than an Implementation

OBIDENITY® is a governance methodology.

It is not an implementation framework.

The architecture defines what identity governance conditions must
remain true.

Organizations remain free to determine how those conditions are
implemented within their own operational, regulatory, organizational,
and technical environments.

This separation allows OBIDENITY® to remain stable while
implementation methods continue to evolve.

The Long-Term Vision

As identities increasingly move across organizations, platforms,
autonomous systems, AI ecosystems, digital assets, and future
intelligent infrastructures, identity governance will become as
fundamental as identity itself.

The long-term objective of OBIDENITY® is to provide a common identity
governance language that enables organizations to identify, classify,
govern, transfer, preserve, audit, and continuously protect identity
regardless of future technological change.

Canonical Principle

Identity technologies will continue to evolve. Identity governance
must remain stable. OBIDENITY® therefore governs the structural
conditions that remain valid regardless of future identity
technologies, platforms, and implementation models.

Canonical Closing Statement

Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture (OBIDENITY®) provides
the identity governance foundation through which identity can be
established, governed, attributed, transferred, preserved, audited,
and continuously verified. By defining the governable spaces of
identity, OBIDENITY® establishes a technology-neutral methodology for
ensuring that identity remains trustworthy, attributable, governable,
and continuous throughout its complete lifecycle.
