Canonical Definition

Authority Verification Module (AVAM) defines the structural conditions
under which authority over a governed identity remains authentic,
legitimate, verifiable, evidence-supported, and continuously
governable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

AVAM governs authority verification.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every exercise of authority can be independently verified before
governance actions are recognized as legitimate.

Authority must not only exist.

Authority must be verifiable.

AVAM governs that verification.

Module Operational Role

AVAM defines the authority verification layer of AIS.

Its role is to verify that every governance authority remains
legitimate before governance rights are exercised.

Module Operational Space

AVAM governs:

  • authority verification,
  • authority authentication,
  • authority legitimacy,
  • governance verification,
  • authority evidence,
  • authority validation,
  • trusted governance authority,
  • continuous authority verification.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must verify governance
authority before permitting actions affecting a governed identity.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Authority Verification Object

Identify the authority relationship requiring verification.

2. Define Verification Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing authority legitimacy,
evidence requirements, verification methods, and governance approval.

3. Define Verification Failure Logic

Identify conditions where authority becomes unverifiable, expired,
unauthorized, inconsistent, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for authority approval, rejection,
suspension, investigation, revalidation, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Verification Records

Preserve verification results, governance decisions, supporting
evidence, authority history, and audit records.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Identity Administration

Scenario

A governance administrator requests authorization to modify a
strategic enterprise identity.

Application

AVAM verifies that the administrator possesses legitimate governance
authority before the request is approved.

Result

Only verified authorities may perform governance actions affecting the
governed identity.

Use Case 2 — Cross-Platform Identity Governance

Scenario

A governed identity is administered across multiple
operational platforms.

Application

AVAM verifies the legitimacy of authority before governance actions
are accepted on each platform.

Result

Authority remains trusted, consistent, and continuously governable
across the complete identity lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Authority is trusted because it can be verified. Authority
Verification preserves governance confidence by ensuring that every
governance action is exercised only by a legitimately verified
authority throughout the complete identity lifecycle.
