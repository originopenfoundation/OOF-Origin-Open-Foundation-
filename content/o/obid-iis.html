Identity Integrity

Core Question

What exactly is the governed identity?

Canonical Definition

Identity Integrity Standard (IIS) defines the structural conditions
under which a governed identity remains unique, authentic,
distinguishable, classifiable, verifiable, and continuously governable
throughout its complete lifecycle.

IIS governs identity integrity.

The standard establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every governed identity remains uniquely identifiable,
consistently represented, and protected from ambiguity, duplication,
substitution, or identity corruption.

Every governed identity requires a unique identity.

Governance preserves the integrity of that identity.

IIS governs that integrity.

Governance Flow Position

Origin → Identity → Ownership → Authority → Permission →
Responsibility → Provenance → Transfer → Continuity → Auditability

Standard Abstract

Origin establishes where a governed identity begins.

Identity establishes what the governed entity is.

Without governed identity:

  • uniqueness cannot be guaranteed,
  • attribution becomes unreliable,
  • ownership cannot be established,
  • authority becomes ambiguous,
  • governance loses precision.


IIS establishes the governance conditions required for trustworthy
identity integrity throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Operational Role

Identity Integrity Standard provides the governance layer responsible
for preserving the uniqueness, authenticity, classification,
verification, and lifecycle integrity of governed identities.

Its role is to ensure that every governed identity remains
consistently identifiable throughout its complete lifecycle.

Operational Space

IIS governs:

  • identity uniqueness,
  • identity validation,
  • identity classification,
  • identity resolution,
  • identity lifecycle continuity,
  • governed identity integrity,
  • identity authenticity,
  • trustworthy identity management.


This governed space ensures that every identity remains unique,
verifiable, and continuously governable throughout its
complete lifecycle.

Why This Standard Exists

Governed identity cannot exist without a trustworthy identity.

Origin establishes where identity begins.

Identity establishes what exists.

IIS exists because Identity Integrity represents the second governable
space of OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture.
