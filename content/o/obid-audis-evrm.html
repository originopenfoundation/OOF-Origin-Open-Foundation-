Canonical Definition

Evidence Verification Module (EVRM) defines the structural conditions
under which evidence supporting a governed identity is independently
verified, authenticated, validated, and continuously governable
throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

EVRM governs evidence verification.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that governance evidence remains authentic, reliable, complete,
independently verifiable, and suitable for governance decisions.

Evidence supports governance.

Verification confirms evidence.

EVRM governs that verification.

Module Operational Role

EVRM defines the evidence verification layer of AUDIS.

Its role is to verify that governance evidence supporting a governed
identity remains authentic and trustworthy.

Module Operational Space

EVRM governs:

  • evidence verification,
  • governance evidence,
  • evidence authentication,
  • evidence validation,
  • governance proof,
  • evidence review,
  • evidence assurance,
  • trusted governance evidence.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must independently verify
evidence supporting the governance of a governed identity.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Evidence Verification Object

Identify the governance evidence requiring independent verification.

2. Define Verification Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing evidence authenticity,
completeness, consistency, traceability, and validation.

3. Define Verification Failure Logic

Identify conditions where evidence becomes incomplete, altered,
unreliable, unverifiable, inconsistent, or governance- invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for verification, investigation, correction,
rejection, revalidation, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Verification Records

Preserve verification results, governance decisions, supporting
evidence, review history, validation records, and audit documentation.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Identity Governance

Scenario

An organization reviews evidence supporting ownership and authority
changes associated with a strategic governed identity.

Application

EVRM independently verifies the authenticity and integrity of the
supporting governance evidence.

Result

Governance decisions rely only on verified and trustworthy evidence.

Use Case 2 — Cross-Platform AI Identity

Scenario

A governed AI identity accumulates governance evidence across multiple
operational platforms.

Application

EVRM verifies the authenticity and consistency of the collected
evidence before governance decisions are approved.

Result

The identity remains supported by independently verified governance
evidence throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence creates confidence only after it has been verified. Evidence
Verification preserves governance trust by ensuring that every
governance decision rests upon authentic, independently validated, and
continuously trustworthy evidence throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.
