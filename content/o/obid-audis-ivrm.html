Canonical Definition

Integrity Verification Module (IVRM) defines the structural conditions
under which the overall governance integrity of a governed identity is
independently verified, evaluated, validated, and continuously
governable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

IVRM governs integrity verification.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that the complete governance state of a governed identity remains
authentic, internally consistent, evidence-supported, and
independently verifiable.

Governance produces integrity.

Verification confirms integrity.

IVRM governs that confirmation.

Module Operational Role

IVRM defines the integrity verification layer of AUDIS.

Its role is to perform holistic governance verification across the
complete lifecycle of a governed identity.

Module Operational Space

IVRM governs:

  • integrity verification,
  • governance integrity,
  • holistic governance validation,
  • governance consistency,
  • identity integrity assessment,
  • governance assurance,
  • integrity evidence,
  • governance verification.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must independently verify
the complete governance integrity of a governed identity.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Integrity Verification Object

Identify the governed identity and the governance elements requiring
overall integrity verification.

2. Define Verification Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing integrity criteria, evidence
requirements, consistency validation, traceability, and
governance approval.

3. Define Verification Failure Logic

Identify conditions where governance integrity becomes inconsistent,
unverifiable, incomplete, contradictory, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for corrective measures, revalidation,
investigation, governance review, suspension, or escalation.

5. Preserve Verification Records

Preserve integrity assessments, governance decisions, supporting
evidence, verification reports, audit findings, and
lifecycle documentation.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Identity Governance

Scenario

An organization performs a comprehensive governance integrity
assessment before certifying a strategic enterprise identity.

Application

IVRM evaluates the complete governance state across origin, ownership,
authority, permissions, responsibility, provenance, transfer,
continuity, and auditability.

Result

The identity receives an independently verified governance
integrity assessment.

Use Case 2 — AI Identity Governance

Scenario

A governed AI identity is reviewed before becoming part of a
cross-organizational identity ecosystem.

Application

IVRM verifies the complete governance integrity of the identity using
evidence collected throughout its lifecycle.

Result

The AI identity enters the new governance environment with
independently verified governance integrity and preserved
governance trust.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity is not assumed—it is demonstrated. Integrity Verification
preserves governance trust by confirming that every layer of a
governed identity remains authentic, consistent, evidence-supported,
and continuously governable throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.
