Canonical Definition

Identity Resolution Module (IRSM) defines the structural conditions
under which a governed identity can be uniquely resolved, accurately
recognized, correctly matched, and continuously governable throughout
the complete identity lifecycle.

IRSM governs identity resolution.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every governed identity can be resolved to the correct governed
entity without ambiguity, conflict, or misidentification.

Identity must be unique.

Identity must also be resolvable.

IRSM governs that resolution.

Module Operational Role

IRSM defines the identity resolution layer of IIS.

Its role is to ensure that every governed identity can be accurately
identified and resolved whenever governance decisions require
identity verification.

Module Operational Space

IRSM governs:

  • identity resolution,
  • identity matching,
  • identity recognition,
  • identity conflict resolution,
  • governance identity lookup,
  • identity correlation,
  • governed identity resolution,
  • trustworthy identity recognition.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must accurately resolve a
governed identity to the correct governed entity across one or more
operational environments.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Identity Resolution Object

Identify the governed identity that must be resolved.

2. Define Resolution Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing identity matching,
recognition, correlation, and resolution accuracy.

3. Define Resolution Failure Logic

Identify conditions where identity resolution becomes ambiguous,
conflicting, incorrect, incomplete, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for identity verification, conflict
resolution, manual review, correction, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Resolution Records

Preserve identity resolution results, governance decisions, conflict
history, supporting evidence, and related documentation.

Use Case 1 — Multi-Platform AI Identity

Scenario

A governed AI identity operates across several independent
operational platforms.

Application

IRSM resolves the identity to the correct governed entity before
governance actions are performed.

Result

The correct identity is consistently recognized across all
governed environments.

Use Case 2 — Enterprise Identity Federation

Scenario

A multinational organization integrates multiple identity systems into
a unified governance framework.

Application

IRSM resolves identities originating from different systems to the
appropriate governed identity.

Result

Identity conflicts are eliminated while governance remains accurate
and consistent.

Canonical Closing Statement

A unique identity has little value if it cannot be reliably
recognized. Identity Resolution ensures that every governance decision
begins with the correct governed identity.
