Canonical Definition

Origin Provenance Attribution Module (OPAM) defines the structural
conditions under which the provenance of a governed identity remains
attributable, traceable, verifiable, and continuously governable
throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

OPAM governs origin provenance attribution.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every governed identity maintains a provable relationship between
its origin, provenance history, and legitimate attribution.

Origin establishes existence.

Provenance preserves history.

OPAM governs that attribution.

Module Operational Role

OPAM defines the origin provenance attribution layer of OIS.

Its role is to preserve trustworthy attribution between a governed
identity, its legitimate origin, and its complete provenance history.

Module Operational Space

OPAM governs:

  • origin provenance attribution,
  • provenance linkage,
  • origin attribution,
  • provenance verification,
  • provenance traceability,
  • governance attribution,
  • historical identity linkage,
  • trustworthy provenance governance.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must preserve verifiable
attribution between a governed identity and its complete
provenance history.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Provenance Attribution Object

Identify the provenance information that must remain attributable to
the governed identity.

2. Define Provenance Attribution Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing provenance attribution,
verification, continuity, and traceability.

3. Define Provenance Failure Logic

Identify conditions where provenance becomes incomplete, inconsistent,
unverifiable, manipulated, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for provenance verification, correction,
investigation, reconciliation, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Provenance Attribution Records

Preserve provenance records, attribution history, governance
decisions, verification evidence, and supporting documentation.

Use Case 1 — AI Digital Identity

Scenario

A governed AI identity operates across multiple platforms while
generating a long operational history.

Application

OPAM preserves verifiable attribution linking every provenance event
back to the original governed identity.

Result

The complete provenance history remains trustworthy, attributable, and
continuously governable.

Use Case 2 — Digital Asset Ownership

Scenario

A governed digital asset changes custodians several times during
its lifecycle.

Application

OPAM preserves continuous provenance attribution connecting every
ownership event to the original governed origin.

Result

The asset maintains a complete, verifiable, and governance-valid
provenance history.

Canonical Closing Statement

Origin explains where identity begins. Provenance explains how
identity arrived here. Origin Provenance Attribution preserves that
complete governance story from the first verified origin to the latest
governed state.
