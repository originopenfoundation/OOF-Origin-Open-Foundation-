Canonical Definition

Authority Transfer Module (AUTM) defines the structural conditions
under which governance authority associated with a governed identity
is transferred while preserving legitimacy, accountability,
traceability, continuity, and continuous governability throughout the
complete identity lifecycle.

AUTM governs authority transfer.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that governance authority is transferred only through legitimate,
authorized, verifiable, and governance-approved transitions.

Authority may be transferred.

Governance legitimacy must remain continuous.

AUTM governs that transfer.

Module Operational Role

AUTM defines the authority transfer layer of TIS.

Its role is to preserve legitimate governance authority whenever
responsibility for governing a governed identity passes to another
authorized participant.

Module Operational Space

AUTM governs:

  • authority transfer,
  • governance authority transition,
  • delegated authority transfer,
  • authority succession,
  • governance continuity,
  • authority verification,
  • governance authorization,
  • authority transition records.


Module Function

The module applies wherever governance authority associated with a
governed identity must be transferred between legitimate
governance participants.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Authority Transfer Object

Identify the governed identity and the governance authority
requiring transfer.

2. Define Transfer Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing authority authorization,
acceptance, verification, limitations, and governance approval.

3. Define Transfer Failure Logic

Identify conditions where authority transfer becomes unauthorized,
incomplete, disputed, unverifiable, or governance- invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for approval, rejection, suspension,
reassignment, investigation, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Transfer Records

Preserve authority transfer decisions, governance approvals,
supporting evidence, transfer history, and authority records.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Identity Governance

Scenario

A senior identity administrator retires and governance authority is
transferred to a newly appointed administrator.

Application

AUTM governs the transition of authority while preserving
governance continuity.

Result

Authority changes without interrupting governance integrity
or accountability.

Use Case 2 — Cross-Organization AI Governance

Scenario

Governance authority over a governed AI identity is transferred from
one organization to another following a managed service transition.

Application

AUTM verifies and records the authority transfer before governance
rights become effective.

Result

Governance authority remains legitimate, traceable, and continuously
governable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Authority creates governance only when it is legitimately held.
Authority Transfer preserves governance continuity by ensuring that
every transfer of authority remains authorized, traceable, and
continuously trustworthy throughout the complete identity lifecycle.
