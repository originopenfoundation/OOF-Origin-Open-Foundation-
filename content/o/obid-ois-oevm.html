Canonical Definition

Origin Evidence Verification Module (OEVM) defines the structural
conditions under which evidence supporting the origin of a governed
identity remains authentic, verifiable, attributable, and continuously
governable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

OEVM governs origin evidence verification.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every governed identity origin is supported by sufficient,
trustworthy, and independently verifiable evidence.

Origin claims require evidence.

Governance verifies that evidence.

OEVM governs that verification.

Module Operational Role

OEVM defines the origin evidence verification layer of OIS.

Its role is to ensure that every governed identity origin can be
supported through reliable and verifiable evidence.

Module Operational Space

OEVM governs:

  • origin evidence verification,
  • evidence authenticity,
  • origin evidence validation,
  • evidence attribution,
  • evidence integrity,
  • governance evidence review,
  • origin proof validation,
  • trustworthy origin verification.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must verify evidence
supporting the legitimate origin of a governed identity.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Origin Evidence Object

Identify the evidence required to support the claimed origin of the
governed identity.

2. Define Evidence Verification Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing evidence authenticity,
sufficiency, attribution, and verification.

3. Define Evidence Failure Logic

Identify conditions where origin evidence becomes insufficient,
unverifiable, inconsistent, manipulated, or governance- invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for evidence verification, additional
evidence requests, investigation, rejection, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Evidence Verification Records

Preserve evidence records, verification results, governance decisions,
supporting documentation, and audit history.

Use Case 1 — Digital Identity Registration

Scenario

A new governed digital identity is submitted for registration within
an enterprise identity platform.

Application

OEVM verifies that the submitted origin evidence satisfies the
required governance conditions before the identity is accepted.

Result

Only identities supported by trustworthy origin evidence become
governed identities.

Use Case 2 — AI Identity Certification

Scenario

An AI provider requests certification for a governed AI identity.

Application

OEVM validates the evidence demonstrating the legitimate origin of the
AI identity before certification.

Result

Certification is granted only when the identity origin is supported by
independently verifiable evidence.

Canonical Closing Statement

Trust begins where evidence replaces assumption. Origin Evidence
Verification ensures that every governed identity is anchored to
origin through verifiable evidence rather than unsupported claims.
