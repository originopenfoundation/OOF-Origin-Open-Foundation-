Canonical Definition

Identity Validation Module (IVAM) defines the structural conditions
under which a governed identity remains valid, authentic, verifiable,
and continuously governable throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.

IVAM governs identity validation.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every governed identity can be independently verified before it
is accepted, trusted, or used within governed environments.

Identity claims require validation.

Governance verifies those claims.

IVAM governs that validation.

Module Operational Role

IVAM defines the identity validation layer of IIS.

Its role is to ensure that every governed identity satisfies
predefined governance validation requirements before being recognized
as a trusted identity.

Module Operational Space

IVAM governs:

  • identity validation,
  • identity verification,
  • identity authenticity,
  • validation assurance,
  • governance validation,
  • identity legitimacy,
  • trusted identity verification,
  • continuous identity validation.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must verify the validity of
governed identities before operational use.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Identity Validation Object

Identify the governed identity requiring validation.

2. Define Validation Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing identity verification,
authenticity, and validation requirements.

3. Define Validation Failure Logic

Identify conditions where identity becomes unverifiable, invalid,
inconsistent, fraudulent, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for validation, rejection, investigation,
correction, suspension, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Validation Records

Preserve validation results, governance decisions, supporting
evidence, validation history, and related documentation.

Use Case 1 — AI Identity Registration

Scenario

An enterprise AI platform registers a new governed AI identity.

Application

IVAM validates the identity before operational activation.

Result

Only verified and governance-approved identities become trusted
operational identities.

Use Case 2 — Cross-Platform Identity Verification

Scenario

A governed identity is presented across multiple organizations and
digital platforms.

Application

IVAM independently validates the identity before it is accepted within
each governed environment.

Result

Identity trust is preserved through consistent and repeatable
governance validation.

Canonical Closing Statement

A claimed identity becomes a trusted identity only after validation.
Identity Validation establishes the governance confidence required for
every governed identity to be recognized, accepted, and relied upon.
