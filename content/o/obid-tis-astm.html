Canonical Definition

Asset Transfer Module (ASTM) defines the structural conditions under
which assets associated with a governed identity are transferred while
preserving ownership integrity, provenance, accountability,
continuity, and continuous governability throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.

ASTM governs asset transfer.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every asset transfer remains legitimate, traceable, verifiable,
and governance-approved without compromising the integrity of the
governed identity.

Assets may be transferred.

Governance relationships must remain intact.

ASTM governs that transfer.

Module Operational Role

ASTM defines the asset transfer layer of TIS.

Its role is to preserve governance integrity whenever governed assets
are transferred between legitimate participants.

Module Operational Space

ASTM governs:

  • asset transfer,
  • governed asset migration,
  • ownership-linked assets,
  • asset transition,
  • asset provenance,
  • governance-approved transfer,
  • asset continuity,
  • governed asset movement.


Module Function

The module applies wherever assets linked to a governed identity must
be transferred between legitimate governance participants.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Asset Transfer Object

Identify the governed asset and the governed identity associated with
the transfer.

2. Define Transfer Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing authorization, ownership
verification, provenance preservation, continuity, and
governance approval.

3. Define Transfer Failure Logic

Identify conditions where asset transfer becomes unauthorized,
incomplete, inconsistent, unverifiable, disputed,
or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for approval, suspension, correction,
investigation, rejection, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Transfer Records

Preserve asset transfer records, governance approvals, supporting
evidence, provenance documentation, and transfer history.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Digital Asset

Scenario

A governed software license, together with its governed identity, is
transferred to a newly acquired business entity.

Application

ASTM governs the transfer while preserving ownership, provenance, and
governance continuity.

Result

The asset remains legitimately governed throughout the
ownership transition.

Use Case 2 — AI Identity Asset Governance

Scenario

A governed AI identity transfers associated models, credentials, and
operational assets to a successor organization.

Application

ASTM validates and governs the complete asset transfer process.

Result

The transfer preserves governance integrity, provenance, and
continuous trust across the complete identity lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Assets derive lasting trust from the governance that follows them.
Asset Transfer preserves that trust by ensuring that every governed
asset moves together with its legitimate governance relationships
throughout the complete identity lifecycle.
