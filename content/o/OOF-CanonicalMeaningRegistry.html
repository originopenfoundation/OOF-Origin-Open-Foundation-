Canonical Definition

OOF Canonical Meaning Registry™ (OOFCMR™) is a Governed
Meaning Registry Infrastructure™ developed by OOF® for the registration,
validation, classification, traceability, governance, and lifecycle
management of meanings across organizations, systems, institutions,
operational environments, and AI ecosystems.

OOFCMR™ does not determine what organizations must mean.

OOFCMR™ provides the methodological conditions under which
meanings can become identifiable, validated, traceable, governable, and
interoperable.

Its purpose is not to impose meaning.

Its purpose is to govern meaning through registration.

OOFCMR™ does not decide what every organization must mean.

Meaning Validation™

A meaning may exist without registration.

A meaning may exist without governance.

However, a meaning that is not validated cannot be reliably governed.

Meaning Validation™ evaluates whether a meaning is:
  • sufficiently defined
  • operationally usable
  • internally consistent
  • traceable
  • non-conflicting
  • suitable for its intended scope
Validation does not determine whether a meaning is universally correct.

Validation determines whether a meaning is sufficiently governed for
reliable use.

Meaning Ownership™

Every critical meaning should have a responsible owner.

Ownership establishes:
  • accountability
  • approval authority
  • change control
  • governance responsibility
Without ownership, meanings drift.

Without ownership, governance becomes difficult.
