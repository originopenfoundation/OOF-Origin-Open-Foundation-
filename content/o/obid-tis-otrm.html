Canonical Definition

Ownership Transfer Module (OTRM) defines the structural conditions
under which ownership of a governed identity is transferred while
preserving legitimacy, provenance, accountability, continuity, and
continuous governability throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

OTRM governs ownership transfer.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every ownership transfer remains authorized, verifiable,
traceable, and governance-approved before ownership changes
become effective.

Ownership may change.

Identity integrity must not.

OTRM governs that transfer.

Module Operational Role

OTRM defines the ownership transfer layer of TIS.

Its role is to govern the legitimate transfer of ownership while
preserving the complete governance history of the governed identity.

Module Operational Space

OTRM governs:

  • ownership transfer,
  • ownership succession,
  • ownership reassignment,
  • governance-approved ownership change,
  • ownership verification,
  • ownership continuity,
  • transfer authorization,
  • ownership governance.


Module Function

The module applies wherever ownership of a governed identity must be
transferred between legitimate participants.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Ownership Transfer Object

Identify the governed identity and the ownership relationship
requiring transfer.

2. Define Transfer Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing ownership authorization,
verification, acceptance, traceability, and governance approval.

3. Define Transfer Failure Logic

Identify conditions where ownership transfer becomes unauthorized,
disputed, incomplete, unverifiable, or governance- invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for approval, rejection, suspension,
investigation, correction, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Transfer Records

Preserve ownership transfer records, governance decisions, supporting
evidence, provenance updates, and transfer history.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Digital Identity

Scenario

A strategic enterprise identity is transferred to a new legal owner
following a corporate acquisition.

Application

OTRM governs the ownership transition and preserves the complete
governance history.

Result

Ownership changes without compromising identity integrity or
governance continuity.

Use Case 2 — AI Identity Governance

Scenario

Ownership of a governed AI identity is transferred from one
organization to another.

Application

OTRM verifies the legitimacy of the transfer and preserves the
complete provenance chain.

Result

The AI identity retains continuous governance integrity throughout the
ownership transition.

Canonical Closing Statement

Ownership may legitimately change, but governance trust must never
restart. Ownership Transfer preserves identity integrity by ensuring
that every new owner inherits a governed identity with its complete
history, legitimacy, and continuity intact throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.
