Governed Space

Ownership Integrity

Core Question

Who legitimately owns the governed identity?

Canonical Definition

Ownership Integrity Standard (OWIS) defines the structural conditions
under which ownership of a governed identity remains legitimate,
attributable, verifiable, transferable, and continuously governable
throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

OWIS governs ownership integrity.

The standard establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that ownership of every governed identity remains clearly established,
continuously verifiable, and protected against unauthorized claims,
conflicts, or unlawful transfer.

Governed identity requires legitimate ownership.

Governance preserves that ownership.

OWIS governs that ownership.

Governance Flow Position

Origin → Identity → Ownership → Authority → Permission →
Responsibility → Provenance → Transfer → Continuity → Auditability

Standard Abstract

Origin establishes where identity begins.

Identity establishes what exists.

Ownership establishes to whom the governed identity
legitimately belongs.

Without governed ownership:

  • ownership cannot be proven,
  • ownership conflicts emerge,
  • authority loses legitimacy,
  • permissions become unreliable,
  • accountability becomes uncertain.


OWIS establishes the governance conditions required for trustworthy
ownership throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Operational Role

Ownership Integrity Standard provides the governance layer responsible
for establishing, validating, preserving, transferring, and protecting
legitimate ownership of governed identities.

Its role is to ensure that ownership remains continuously
attributable, verifiable, and governance-valid.

Operational Space

OWIS governs:

  • ownership assignment,
  • ownership validation,
  • shared ownership,
  • ownership conflict resolution,
  • ownership evidence,
  • governed ownership,
  • ownership continuity,
  • trustworthy ownership governance.


This governed space ensures that ownership remains legitimate and
continuously governable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Why This Standard Exists

A governed identity may exist without being used.

It cannot participate in governance without legitimate ownership.

OWIS exists because Ownership Integrity represents the third
governable space of OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity
Governance Architecture.
