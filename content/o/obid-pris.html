Governed Space

Identity Provenance

Core Question

Can the complete history of a governed identity be proven?

Canonical Definition

Provenance Integrity Standard (PRIS) defines the structural conditions
under which the provenance of a governed identity remains authentic,
complete, traceable, verifiable, evidence-supported, and continuously
governable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

PRIS governs identity provenance.

The standard establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every significant event, relationship, modification, transfer,
and governance decision contributing to the history of a governed
identity remains permanently attributable and
independently verifiable.

Identity has an origin.

Identity creates a history.

PRIS governs that history.

Governance Flow Position

Origin → Identity → Ownership → Authority → Permission →
Responsibility → Provenance → Transfer → Continuity → Auditability

Standard Abstract

A governed identity accumulates history throughout its lifecycle.

Every governance action contributes to its provenance.

Without governed provenance:

  • identity history becomes fragmented,
  • governance evidence may disappear,
  • attribution becomes unreliable,
  • historical trust deteriorates,
  • governance reconstruction becomes impossible.


PRIS establishes the governance conditions required for trustworthy,
continuous, and independently verifiable identity provenance.

Operational Role

Provenance Integrity Standard provides the governance layer
responsible for governing how the history of a governed identity is
created, preserved, reconstructed, verified, and
continuously governed.

Its role is to ensure that the complete provenance of every governed
identity remains authentic, traceable, evidence- supported,
and governance-approved.

Operational Space

PRIS governs:

  • provenance chains,
  • event traceability,
  • identity history,
  • governance events,
  • evidence preservation,
  • provenance verification,
  • historical integrity,
  • identity provenance.


This governed space ensures that provenance remains continuously
compatible with trustworthy identity governance.

Why This Standard Exists

Trustworthy identity requires more than knowing what an identity
is today.

Governance must also preserve how that identity became what it is.

Without governed provenance, trust cannot be
independently reconstructed.

PRIS exists because Identity Provenance represents the seventh
governable space of OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity
Governance Architecture.

Trustworthy identity requires trustworthy provenance. Every governed

Identity is trusted not only because it exists, but because its

permanently connected to its authentic and verifiable
lifecycle history.
