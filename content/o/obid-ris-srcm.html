Canonical Definition

Shared Responsibility Coordination Module (SRCM) defines the
structural conditions under which multiple participants share,
coordinate, distinguish, and continuously govern responsibilities
associated with a governed identity throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.

SRCM governs shared responsibility coordination.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that shared responsibilities remain clearly allocated, coordinated,
attributable, and continuously governable without creating
responsibility ambiguity.

Responsibility may be shared.

Accountability must remain clear.

SRCM governs that coordination.

Module Operational Role

SRCM defines the shared responsibility coordination layer of RIS.

Its role is to preserve governance clarity whenever multiple
participants jointly hold responsibilities for the same
governed identity.

Module Operational Space

SRCM governs:

  • shared responsibility,
  • responsibility coordination,
  • multi-party accountability,
  • responsibility allocation,
  • governance coordination,
  • responsibility relationships,
  • collaborative governance,
  • responsibility integrity.


Module Function

The module applies wherever two or more legitimate participants share
governance responsibilities for the same governed identity.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Shared Responsibility Object Identify the governed
identity and the responsibilities shared between
multiple participants.

2. Define Coordination Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing responsibility allocation,
coordination, communication, and accountability boundaries.

3. Define Coordination Failure Logic

Identify conditions where shared responsibility becomes ambiguous,
conflicting, duplicated, incomplete, or governance- invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for coordination, clarification,
reassignment, conflict resolution, or governance escalation.

5. Preserve Responsibility Coordination Records

Preserve responsibility assignments, coordination decisions,
governance records, supporting evidence, and accountability history.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Identity Governance

Scenario

A strategic enterprise identity is jointly governed by the
cybersecurity, legal, and compliance departments.

Application

SRCM coordinates responsibilities and clearly distinguishes the
governance obligations of each department.

Result

Shared governance remains coordinated, attributable, and
continuously governable.

Use Case 2 — Multi-Organization Identity Governance

Scenario

Multiple organizations jointly govern a shared digital
identity ecosystem.

Application

SRCM defines and coordinates the responsibilities assigned to each
participating organization.

Result

Shared responsibility remains transparent, coordinated, and
governance-valid throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Shared responsibility must never become shared uncertainty. Shared
Responsibility Coordination preserves governance integrity by ensuring
that every participant understands, maintains, and continuously
governs their legitimate responsibilities throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.
