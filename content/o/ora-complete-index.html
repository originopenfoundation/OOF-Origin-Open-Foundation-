ORA™ Complete Standards & Modules Index

Operational Reality Architecture

Official Operational Reality Map for Autonomous Systems, AI
Systems, Organizations, Critical Infrastructure, and Complex
Operational Environments

This document serves as the official OOF® operational reality
governance map and structural index used for operational reality
identification, operational governance classification, operational
diagnostics, operational architecture navigation, and operational
problem placement across human, organizational, AI, autonomous,
industrial, and cyber-physical environments.

ORA™ consists of 12 Parent Standards and 60 Core Modules.

Additional standards and modules may be added when new governable
operational spaces are identified. However, the standards and modules
listed below form the core structural backbone of ORA™.

Architectural Position

ORA™ governs the complete operational lifecycle.

The architecture progresses:

Operational Reality → Operational Boundaries → Operational
Responsibility → Operational Risk → Operational Validity →
Operational Constraints → Operational Escalation → Operational
Authority → Operational Decision → Operational Dependencies →
Operational Evidence → Operational Recovery

Together these standards govern how operational reality remains
valid throughout operational execution, coordination, governance,
accountability, evidence preservation, and recovery.

Canonical Principle

ORA™ does not govern what a system intends to achieve. ORA™
governs how operational reality itself remains valid, observable,
coordinated, accountable, auditable, and recoverable before, during,
and after execution.
