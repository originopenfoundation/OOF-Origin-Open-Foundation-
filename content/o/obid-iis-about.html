About Identity Integrity Standard (IIS)

Governing Identity Integrity

Identity Integrity Standard (IIS) defines the governance conditions
under which governed identity remains unique, authentic, verifiable,
distinguishable, and continuously governable throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.

Origin determines where identity begins.

Identity determines what the governed entity is.

IIS governs this identity governance space.

What Is Identity Integrity?

Identity integrity is the governed condition in which an identity
remains uniquely identifiable, authentic, classifiable, verifiable,
and protected from ambiguity, duplication, substitution, or identity
corruption throughout its complete lifecycle.

It enables organizations to establish trustworthy identities, preserve
identity continuity, validate identity authenticity, and ensure that
every governed identity remains consistently recognizable across
systems, organizations, platforms, and future
operational environments.

Canonical Definition

Identity Integrity Standard (IIS) defines the structural conditions
under which a governed identity remains unique, authentic,
distinguishable, classifiable, verifiable, and continuously governable
throughout its complete lifecycle.

Why IIS Exists

Governed identity cannot exist without trustworthy identity integrity.

Without governed identity, ownership cannot be established, authority
cannot be assigned, responsibility cannot be attributed, and
governance cannot reliably operate.

IIS exists because Identity Integrity represents the second governable
space of OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture.

What IIS Governs

IIS governs the structural conditions under which identity remains:

  • unique,
  • authentic,
  • distinguishable,
  • verifiable,
  • continuous,
  • governable.


Rather than governing identity technologies or authentication
mechanisms, IIS governs the governance conditions required for
trustworthy identity integrity.

Architectural Position

Identity Integrity Standard serves as the second governance space of
OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture.

It establishes the governance foundation that transforms a verified
origin into a uniquely governable identity capable of supporting
ownership, authority, permissions, responsibility, provenance,
transfer, continuity, and auditability.

Without governed identity, the remaining governance architecture
cannot operate reliably.

Canonical Closing Statement

Origin establishes existence. Identity establishes uniqueness.
Identity Integrity provides the governance foundation that enables
every governed identity to remain authentic, distinguishable, and
continuously governable throughout its complete lifecycle.
