About Responsibility Integrity Standard (RIS)

Governing Responsibility Integrity

Responsibility Integrity Standard (RIS) defines the governance
conditions under which responsibility associated with a governed
identity remains legitimate, attributable, verifiable, continuous, and
continuously governable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Authority determines who may act.

Permissions determine what actions are permitted.

Responsibility determines who remains answerable for those actions.

RIS governs this identity governance space.

What Is Responsibility Integrity?

Responsibility integrity is the governed condition in which
responsibility associated with a governed identity remains clearly
assigned, continuously attributable, independently verifiable,
preserved across governance changes, and compatible with governance
throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

It enables organizations to assign responsibility, coordinate shared
responsibilities, preserve responsibility continuity, attribute
governance consequences, and ensure that every governed identity
remains connected to accountable participants.

Canonical Definition

Responsibility Integrity Standard (RIS) defines the structural
conditions under which responsibility associated with a governed
identity remains legitimate, attributable, verifiable, continuous, and
continuously governable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Why RIS Exists

Governance cannot exist without responsibility.

Every governance action produces accountable participants.

Without governed responsibility, accountability becomes fragmented,
governance obligations become unclear, and identity governance loses
trust and continuity.

RIS exists because Responsibility Integrity represents the sixth
governable space of OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity
Governance Architecture.

What RIS Governs

RIS governs the structural conditions under which
responsibility remains:

  • legitimate,
  • attributable,
  • verifiable,
  • continuous,
  • traceable,
  • governable.


Rather than governing legal liability or organizational reporting
structures, RIS governs the governance conditions required for
trustworthy responsibility integrity.

Architectural Position

Responsibility Integrity Standard serves as the sixth governance space
of OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture.

It establishes the governance foundation that connects every governed
identity with accountable participants before provenance is
established, transfers occur, continuity is preserved, and
auditability is achieved.

Without governed responsibility, trustworthy identity governance
cannot be sustained.

Canonical Closing Statement

Authority enables governance. Permissions enable action.
Responsibility preserves accountability. Responsibility Integrity
ensures that every governed identity remains continuously connected to
those responsible for its governance throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.
