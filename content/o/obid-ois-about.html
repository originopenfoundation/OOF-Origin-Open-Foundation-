About Origin Integrity Standard (OIS)

Governing Origin Integrity

Origin Integrity Standard (OIS) defines the governance conditions
under which the origin of a governed identity remains authentic,
verifiable, attributable, traceable, and continuously governable
throughout its complete lifecycle.

Every governed identity begins with an origin.

That origin establishes the governance foundation upon which identity,
ownership, authority, responsibility, and trust are built.

OIS governs this identity governance space.

What Is Origin Integrity?

Origin integrity is the governed condition under which the origin of
an identity can be established, verified, attributed, and continuously
preserved throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

It enables organizations to establish trustworthy identity
foundations, preserve attribution, validate origin authenticity,
maintain governance continuity, and ensure that every governed
identity remains connected to its legitimate origin.

Canonical Definition

Origin Integrity Standard (OIS) defines the structural conditions
under which the origin of a governed identity remains authentic,
verifiable, attributable, traceable, and continuously governable
throughout its complete lifecycle.

Why OIS Exists

Governed identity cannot begin with ownership, authority, permission,
or responsibility.

It must begin with a trustworthy origin.

OIS exists because origin represents the first governable space of
OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture.

What OIS Governs

OIS governs the structural conditions under which identity
origin remains:

  • authentic,
  • verifiable,
  • attributable,
  • traceable,
  • trustworthy,
  • governable.


Rather than governing identity technologies or identity management
platforms, OIS governs the governance conditions required for
establishing trustworthy identity origin.

Architectural Position

Origin Integrity Standard serves as the first governance space of
OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture.

It establishes the governance foundation upon which identity,
ownership, authority, permission, responsibility, provenance,
transfer, continuity, and auditability are built.

Without governed origin, trustworthy governed identity cannot exist.

Canonical Closing Statement

Trustworthy identity begins with trustworthy origin. Origin Integrity
establishes the governance foundation that enables every governed
identity to remain authentic, verifiable, attributable, and
continuously connected to its legitimate origin throughout its
complete lifecycle.
