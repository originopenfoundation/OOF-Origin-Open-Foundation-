Canonical Definition

Origin Chain Continuity Module (OCCM) defines the structural
conditions under which the continuity of origin remains preserved,
traceable, uninterrupted, and continuously governable throughout the
complete identity lifecycle.

OCCM governs origin chain continuity.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that the relationship between a governed identity and its legitimate
origin remains continuously preserved despite operational,
organizational, technological, or lifecycle changes.

Origin establishes identity.

Continuity preserves origin.

OCCM governs that continuity.

Module Operational Role

OCCM defines the origin continuity layer of OIS.

Its role is to preserve an uninterrupted governance chain connecting
every governed identity to its legitimate origin.

Module Operational Space

OCCM governs:

  • origin chain continuity,
  • continuity preservation,
  • origin relationship continuity,
  • lifecycle continuity,
  • continuity verification,
  • governance continuity,
  • origin chain integrity,
  • trustworthy continuity management.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must preserve continuous
linkage between a governed identity and its legitimate origin across
its complete lifecycle.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Origin Chain

Identify the complete governance chain connecting the governed
identity to its legitimate origin.

2. Define Continuity Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing uninterrupted preservation
of the origin chain.

3. Define Continuity Failure Logic

Identify conditions where origin continuity becomes broken,
incomplete, inconsistent, unverifiable, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for continuity restoration, investigation,
reconciliation, escalation, or governance review.

5. Preserve Continuity Records Preserve continuity records, governance
events, lifecycle history, restoration actions, and
supporting documentation.

Use Case 1 — Cross-Platform Digital Identity

Scenario

A governed identity is migrated across multiple digital platforms
during its operational lifecycle.

Application

OCCM preserves an uninterrupted governance chain linking the identity
to its original source.

Result

The identity maintains continuous origin integrity regardless of
platform changes.

Use Case 2 — Long-Term AI Identity

Scenario

An AI identity evolves through multiple software generations and
infrastructure upgrades.

Application

OCCM maintains continuous origin linkage throughout every
authorized transition.

Result

The AI identity remains continuously attributable to its original
governed origin.

Canonical Closing Statement

Identity may evolve, but its origin must never become disconnected.
Origin Chain Continuity preserves the unbroken governance relationship
that allows a governed identity to remain authentic across time,
change, and generations.
