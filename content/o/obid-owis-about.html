About Ownership Integrity Standard (OWIS)

Governing Ownership Integrity

Ownership Integrity Standard (OWIS) defines the governance conditions
under which ownership of a governed identity remains legitimate,
attributable, verifiable, transferable, and continuously governable
throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Origin establishes where identity begins.

Identity establishes what exists.

Ownership establishes to whom the governed identity
legitimately belongs.

OWIS governs this identity governance space.

What Is Ownership Integrity?

Ownership integrity is the governed condition in which ownership of a
governed identity remains legitimate, continuously attributable,
independently verifiable, protected from unauthorized claims, and
preserved throughout its complete lifecycle.

It enables organizations to establish lawful ownership, validate
ownership claims, resolve ownership conflicts, preserve ownership
continuity, and ensure that every governed identity remains under
legitimate governance.

Canonical Definition

Ownership Integrity Standard (OWIS) defines the structural conditions
under which ownership of a governed identity remains legitimate,
attributable, verifiable, transferable, and continuously governable
throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

Why OWIS Exists

A governed identity cannot participate in trusted governance unless
its legitimate owner is known.

Without governed ownership, authority cannot be legitimately
exercised, permissions cannot be reliably granted, responsibility
cannot be fairly assigned, and governance disputes become difficult
to resolve.

OWIS exists because Ownership Integrity represents the third
governable space of OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity
Governance Architecture.

What OWIS Governs

OWIS governs the structural conditions under which ownership remains:

  • legitimate,
  • attributable,
  • verifiable,
  • transferable,
  • continuous,
  • governable.


Rather than governing legal property systems or contractual ownership
mechanisms, OWIS governs the governance conditions required for
trustworthy ownership integrity.

Architectural Position

Ownership Integrity Standard serves as the third governance space of
OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture.

It establishes the governance foundation that connects a governed
identity with its legitimate owner before authority, permissions,
responsibility, provenance, transfer, continuity, and auditability can
be governed.

Without governed ownership, the remaining governance relationships
cannot be established with confidence.

Canonical Closing Statement

Origin creates identity. Identity enables ownership. Ownership
Integrity establishes the trusted governance relationship that
connects every governed identity to its legitimate owner throughout
its complete lifecycle.
