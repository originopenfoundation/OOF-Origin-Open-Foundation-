About Auditability Integrity Standard (AUDIS)

Governing Auditability Integrity

Auditability Integrity Standard (AUDIS) defines the governance
conditions under which every governance aspect of a governed identity
remains independently reviewable, verifiable, evidence-supported,
traceable, and continuously governable throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.

Every governed identity creates governance evidence.

Every governance decision should be independently verifiable.

AUDIS governs this identity governance space.

What Is Auditability Integrity?

Auditability integrity is the governed condition in which every
governance aspect of a governed identity remains transparent,
independently reviewable, evidence-supported, traceable, and
continuously verifiable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

It enables organizations to reconstruct governance history, verify
governance decisions, audit governance relationships, validate
supporting evidence, and ensure that every governed identity remains
independently trustworthy.

Canonical Definition

Auditability Integrity Standard (AUDIS) defines the structural
conditions under which every governance aspect of a governed identity
remains independently reviewable, verifiable, evidence-supported,
traceable, and continuously governable throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.

Why AUDIS Exists

Governance cannot rely solely on declarations.

Trustworthy governance requires independent verification supported
by evidence.

Without governed auditability, governance transparency declines,
accountability weakens, compliance becomes difficult to verify, and
long-term trust cannot be sustained.

AUDIS exists because Auditability Integrity represents the tenth
governable space of OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity
Governance Architecture.

What AUDIS Governs

AUDIS governs the structural conditions under which
auditability remains:

  • independently reviewable,
  • verifiable,
  • evidence-supported,
  • traceable,
  • transparent,
  • governable.


Rather than governing audit software, logging technologies, or
compliance procedures, AUDIS governs the governance conditions
required for trustworthy auditability integrity.

Architectural Position

Auditability Integrity Standard serves as the tenth governance space
of OBIDENITY® — Origin-Bound Identity Governance Architecture.

It establishes the governance foundation that enables the complete
lifecycle of a governed identity to be independently verified through
authentic evidence, transparent governance records, and
complete traceability.

Without governed auditability, trustworthy identity governance cannot
be independently demonstrated.

Canonical Closing Statement

Trust becomes durable when it can be independently verified.
Auditability Integrity preserves governance confidence by ensuring
that every governed identity remains transparent, evidence-supported,
and continuously verifiable throughout the complete
identity lifecycle.
