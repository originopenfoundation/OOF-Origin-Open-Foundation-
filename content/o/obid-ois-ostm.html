Canonical Definition

Origin Source Traceability Module (OSTM) defines the structural
conditions under which the source of a governed identity origin
remains identifiable, attributable, traceable, and continuously
governable throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

OSTM governs origin source traceability.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that every governed identity can be continuously traced back to its
legitimate origin source.

Every governed identity has a source.

Governance preserves continuous traceability to that source.

OSTM governs that traceability.

Module Operational Role

OSTM defines the origin source traceability layer of OIS.

Its role is to ensure that the legitimate source of every governed
identity remains continuously identifiable and traceable.

Module Operational Space

OSTM governs:

  • origin source traceability,
  • origin source attribution,
  • source identification,
  • source continuity,
  • source relationship preservation,
  • governed origin traceability,
  • origin source validation,
  • trustworthy source tracking.


Module Function

The module applies wherever organizations must preserve continuous
traceability between a governed identity and its legitimate
origin source.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Origin Source Object

Identify the legitimate source from which the governed
identity originates.

2. Define Traceability Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing continuous source
identification, attribution, and traceability.

3. Define Traceability Failure Logic

Identify conditions where origin source traceability becomes
incomplete, inconsistent, unverifiable, or governance-invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for verification, investigation, correction,
escalation, or restoration of source traceability.

5. Preserve Source Traceability

Preserve source records, attribution evidence, governance decisions,
traceability history, and supporting documentation.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Identity Registry

Scenario

An enterprise maintains a registry of governed digital identities
across multiple business systems.

Application

OSTM preserves continuous traceability between every identity and its
legitimate origin source.

Result

The organization can always determine where each governed
identity originated.

Use Case 2 — Autonomous Robotics Platform

Scenario

A robotics platform deploys autonomous robots with governed
operational identities.

Application

OSTM maintains traceability from every operational identity to its
authorized origin source.

Result

Every robot remains continuously connected to its legitimate origin
throughout its operational lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Trustworthy identity requires a traceable origin source. Origin Source
Traceability preserves the continuous governance relationship between
every governed identity and the source from which it
legitimately originated.
