Canonical Definition

Authority Escalation Module (AESM) defines the structural conditions
under which governance authority over a governed identity is escalated
to a higher legitimate authority while preserving governance
integrity, accountability, traceability, and continuous governability
throughout the complete identity lifecycle.

AESM governs authority escalation.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure
that authority is escalated only when predefined governance conditions
require higher-level authorization or intervention.

Not every authority may decide everything.

Governance defines when authority must escalate.

AESM governs that escalation.

Module Operational Role

AESM defines the authority escalation layer of AIS.

Its role is to ensure that governance decisions exceeding delegated
authority are transferred to the appropriate governance level.

Module Operational Space

AESM governs:

  • authority escalation,
  • governance escalation,
  • higher-level authorization,
  • escalation conditions,
  • escalation pathways,
  • authority transition,
  • governance intervention,
  • escalation traceability.


Module Function

The module applies wherever governance authority must be escalated
beyond the authority currently assigned to a governed identity.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Escalation Object

Identify the governance decisions or actions requiring
authority escalation.

2. Define Escalation Conditions

Establish governance conditions governing when, how, and to whom
authority must be escalated.

3. Define Escalation Failure Logic

Identify conditions where authority escalation becomes bypassed,
delayed, unauthorized, incomplete, or governance- invalid.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for escalation, review, approval,
intervention, reassignment, or rejection.

5. Preserve Escalation Records

Preserve escalation requests, governance decisions, supporting
evidence, approval history, and escalation records.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Identity Governance

Scenario

An identity administrator requests authority to transfer ownership of
a strategic digital identity.

Application

AESM escalates the request to the authorized governance authority
responsible for ownership decisions.

Result

The governance decision is made by the appropriate level of authority.

Use Case 2 — AI Identity Governance

Scenario

An AI identity management platform detects a governance action beyond
the authority assigned to a regional administrator.

Application

AESM escalates the governance request to the global identity
governance authority.

Result

Authority remains legitimate, accountable, and
continuously governable.

Canonical Closing Statement

Authority demonstrates its maturity by recognizing its limits.
Authority Escalation preserves governance integrity by ensuring that
decisions always reach the authority legitimately empowered to make
them throughout the complete identity lifecycle.
