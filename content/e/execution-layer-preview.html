EXECUTION LAYER — Enforcement Standard

Governance & Enforcement · Enforcement Architecture

Execution Layer™ defines the conditions under which actions in a system
are permitted, executed, and validated.

It ensures that no action can occur without permission, traceability,
and enforced constraints, and that all outcomes remain bound
to their execution process.

The standard establishes execution as a governed, verifiable process —
not just an outcome.
