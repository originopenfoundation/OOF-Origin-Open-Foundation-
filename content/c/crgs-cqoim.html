Canonical Definition

Capability Qualification Origin Integrity Module (CQOIM) defines the
structural conditions under which qualifications, certifications,
licenses, approvals, competencies, authorizations, training
credentials, and capability claims remain attributable to
identifiable, legitimate, traceable, governable, accountable, and
operationally valid qualification sources throughout the
capability lifecycle.

CQOIM governs qualification origin.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine where capability qualifications originated, who issued
them, under what authority they were issued, and whether the
qualification source remains legitimate.

Module Operational Role

CQOIM defines the qualification-origin governance layer of CRGS.

Its role is to preserve visibility of the sources from which
capability qualifications originate.

Module Operational Space

CQOIM governs:

  • qualification origin
  • certification origin
  • competency-source traceability
  • qualification attribution
  • credential provenance
  • qualification legitimacy
  • qualification accountability
  • capability-source governance


The module applies wherever governance requires verification
of capability origins.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • identifiable qualification sources
  • traceable certification origins
  • reconstructable qualification history
  • accountable credential issuance
  • governance-valid capability attribution
  • operationally valid qualification provenance


Its function is to ensure that governance can determine where
capability qualifications originated and whether they
remain trustworthy.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Qualification Origin Object

The organization must define which qualification environments
require origin governance.

This may include:

  • certification systems
  • licensing authorities
  • training organizations
  • educational institutions
  • contractor qualification systems
  • workforce validation programs
  • AI capability validation frameworks
  • Human-AI operational environments


2. Define Qualification Origin Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which qualification
origin remains valid.

This includes:

  • source-identification requirements
  • credential requirements
  • traceability requirements
  • accountability requirements
  • legitimacy requirements
  • governance-valid qualification conditions


3. Define Origin Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how qualification-origin failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • unverifiable certifications
  • unidentified qualification sources
  • fraudulent credentials
  • undocumented qualifications
  • governance-blind certifications
  • illegitimate qualification issuers


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
qualification-origin failures.

Governance response may include:

  • credential verification
  • qualification review
  • governance intervention
  • qualification reconstruction
  • corrective actions
  • capability reassessment
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • qualification sources
  • certification records
  • validation activities
  • governance reviews
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting capability states


A capability-readiness environment must not remain
qualification-valid if materially significant qualifications cannot
be attributed, reconstructed, reviewed, validated, or governed.

Use Case 1 — Certified Workforce Environment

Scenario

A company assigns a worker to perform specialized welding activities
requiring specific certifications and qualifications.

Application

CQOIM reconstructs the origin of qualifications and determines
whether certification sources remain legitimate.

Result

Governance gains visibility into the origin and legitimacy
of capability qualifications.

Use Case 2 — AI Capability Environment

Scenario

An orchestration platform relies upon capability certifications and
validation records before assigning operational responsibilities
to AI agents.

Application

CQOIM reconstructs the origin of capability claims and
validation credentials.

Result

Governance gains visibility into the source and legitimacy of
capability qualifications across intelligent operational systems.
