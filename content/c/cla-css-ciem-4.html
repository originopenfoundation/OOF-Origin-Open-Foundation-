Classification Inclusion & Exclusion

Canonical Definition

Classification Inclusion & Exclusion Module defines the governance
framework for determining, documenting, validating, and continuously
managing which entities, objects, processes, systems, events, or
information shall be included within or excluded from a governed
classification scope. It establishes inclusion criteria, exclusion
criteria, governance controls, decision rules, validation
mechanisms, and continuous management necessary to ensure that
classification remains objective, transparent, complete, and
methodologically consistent.

Operational Role

Classification Inclusion & Exclusion Module governs the inclusion
and exclusion layer of classification governance by ensuring that
every classification decision is based upon explicitly defined,
governance-approved, and methodologically valid inclusion and
exclusion rules.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Inclusion & Exclusion Requirements

Establish inclusion criteria, exclusion criteria, governance rules,
decision thresholds, responsible authorities, and acceptance
conditions.

2. Define Inclusion & Exclusion Methodology

Develop standardized procedures for identifying, documenting,
reviewing, validating, approving, and maintaining inclusion and
exclusion decisions throughout the complete classification
lifecycle.

3. Define Inclusion & Exclusion Validation Logic

Verify that inclusion and exclusion decisions remain objective,
governance-compliant, methodologically consistent, operationally
justified, and independently reproducible.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish procedures for disputed inclusion decisions, incorrect
exclusions, governance exceptions, reassessment activities,
corrective actions, rule revisions, and continuous improvement.

5. Preserve Inclusion & Exclusion Records

Maintain inclusion decisions, exclusion decisions, governance
approvals, validation reports, supporting evidence, revision
history, timestamps, and complete governance audit trails throughout
the classification lifecycle.

Use Case 1 — Enterprise Information Governance

Scenario

An organization determines which categories of information must be
included within its corporate classification framework and which
information falls outside its governance scope.

Application

Classification Inclusion & Exclusion Module governs the
establishment and validation of inclusion and exclusion rules before
operational classification begins.

Result

Classification remains complete, transparent, governance-valid, and
methodologically consistent across the organization.

Use Case 2 — National Critical Infrastructure Classification

Scenario

A regulatory authority determines which infrastructure operators are
subject to national classification requirements and which
organizations are excluded.

Application

Classification Inclusion & Exclusion Module governs the objective
evaluation of inclusion and exclusion criteria, governance
approvals, and supporting evidence before regulatory classifications
are assigned.

Result

Classification decisions remain transparent, independently
reproducible, governance-compliant, and consistently applied across
all regulated entities.

Canonical Closing Statement

Classification Inclusion & Exclusion Module establishes the
canonical governance framework for governing inclusion and exclusion
within classification. By governing inclusion criteria, exclusion
criteria, governance controls, validation mechanisms, decision
methodology, and continuous management, the module enables objective
classification, methodological consistency, governance transparency,
and trustworthy classification across all operational domains.
