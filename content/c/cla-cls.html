Governed Space

Classification Lifecycle

Core Question

How can classifications remain governed, current, controlled, and
traceable throughout their complete lifecycle?

Canonical Definition

Classification Lifecycle Standard defines the governance conditions
required to manage, control, monitor, update, and retire
classifications throughout their complete lifecycle. It establishes
lifecycle methodology, governance controls, change management
principles, version governance, monitoring mechanisms, and
retirement procedures necessary to ensure that classifications
remain accurate, current, traceable, operationally relevant, and
independently verifiable from creation through retirement.

Assurance

Standard Abstract

Creating and validating a classification does not guarantee that it
will remain accurate or operationally relevant over time.

Classifications evolve as systems, regulations, knowledge,
environments, and operational conditions change.

Classification Lifecycle Standard establishes the governance
framework required to manage the complete lifecycle of
classifications, ensuring they remain controlled, traceable,
current, and suitable for continued operational use.

Operational Role

Classification Lifecycle Standard governs the lifecycle layer of
classification governance by establishing standardized methods for
activating, maintaining, updating, versioning, monitoring, retiring,
and preserving classifications throughout their complete operational
lifecycle.

Operational Space

Classification Lifecycle Standard governs:

  • classification activation,
  • classification lifecycle,
  • classification change,
  • classification versioning,
  • classification retirement,
  • lifecycle monitoring,
  • lifecycle governance.


Why This Standard Exists

A validated classification may become outdated, obsolete, or
operationally incorrect as conditions change.

Without lifecycle governance, organizations risk relying on
classifications that no longer reflect current reality, introducing
operational inconsistency, governance failures, and decision-making
risks.

Classification Lifecycle Standard exists because Classification
Lifecycle represents the sixth governed space of CLA™ —
Classification Architecture, ensuring that classifications remain
governed throughout their complete lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Classification Lifecycle Standard establishes the canonical
governance framework for managing the complete lifecycle of
classifications. By governing lifecycle methodology, activation,
change management, version governance, monitoring, retirement, and
continuous lifecycle governance, the standard enables trustworthy
classifications, operational continuity, accountable governance, and
independent verification across all operational domains.
