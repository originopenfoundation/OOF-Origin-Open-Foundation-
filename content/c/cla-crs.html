Governed Space

Classification Registry

Core Question

How can classifications be centrally registered, uniquely
identified, governed, discovered, and managed throughout their
complete operational lifecycle?

Canonical Definition

Classification Registry Standard defines the governance conditions
required to register, identify, organize, maintain, discover, and
govern classifications within a centralized registry throughout
their complete lifecycle. It establishes registry methodology,
governance controls, registration principles, unique identification,
registry integrity, discovery mechanisms, and registry management
necessary to ensure that every classification remains uniquely
identifiable, traceable, accessible, governed, and independently
verifiable.

Interoperability → Classification Intelligence → Classification
Assurance

Standard Abstract

Creating, validating, and maintaining classifications is
insufficient unless they can be centrally managed and uniquely
identified.

Organizations require a governed registry that enables discovery,
traceability, version control, operational management, and
authoritative reference for every classification.

Classification Registry Standard establishes the governance
framework for managing classifications through a centralized
registry that preserves integrity, discoverability, continuity, and
long-term operational governance.

Operational Role

Classification Registry Standard governs the registry layer of
classification governance by establishing standardized methods for
registering, uniquely identifying, organizing, maintaining,
discovering, and managing classifications throughout their complete
lifecycle.

Operational Space

Classification Registry Standard governs:

  • classification registration,
  • classification registry,
  • classification identification,
  • registry organization,
  • classification discovery,
  • registry maintenance,
  • registry governance.


Why This Standard Exists

Without a governed registry, classifications become difficult to
locate, identify, manage, compare, reuse, and audit.

Organizations require a single authoritative source that preserves
classification integrity, operational continuity, traceability, and
governance consistency.

Classification Registry Standard exists because Classification
Registry represents the seventh governed space of CLA™ —
Classification Architecture, providing the authoritative governance
layer for classification registration and registry management.

Canonical Closing Statement

Classification Registry Standard establishes the canonical
governance framework for managing centralized classification
registries. By governing registration methodology, unique
identification, registry organization, discoverability, registry
