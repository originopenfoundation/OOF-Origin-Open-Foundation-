CLIA® — Cognitive Layer Integrity Standard

Subcategory: Cognitive Architecture & Decision Integrity


CLIA® defines the conditions under which AI cognition is considered valid.

A system is valid only if its reasoning, memory, and identity remain controlled and consistent during operation.

It does not evaluate output — it evaluates the structure of thinking behind it.

Without cognitive integrity, systems may appear functional while operating in unstable states.

CLIA® establishes a clear boundary between valid cognition and uncontrolled behavior.
