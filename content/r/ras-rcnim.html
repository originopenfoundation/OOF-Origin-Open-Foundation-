Canonical Definition

Relationship Continuity Integrity Module (RCNIM) defines the
structural conditions under which relationships remain continuously
traceable, reconstructable, governable, and operationally valid
throughout changes in time, structure, ownership, delegation,
supervision, organizational form, and operational activity.

RCNIM governs relationship continuity.

The module establishes the conditions required to determine whether
a relationship remained continuous, changed, transferred, evolved,
fragmented, or terminated throughout its lifecycle.

Module Operational Role

RCNIM serves as the continuity layer of the
Relationship Accountability Standard.

Its role is to preserve visibility of relationships
throughout operational change.

RCNIM answers the question:

Did the relationship remain continuous throughout the
operational lifecycle?

Module Operational Space

RCNIM governs:

  • relationship continuity
  • relationship evolution
  • relationship transition
  • relationship transfer
  • relationship preservation
  • continuity traceability
  • governance continuity


The module applies wherever relationships change over time.

Module Function

The module ensures that governance can determine:

  • when a relationship started
  • when a relationship changed
  • when a relationship transferred
  • when a relationship expanded
  • when a relationship contracted
  • when a relationship ended
  • whether continuity remained preserved


Its function is to prevent accountability gaps caused
by relationship fragmentation.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Relationship Lifecycle

Determine the lifecycle of the relationship being analyzed.

Examples:

  • employment lifecycle
  • contractor lifecycle
  • supplier lifecycle
  • supervisory lifecycle
  • Human-AI lifecycle
  • agent lifecycle


2. Identify Continuity Events

Identify events affecting relationship continuity.

Examples:

  • contract changes
  • organizational changes
  • delegation changes
  • ownership changes
  • supervisory changes
  • operational restructuring


3. Map Relationship Evolution

Determine how the relationship evolved throughout time.

Governance must identify:

  • preserved relationships
  • transferred relationships
  • expanded relationships
  • reduced relationships
  • terminated relationships


4. Validate Continuity Integrity

Determine whether continuity remained operationally valid.

Governance must identify:

  • continuity breaks
  • hidden transitions
  • undocumented transfers
  • relationship fragmentation
  • governance discontinuities


5. Preserve Continuity Traceability

Maintain sufficient evidence to reconstruct relationship continuity
throughout governance analysis.

Use Case 1 — Contractor Transition Chain

Scenario

A contractor relationship is repeatedly transferred between
organizations while operational work continues uninterrupted.

Application

RCNIM reconstructs continuity across multiple
contractual transitions.

Result

Governance preserves visibility of the relationship
despite structural changes.

Use Case 2 — Long-Term Human-AI Environment

Scenario

An AI system evolves through multiple operational versions while
continuing to interact with the same users and
governance structures.

Application

RCNIM tracks continuity of relationships across evolving
operational states.

Result

Governance maintains traceability despite system evolution.

Canonical Closing Statement

Relationship Continuity Integrity Module (RCNIM) defines the
structural conditions under which relationships remain continuously
traceable, reconstructable, governable, and operationally valid
throughout changes in time, structure, ownership, delegation,
supervision, organizational form, and operational activity.

Relationships may change, transfer, evolve, or terminate. Governance
integrity depends upon preserving visibility of those changes
throughout the relationship lifecycle.
