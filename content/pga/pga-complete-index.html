This document serves as the official OOF® Prediction Governance architecture map and structural index used for Prediction Governance design, Prediction Lifecycle navigation, predictive-system diagnostics, architecture development, Prediction Governance assessment, lifecycle governance, Predictive Performance analysis, historical reconstruction, and interoperability across human forecasting, AI systems, predictive models, prediction markets, autonomous agents, organizations, decision systems, collective intelligence environments, and future predictive systems.

PGA™ consists of 12 Parent Standards and 60 Core Modules.

Additional standards and modules may be added when new governable Prediction spaces are identified. However, the standards and modules listed below form the core structural backbone of PGA™.

1. Prediction Object & Purpose Standard (POPS)

Governed Space: Prediction Object & Predictive Purpose

Core Question: What exactly is being predicted, for what purpose, over what horizon, and within what predictive boundary?

Modules:

POIM — Prediction Object Identification Module PPDM — Prediction Purpose Definition Module PTSM — Prediction Target Specification Module PHDM — Prediction Horizon Definition Module PBEM — Prediction Boundary Establishment Module

2. Prediction Methodology Standard (PMES)

Governed Space: Prediction Methodology

Core Question: By what documented and reproducible methodology is the prediction legitimately constructed?

Modules:

PMSM — Prediction Methodology Specification Module MSDM — Methodological Structure Definition Module PLDM — Prediction Logic Documentation Module MAPM — Methodology Application Module MVGM — Methodology Version Governance Module

3. Prediction Basis Standard (PBAS)

Governed Space: Prediction Basis

Core Question: What information, data, observations, evidence references, and signals legitimately form the basis of the prediction?

Modules:

PIIM — Prediction Information Identification Module PSMM — Prediction Signal Mapping Module SRVM — Source Relevance & Validity Module ISUM — Information Sufficiency Module PBTRM — Prediction Basis Traceability Module

4. Prediction Assumption Standard (PAS)

Governed Space: Prediction Assumptions

Core Question: What assumptions must hold for the prediction and its stated probability, confidence, feasibility, or expected outcome to remain meaningful?

Modules:

PAIM — Prediction Assumption Identification Module ADMM — Assumption Dependency Mapping Module ASNM — Assumption Sensitivity Module AMAM — Assumption Materiality Assessment Module ACGM — Assumption Change Governance Module

5. Prediction Formation Standard (PFOS)

Governed Space: Prediction Formation

Core Question: How are methodology, Prediction Basis, assumptions, and applicable conditions transformed into a specific governed prediction?

Modules:

PCOM — Prediction Component Organization Module PCIM — Prediction Condition Integration Module POFM — Prediction Output Formation Module PSEM — Prediction State Establishment Module PFTM — Prediction Formation Traceability Module

6. Prediction Probability & Uncertainty Standard (PPUS)

Governed Space: Predictive Probability & Uncertainty

Core Question: How should probability, uncertainty, confidence, ranges, and conditionality be represented without overstating what the prediction actually establishes?

Modules:

PRPM — Prediction Probability Representation Module PUNM — Prediction Uncertainty Module CFRM — Confidence Representation Module PRGM — Prediction Range Governance Module CPM — Conditional Probability Module

7. Prediction Feasibility Standard (PFES)

Governed Space: Prediction Feasibility

Core Question: Is the predicted outcome realistically achievable under the stated assumptions, constraints, time horizon, Prediction Basis, and relevant conditions?

Modules:

FCIM — Feasibility Condition Identification Module PCMM — Prediction Constraint Mapping Module OPAM — Operational Plausibility Assessment Module EOFM — Execution & Outcome Feasibility Module FSDM — Feasibility State Determination Module

8. Collective Prediction Governance Standard (CPGS)

Governed Space: Collective Prediction

Core Question: Under what conditions may predictions originating from multiple participants, models, agents, markets, or forecasting mechanisms be combined and represented as a legitimate collective prediction?

Modules:

PSDM — Prediction Source Diversity Module PDVM — Prediction Diversity Validation Module PAGM — Prediction Aggregation Governance Module PICM — Prediction Independence & Concentration Module CPRM — Collective Prediction Representation Module

9. Prediction Communication & Reliance Standard (PCRS)

Governed Space: Prediction Communication & Reliance

Core Question: How may a prediction be communicated, interpreted, and relied upon without exceeding what its methodology, uncertainty, feasibility, intended purpose, and governed conditions legitimately support?

Modules:

PRSM — Prediction Representation & Signaling Module PLDIM — Prediction-Led Decision Interpretation Module PIBM — Prediction Interpretation Boundary Module RLCM — Reliance Legitimacy & Context Module PUBM — Prediction Use Boundary Module

10. Prediction Monitoring & Revision Standard (PMRS)

Governed Space: Prediction Monitoring & Revision

Core Question: When changing information or conditions materially affect a prediction, when must it be preserved, reviewed, revised, superseded, or withdrawn?

Modules:

PCHM — Prediction Change Monitoring Module RTM — Review Trigger Module PRVM — Prediction Revision Module PSSM — Prediction Supersession Module PWM — Prediction Withdrawal Module

11. Prediction Outcome & Performance Standard (POFPS)

Governed Space: Prediction Outcome & Predictive Performance

Core Question: Once a sufficiently governed outcome becomes available, how should the prediction be associated with that outcome and its predictive performance legitimately determined over time?

Modules:

POAM — Prediction Outcome Association Module POCM — Prediction Outcome Comparison Module PEMM — Prediction Error Measurement Module PCALM — Prediction Calibration Module PPCM — Predictive Performance Comparison Module

12. Prediction Record & Continuity Standard (PRCS)

Governed Space: Prediction Record & Historical Continuity

Core Question: How must a prediction and its complete history remain identifiable, reconstructable, versioned, and historically continuous across formation, revision, supersession, outcome, performance assessment, retirement, and lifecycle re-entry?

Modules:

PRFM — Prediction Record Formation Module PVCM — Prediction Version Continuity Module PHPM — Prediction History Preservation Module PRSTM — Prediction Record State Transition Module PRRM — Prediction Record Re-Entry Module

Architectural Position

PGA™ governs the complete Prediction Governance lifecycle from definition of the Prediction Object and predictive purpose through Predictive Performance and historical continuity.

The architecture progresses:

Prediction Object & Purpose → Prediction Methodology → Prediction Basis → Prediction Assumptions → Prediction Formation → Probability & Uncertainty → Prediction Feasibility → Collective Prediction where applicable → Communication & Reliance → Monitoring & Revision → Outcome & Performance → Prediction Record & Continuity

Together these standards govern what is being predicted and why, how the prediction is methodologically constructed, what information and assumptions support it, how a specific prediction is formed, how probability and uncertainty are represented, whether the predicted outcome is feasible, how multiple predictions may legitimately become a collective prediction, how predictions are communicated and relied upon, how they respond to changing conditions, how they are associated with governed outcomes and assessed for Predictive Performance, and how their complete history remains identifiable and reconstructable.

Collective Prediction is conditional rather than mandatory.

The single-predictor path may proceed:

Prediction Feasibility → Communication & Reliance

The collective path may proceed:

Prediction Feasibility → Collective Prediction → Communication & Reliance

Architecture Position ≠ Mandatory Processing Through Every Optional Governance Space.

Its lifecycle becomes recursive when material predictive conditions change:

Governed Prediction → Monitoring → Review → Revision / Supersession / Withdrawal → Preserved Historical Prediction State

Where a sufficiently governed outcome becomes available:

Historical Prediction → Outcome Association → Outcome Comparison → Error Measurement → Calibration where applicable → Predictive Performance Comparison

Where a historical Prediction Record later becomes relevant:

Historical / Inactive Prediction Record → Re-Entry Evaluation → Re-Entry Classification → Re-Entered Prediction Record State or New Prediction Formation

This recursive structure allows Prediction Governance to remain responsive to changes in Prediction Basis, assumptions, probability, uncertainty, feasibility, communication, reliance, operational conditions, outcomes, Predictive Performance, and historical lifecycle state.

Cross-Architecture Boundary

PGA™ ↔ ORA™ ORA™ governs what is actually happening in Operational Reality . PGA™ governs the prediction and its relationship to sufficiently governed outcomes without establishing Operational Reality itself .

PGA™ ↔ VALIDOS™ PGA™ governs the Prediction Lifecycle and its methodological conditions . VALIDOS™ governs whether the total validation basis sufficiently supports a Validation Determination and intended reliance .

PGA™ ↔ INTEGROS® PGA™ governs prediction-specific methodology, basis, assumptions, formation, uncertainty, feasibility, lifecycle, performance, and continuity . INTEGROS® governs integrity across objects, states, evidence, and processes .

PGA™ ↔ ADIT® PGA™ governs which information, observations, evidence references, and signals form the Prediction Basis . ADIT® governs evidential observation, preservation, verification, examination, and auditability .

PGA™ ↔ AGA™ PGA™ governs Prediction Governance methodology and governed predictive conditions . AGA™ governs authority, responsibility, accountability, attribution, and decision relationships surrounding prediction-related activity .

PGA™ ↔ AIG® PGA™ governs the prediction as a governed object . AIG® governs AI behavioral governance and behavioral integrity .

PGA™ ↔ ASGA™ PGA™ governs predictions produced or used within predictive environments . ASGA™ governs autonomous- system authority, operation, action, constraints, escalation, and governability .

PGA™ ↔ SIMULOS® PGA™ governs predictions . SIMULOS® governs simulation purpose, structure, execution, outputs, and lifecycle .

PGA™ ↔ Prediction Models / Forecasting Mechanisms PGA™ governs the Prediction Lifecycle . Prediction models, human forecasters, agents, markets, and forecasting mechanisms govern or perform prediction generation, calculation, estimation, inference, judgment, or signal production .

Canonical Principle

PGA™ does not generate predictions, govern the future itself, establish Operational Reality, determine evidence validity, perform general Validation Governance, allocate accountability, govern general AI behavior, control autonomous action, or treat model outputs, simulation outputs, market prices, collective signals, or probability values as Governed Predictions by default. PGA™ governs the methodological conditions under which a Prediction Object and predictive purpose are defined, Prediction Methodology is documented, Prediction Basis and assumptions are governed, a specific prediction is formed, probability and uncertainty are represented, feasibility is assessed, collective prediction is governed where applicable, communication and reliance remain bounded, changing conditions trigger monitoring and revision, predictions are associated with sufficiently governed outcomes, Predictive Performance is assessed, and the complete Prediction Record remains identifiable, versioned, historically continuous, reconstructable, and capable of governed lifecycle re-entry.

PGA™ governs the prediction — not the future.
