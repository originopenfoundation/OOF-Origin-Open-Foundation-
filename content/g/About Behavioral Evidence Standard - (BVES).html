About the Behavioral Evidence Standard (BVES)

Governing AI Behavioral
Evidence

Behavioral Evidence Standard (BVES) defines the governance conditions under
which AI behavior produces trustworthy evidence that can support
accountability, compliance, validation, investigation, regulatory oversight,
and future governance decisions.

What Is AI Behavioral Evidence?

AI Behavioral Evidence is the collection of governance-relevant information
created by AI behavior during operational execution.

It represents the factual basis upon which organizations can understand,
verify, justify, investigate, and govern AI behavior.

Behavioral evidence answers questions such as:

  • What behavior occurred?
  • When did it occur?
  • Why did it occur?
  • What evidence supports it?
  • Can the behavior be proven?
  • Can governance decisions rely upon it?


Behavior disappears once execution ends.

Evidence preserves the reality of that behavior.

BVES exists to govern that evidence.

Why BVES Exists

Organizations increasingly depend on AI to support operational, financial,
healthcare, industrial, governmental, and autonomous decisions.

Without trustworthy behavioral evidence:

  • accountability weakens,
  • investigations become incomplete,
  • regulatory compliance becomes difficult,
  • governance decisions rely on assumptions,
  • organizational learning is reduced,
  • operational trust declines.


BVES ensures that AI behavior continuously generates evidence suitable for
governance long after execution has finished.

What BVES Governs

BVES governs the structural conditions under which AI behavioral evidence
remains: authentic, complete, attributable, reliable, preserved,
governance-valid, independently verifiable, suitable for future governance
decisions.

Rather than governing AI intelligence or implementation, BVES governs the
evidence produced by AI behavior regardless of technology.

Behavioral Governance Flow

Behavioral Integrity → Behavioral Boundaries → Behavioral Escalation →
Behavioral Auditability → Behavioral Evidence → Behavioral Trust → Behavioral
Correction → Behavioral Continuity → Behavioral Interoperability → Behavioral
Safety

Each behavioral space builds upon the previous one.

Together they form the complete behavioral governance lifecycle defined by
AIG®.

What BVES Is

BVES is:

  • an AI behavioral evidence standard,
  • a governance evidence framework,
  • an evidence preservation standard,
  • a governance reference standard,
  • a behavioral evidence methodology,
  • the evidence layer of AI Governance Architecture.


What BVES Is Not

BVES is not:

  • a logging platform,
  • a database,
  • a monitoring system,
  • a software product,
  • a programming framework,
  • an implementation methodology.


BVES governs evidence as a governance space rather than as a technical
function.

Problems BVES Helps Solve

BVES helps organizations answer questions such as:

  • Can AI behavior be proven?
  • Is governance evidence complete?
  • Can regulators verify behavioral evidence?
  • Can investigations rely on available evidence?
  • Is behavioral evidence trustworthy?
  • Can future decisions rely on past behavioral evidence?
  • Is accountability supported by evidence rather than assumptions?
