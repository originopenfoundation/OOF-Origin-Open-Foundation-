Governance Architecture

Official Governance Architecture Map for Organizations, Governments, Institutions,
AI Systems, Autonomous Systems, Human-AI Environments, and Multi-Organization
Governance Ecosystems

This document serves as the official OOF® governance architecture map
and structural index used for governance design, governance navigation,
governance classification, governance diagnostics, governance architecture
development, and governance interoperability across organizational,
institutional, governmental, AI, autonomous, and future governance environments.

GOA™ consists of 10 Parent Standards and 50 Core Modules.

Additional standards and modules may be added when new governable
governance spaces are identified. However, the standards and modules
listed below form the core structural backbone of GOA™.

Architectural Position

GOA™ governs the complete governance lifecycle.

The architecture progresses:
Governance Authority → Governance Boundaries → Governance Scope →
Governance Delegation → Governance Accountability → Governance Validation →
Governance Integrity → Governance Transparency → Governance Continuity →
Governance Interoperability

Together these standards govern how governance is established, bounded,
delegated, validated, protected, understood, preserved, and coordinated
across organizations, institutions, governments, AI systems,
autonomous systems, and future governance ecosystems.

Canonical Principle

GOA™ does not govern a specific operational domain. GOA™
governs how governance itself remains legitimate, accountable,
validated, protected, transparent, continuous, interoperable, and
trustworthy throughout its complete lifecycle.
