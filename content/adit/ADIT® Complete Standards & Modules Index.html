Official Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture Map for AI
Systems, Autonomous Systems, Digital Assets, Organizations, Critical

This document serves as the official OOF® audit and evidence governance
architecture map and structural index used for audit governance design, audit
governance navigation, audit governance classification, evidence governance,
audit diagnostics, audit architecture development, audit governance
validation, and audit interoperability across AI systems, autonomous systems,
organizations, digital assets, critical infrastructure, and future operational
environments.

ADIT® consists of 10 Parent Standards and 50 Core Modules.

Additional standards and modules may be added when new governable audit and
evidence spaces are identified. However, the standards and modules listed
below form the core structural backbone of ADIT®.

Architectural Position

ADIT® governs the complete audit and evidence governance lifecycle.

The architecture progresses:

Audit Observation → Evidence Formation → Evidence Integrity → Evidence
Correlation → Evidence Verification → Audit Execution → Audit Findings → Audit
Response → Audit Preservation → Audit Assurance

Together these standards govern how operational reality is continuously
observed, transformed into governed evidence, verified, audited, translated
into objective findings, acted upon through governed responses, preserved for
future reconstruction, and independently assured across AI systems, autonomous
systems, organizations, digital assets, critical infrastructure, and future
operational environments.

Canonical Principle

ADIT® does not govern operational execution, compliance, certification, or
regulatory authority. ADIT® governs the audit and evidence conditions under
which operational reality remains continuously observable, objectively
evidenced, independently auditable, reconstructable, preserved, and
continuously assured throughout its complete operational lifecycle.
