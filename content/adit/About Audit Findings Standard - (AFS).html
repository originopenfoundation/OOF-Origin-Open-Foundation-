About Audit Findings Standard - (AFS)

Understanding Audit Findings Standard

What Is Audit Findings Standard?

Audit Findings Standard is the seventh Parent Standard of ADIT® — Continuous
Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture™. It governs how audit findings are
objectively identified, documented, classified, validated, and governed using
verified evidence generated through governed audit execution.

Audit Findings Standard recognizes that completing an audit does not
automatically produce reliable findings. Audit conclusions must be derived
through a standardized methodology that objectively evaluates verified
evidence, documents supporting rationale, and classifies findings according to
established governance requirements.

Canonical Definition

Audit Findings Standard defines the governance conditions required to
establish, validate, document, classify, and preserve audit findings produced
through governed audit execution. It establishes finding principles, finding
methodology, classification requirements, governance controls, validation
mechanisms, and continuous findings assurance necessary to ensure that audit
findings remain objective, evidence-based, transparent, repeatable, and
independently verifiable throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed
entity.

Why Audit Findings Standard Exists

Audit findings influence governance decisions, corrective actions, regulatory
compliance, and organizational accountability.

Without a standardized methodology, findings may become inconsistent,
subjective, or insufficiently supported by evidence. Organizations therefore
require governed rules for producing findings that are objective, repeatable,
transparent, and independently verifiable.

Audit Findings Standard exists to ensure that every audit finding is supported
by verified evidence and generated through a governed audit methodology.

What
Audit Findings Standard Governs

Audit Findings Standard governs:

  • Audit findings
  • Finding classification
  • Finding validation
  • Finding documentation
  • Finding prioritization
  • Finding governance
  • Findings assurance

Canonical Closing Statement

Audit Findings Standard establishes the canonical governance framework for
producing objective, evidence-based audit findings. By governing finding
identification, classification, validation, documentation, and continuous
findings assurance, Audit Findings Standard enables reliable audit
conclusions, operational reconstruction, accountable governance, and
independent verification across all operational domains.
