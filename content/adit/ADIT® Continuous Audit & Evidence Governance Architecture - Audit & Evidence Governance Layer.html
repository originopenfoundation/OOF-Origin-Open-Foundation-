ADIT®
Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture

Audit & Evidence Governance Layer

Official Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture for AI Systems,
Autonomous Systems, Digital Assets, Organizations, Critical Infrastructure,
and Future Operational Environments

ADIT® serves as the official OOF® audit and evidence governance architecture
used for continuous audit governance, evidence governance, audit execution,
audit findings, audit preservation, audit assurance, and audit governance
navigation across AI systems, autonomous systems, organizations, digital
assets, critical infrastructure, and future operational environments.

Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture (ADIT®) does not define
auditing itself. ADIT® defines the governance architecture required to ensure
that audit and evidence remain continuously observable, evidentially
supported, objectively verifiable, reconstructable, independently auditable,
and continuously assured throughout the complete audit lifecycle.

ADIT® consists of 10 Core Parent Standards forming the primary audit and
evidence governance backbone within the OOF® ecosystem.

Additional compatible standards, bridge standards, and specialized audit and
evidence governance standards may extend the architecture when new audit
governance spaces are identified. However, the standards listed below form the
official core governance structure of ADIT®.

Canonical Definition

Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture (ADIT®) is the audit and
evidence governance architecture that defines the structural conditions under
which operational reality remains continuously observable, evidenced,
correlated, verified, auditable, reconstructable, preserved, and independently
assured throughout the complete operational lifecycle.

Core Question

How can operational reality remain continuously evidenced, auditable,
reconstructable, independently verifiable, and continuously assured throughout
the complete operational lifecycle?

Governance Flow

Audit Observation → Evidence Formation → Evidence Integrity → Evidence
Correlation → Evidence Verification → Audit Execution → Audit Findings → Audit
Response → Audit Preservation → Audit Assurance

Core Parent Standards

1. Audit Observation Standard

Governed Space: Audit Observation

Core Question: What operational reality must be continuously observed for
trustworthy audit?

2. Evidence Formation Standard

Governed Space: Evidence Formation

Core Question: How does operational reality become governed audit evidence?

3. Evidence Integrity Standard

Governed Space: Evidence Integrity

Core Question: How can audit evidence preserve its integrity throughout the
complete audit lifecycle?

4. Evidence Correlation Standard

Governed Space: Evidence Correlation

Core Question: How are individual evidence objects connected into a coherent
audit reality?

5. Evidence Verification Standard

Governed Space: Evidence Verification

Core Question: How can audit evidence be objectively and independently
verified before audit use?

6. Audit Execution Standard

Governed Space: Audit Execution

Core Question: How should governed audits be performed in a consistent,
objective, and independently verifiable manner?

7. Audit Findings Standard

Governed Space: Audit Findings

Core Question: How can objective audit findings be established from verified
evidence?

8. Audit Response Standard

Governed Space: Audit Response

Core Question: How should validated audit findings be transformed into
governed organizational responses?

9. Audit Preservation Standard

Governed Space: Audit Preservation

Core Question: How can audit history remain preserved, accessible,
trustworthy, and reconstructable throughout its complete lifecycle?

10. Audit Assurance Standard

Governed Space: Audit Assurance

Core Question: How can the complete audit lifecycle remain continuously
trustworthy, objective, independent, complete, and independently verifiable?

Compatible OOF® Architectures

ADIT® interoperates with multiple OOF® architectures governing complementary
governance spaces.

GOA™ — Governance Architecture provides the constitutional governance layer
defining how governance itself is structured.

ORA™ — Operational Reality Architecture governs operational execution and
operational reality that become the subject of audit.

OBIDENITY® — Identity Governance Architecture governs identity, ownership,
authority, provenance, and continuity of audit participants and evidence
sources.

INTEGROS® — Integrity Governance Architecture governs the integrity conditions
of governed entities, while ADIT® governs the evidence and auditability of
those conditions.

AGA™ — Accountability Governance Architecture governs accountability
relationships arising from audit findings and organizational responses.

CLIA® — Cognitive Governance Intelligence Architecture governs cognition,
reasoning, and cognitive evidence relevant to governed audit activities.

MGIA™ — Memory Governance Intelligence Architecture governs memory continuity
and organizational memory supporting long-term audit reconstruction.

AIG® — AI Governance Architecture governs AI behavioral governance while ADIT®
governs the continuous auditability of AI behavior.

ASGA™ — Autonomous Systems Governance Architecture governs autonomous
operational environments while ADIT® governs their continuous audit and
evidence lifecycle.

RIS™ — Runtime Integrity Architecture governs runtime execution integrity
while ADIT® governs the evidence, auditability, preservation, and assurance of
runtime operations.

ADIT® serves as the common audit and evidence governance architecture
supporting the broader OOF® ecosystem by ensuring that governed operational
reality remains continuously evidenced, auditable, reconstructable,
independently verifiable, and continuously assured throughout its complete
lifecycle.

Architectural Position

ADIT® governs the complete audit and evidence governance lifecycle.

The architecture progresses through:

Audit Observation → Evidence Formation → Evidence Integrity → Evidence
Correlation → Evidence Verification → Audit Execution → Audit Findings → Audit
Response → Audit Preservation → Audit Assurance

Together these standards govern how operational reality is continuously
observed, transformed into governed evidence, verified, audited, translated
into objective findings, acted upon through governed responses, preserved for
future reconstruction, and independently assured across AI systems, autonomous
systems, organizations, digital assets, critical infrastructure, and future
operational environments.

Canonical Principle

ADIT® does not govern operational execution, compliance, certification, or
regulatory authority. ADIT® governs the audit and evidence conditions under
which operational reality remains continuously observable, objectively
evidenced, independently auditable, reconstructable, preserved, and
continuously assured throughout its complete operational lifecycle.
