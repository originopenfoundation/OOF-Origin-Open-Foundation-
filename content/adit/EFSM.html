Governed Space

Evidence Structuring

Canonical Definition

Evidence Structuring Module (EFSM™) defines the governance framework for
organizing, structuring, standardizing, and preparing qualified evidence into
a consistent and governable format. It establishes evidence structure
requirements, organizational principles, structural models, governance
controls, and formatting mechanisms necessary to ensure that evidence remains
understandable, interoperable, reconstructable, and suitable for audit
throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

EFSM governs the structural layer of evidence formation by transforming
qualified evidence into a standardized and consistently organized evidence
structure before integrity governance begins.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Evidence Structure Requirements

Identify the structural elements, metadata, identifiers, relationships,
organizational requirements, and governance objectives required for
standardized evidence.

2. Define Structuring Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for organizing, formatting, classifying,
labeling, and maintaining evidence structures across governed operational
environments.

3. Define Structuring Logic

Define how qualified evidence is organized into consistent structures that
support interoperability, traceability, reconstruction, auditability, and
long-term governance.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for incomplete structures, inconsistent
formatting, missing structural elements, organizational conflicts, structural
non-conformity, and corrective actions where required.

5. Preserve Structure Records

Maintain evidence structures, metadata, structural revisions, governance
approvals, organizational relationships, timestamps, and supporting
documentation throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An autonomous AI ecosystem continuously generates qualified evidence from
operational observations across multiple intelligent agents.

Application

EFSM governs how evidence is organized into standardized structures containing
consistent metadata, identifiers, operational relationships, and governance
information before entering the integrity lifecycle.

Result

Evidence remains consistently structured, interoperable, reconstructable, and
ready for subsequent integrity governance.

Use Case 2 — Critical Infrastructure

Scenario

A national transportation network generates evidence from multiple operational
systems during continuous service delivery.

Application

EFSM standardizes the structure of operational evidence across infrastructure
components, ensuring consistency, interoperability, and long-term
auditability.

Result

Evidence maintains a uniform structure suitable for governance oversight,
operational reconstruction, and independent verification.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Structuring Module establishes the canonical governance framework for
organizing and standardizing evidence before integrity governance begins. By
governing evidence structures, organizational consistency, metadata,
structural relationships, and formatting requirements, EFSM ensures that
evidence remains understandable, interoperable, reconstructable, and
continuously governable throughout the complete lifecycle of every governed
entity.
