Evidence Composition

Canonical Definition

Evidence Composition Module (EFCM™) defines the governance framework for
composing, organizing, and integrating qualified evidence into coherent and
governable evidence objects. It establishes composition principles, evidence
assembly requirements, relationship models, governance controls, structural
consistency rules, and composition validation mechanisms necessary to ensure
that evidence is complete, coherent, and operationally meaningful throughout
the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

EFCM governs the composition layer of evidence formation by combining
qualified evidence elements into complete and governable evidence objects
capable of supporting audit, operational reconstruction, accountability, and
independent verification.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Evidence Composition Requirements

Identify the evidence elements, relationships, supporting information,
metadata, dependencies, and governance requirements necessary to compose
complete evidence objects.

2. Define Composition Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for assembling, organizing, linking, and
maintaining evidence components into consistent and governable evidence
structures.

3. Define Composition Logic

Define how evidence components are evaluated for completeness, consistency,
relationship integrity, operational relevance, and governance conformity
before becoming formal evidence objects.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for incomplete evidence composition, missing
components, inconsistent relationships, conflicting evidence elements,
composition failures, and corrective actions where required.

5. Preserve Composition Records

Maintain evidence composition structures, component relationships, governance
approvals, composition revisions, timestamps, and supporting documentation
throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An autonomous AI ecosystem generates evidence from multiple observations,
decisions, interactions, and operational events across several intelligent
agents.

Application

EFCM governs how individual evidence elements are assembled into complete
evidence objects representing the full operational reality of each governed
activity.

Result

Evidence remains complete, coherent, and suitable for continuous auditability,
operational reconstruction, and independent verification.

Use Case 2 — Industrial Operations

Scenario

A manufacturing facility generates evidence from production systems, quality
inspections, sensor networks, and operator interventions.

Application

EFCM governs the composition of these individual evidence elements into
complete operational evidence supporting quality assurance, compliance, and
incident investigation.

Result

Operational evidence accurately represents the complete activity and supports
transparent governance, reliable audit, and objective decision-making.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Composition Module establishes the canonical governance framework for
assembling qualified evidence into complete and governable evidence objects.
By governing evidence composition, structural consistency, relationship
integrity, and operational completeness, EFCM enables trustworthy evidence,
continuous auditability, operational reconstruction, accountable governance,
and independent verification throughout the complete lifecycle of every
governed entity.
