Governed Space

Audit Preservation

Core Question

How can audit evidence, execution history, findings, responses, and governance
records remain preserved, accessible, trustworthy, and reconstructable
throughout their complete lifecycle?

Canonical Definition

Audit Preservation Standard defines the governance conditions required to
preserve audit evidence, execution records, findings, responses, decisions,
approvals, and supporting governance information throughout the complete audit
lifecycle. It establishes preservation methodology, lifecycle controls,
retention requirements, governance mechanisms, preservation validation, and
continuous preservation assurance necessary to ensure that audit history
remains complete, accessible, trustworthy, traceable, and independently
reconstructable over time.

Governance Flow Position

Audit Observation → Evidence Formation → Evidence Integrity → Evidence
Correlation → Evidence Verification → Audit Execution → Audit Findings → Audit
Response → Audit Preservation → Audit Assurance

Standard Abstract

An audit cannot remain trustworthy if its history cannot be preserved.

Evidence, decisions, findings, responses, approvals, and execution records
must remain available long after operational activities have ended.

Audit Preservation Standard establishes the canonical methodology for
preserving the complete audit history so that operational reality remains
reconstructable and independently verifiable throughout its required
lifecycle.

Operational Role

Audit Preservation Standard governs the preservation layer of audit governance
by establishing standardized methods for retaining, protecting, organizing,
validating, retrieving, and preserving complete audit records and supporting
evidence.

Operational Space

Audit Preservation Standard governs:

  • audit preservation
  • audit record retention
  • audit archives
  • audit continuity
  • audit history
  • audit retrieval
  • preservation assurance.


Why This Standard Exists

Audit value extends beyond the moment an audit is completed.

Organizations require a governed methodology to preserve audit records for
future governance decisions, regulatory review, investigations, historical
reconstruction, organizational learning, and independent assurance.

Audit Preservation Standard exists because Audit Preservation represents the
ninth governed space of ADIT® — Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance
Architecture™, ensuring that complete audit history remains preserved and
trustworthy throughout its lifecycle.
