Governed Space

Audit Findings

Core Question

How can audit conclusions be objectively established, documented, classified,
and governed based exclusively on verified evidence and governed audit
execution?

Canonical Definition

Audit Findings Standard defines the governance conditions required to
establish, validate, document, classify, and preserve audit findings produced
through governed audit execution. It establishes finding principles, finding
methodology, classification requirements, governance controls, validation
mechanisms, and continuous findings assurance necessary to ensure that audit
findings remain objective, evidence-based, transparent, repeatable, and
independently verifiable throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed
entity.

Governance Flow Position

Audit Observation → Evidence Formation → Evidence Integrity → Evidence
Correlation → Evidence Verification → Audit Execution → Audit Findings → Audit
Response → Audit Preservation → Audit Assurance

Standard Abstract

A completed audit does not automatically produce governed findings.

Audit findings must be objectively derived from verified evidence using
standardized methodology, documented reasoning, and governed evaluation
criteria.

Audit Findings Standard establishes the canonical methodology for transforming
governed audit execution into objective, evidence-supported audit conclusions
before organizational responses begin.

Operational Role

Audit Findings Standard governs the findings layer of audit governance by
establishing standardized methods for identifying, documenting, classifying,
validating, and preserving audit findings generated through governed audit
execution.

Operational Space

Audit Findings Standard governs:

  • audit findings
  • finding classification
  • finding validation
  • finding documentation
  • finding prioritization
  • finding governance
  • findings assurance.


Why This Standard Exists

Audit execution must produce findings that are objective, reproducible, and
supported by verified evidence.

Without standardized governance, audit findings may become subjective,
inconsistent, or insufficiently supported.

Audit Findings Standard exists because Audit Findings represents the seventh
governed space of ADIT® — Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance
Architecture™, providing the formal methodology for producing governed audit
conclusions before audit responses are initiated.
