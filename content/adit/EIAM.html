Evidence Authenticity

Canonical Definition

Evidence Authenticity Module defines the governance framework for
establishing, validating, preserving, and continuously demonstrating that
governed evidence is genuine, originates from its declared operational source,
and has not been falsified, substituted, fabricated, or fraudulently
represented. It establishes authenticity principles, origin validation
requirements, authenticity controls, governance mechanisms, and continuous
validation conditions necessary to preserve trustworthy evidence throughout
the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

Evidence Authenticity Module governs the authenticity layer of evidence
integrity by ensuring that every governed evidence object can be objectively
demonstrated to be genuine and originate from its declared source.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Evidence Authenticity Requirements

Identify authenticity requirements, origin criteria, source validation rules,
governance objectives, responsible authorities, and operational conditions
required to establish authentic evidence.

2. Define Authenticity Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for validating evidence origin, confirming
authenticity, documenting provenance, preserving authenticity indicators, and
maintaining authenticity throughout the evidence lifecycle.

3. Define Authenticity Logic

Define how evidence authenticity is evaluated based on origin, provenance,
source identity, operational consistency, governance conformity, and
authenticity validation requirements.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for suspected falsification, disputed origin,
authenticity failures, substituted evidence, fabricated evidence, authenticity
conflicts, and corrective actions where required.

5. Preserve Authenticity Records

Maintain authenticity validations, origin records, provenance information,
governance approvals, validation history, timestamps, and supporting
documentation throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An autonomous AI platform generates governed evidence documenting high-value
operational decisions across distributed intelligent agents.

Application

Evidence Authenticity Module governs the validation of evidence origin,
confirms that every evidence object originates from its declared operational
source, and continuously preserves authenticity throughout the evidence
lifecycle.

Result

Governed evidence remains demonstrably genuine, objectively trustworthy, and
suitable for audit, accountability, and independent verification.

Use Case 2 — Digital Forensics

Scenario

Digital evidence collected following a cybersecurity incident is presented
during forensic investigation and legal review.

Application

Evidence Authenticity Module governs the verification of evidence origin and
authenticity before the evidence is accepted for forensic analysis and audit.

Result

Every evidence object can be objectively demonstrated as authentic,
strengthening confidence in forensic conclusions and governance decisions.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Authenticity Module establishes the canonical governance framework
for preserving the authenticity of governed evidence throughout its complete
lifecycle. By governing evidence origin, authenticity validation, provenance,
and continuous authenticity preservation, the module ensures that governed
evidence remains genuine, trustworthy, objectively demonstrable, and suitable
for audit, operational reconstruction, accountable governance, and independent
verification across all operational domains.
