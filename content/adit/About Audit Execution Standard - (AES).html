About Audit Execution Standard - (AES)

Understanding Audit Execution Standard

What Is Audit Execution Standard?

Audit Execution Standard is the sixth Parent Standard of ADIT® — Continuous
Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture™. It governs how audits are
objectively, consistently, transparently, and repeatably executed using
objectively verified evidence.

Audit Execution Standard recognizes that verified evidence alone does not
produce reliable audit outcomes. A standardized audit methodology is required
to evaluate evidence consistently, apply governance requirements objectively,
document audit activities, and ensure that audit conclusions are repeatable
and independently verifiable.

Canonical Definition

Audit Execution Standard defines the governance conditions required to execute
audits using objectively verified evidence. It establishes audit execution
principles, execution methodology, procedural controls, governance mechanisms,
execution validation, and continuous execution assurance necessary to ensure
that audits are performed consistently, transparently, objectively, and in
accordance with established governance requirements throughout the complete
lifecycle of a governed entity.

Why Audit Execution Standard Exists

Even perfectly verified evidence can produce inconsistent audit results if
different auditors apply different methods.

Organizations require a standardized methodology that governs how audits are
planned, performed, documented, reviewed, and completed. This ensures that
audit outcomes are based on governed procedures rather than individual
judgment.

Audit Execution Standard exists to establish a consistent, transparent, and
repeatable methodology for executing audits across all operational domains.

What Audit Execution Standard Governs

Audit Execution Standard governs:

  • Audit execution
  • Audit procedures
  • Audit methodology
  • Audit activities
  • Execution consistency
  • Execution governance
  • Execution assurance

Scenario

An organization performs a governance audit of an autonomous AI platform using
verified operational evidence collected throughout the AI lifecycle.

Application

Audit Execution Standard governs how auditors execute the audit using
standardized procedures, documented methodology, objective evaluation
criteria, and governed execution controls.

Result

The audit is performed consistently, transparently, and repeatably, producing
reliable findings supported by verified evidence.
