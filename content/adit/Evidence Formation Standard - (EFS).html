Governed Space

Evidence Formation

Core Question

How can governed audit observations be transformed into trustworthy evidence
suitable for continuous auditability, reconstruction, and independent
verification?

Canonical Definition

Evidence Formation Standard (EFS™) defines the governance conditions under
which validated audit observations become structured, attributable, authentic,
complete, and governable evidence. It establishes evidence formation
principles, transformation requirements, evidence qualification criteria,
formation mechanisms, governance controls, and acceptance conditions necessary
to ensure that evidence is consistently created from governed audit
observations throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Governance Flow Position

Audit Observation → Evidence Formation → Evidence Integrity → Evidence
Correlation → Evidence Verification → Audit Execution → Audit Findings → Audit
Response → Audit Preservation → Audit Assurance

Standard Abstract

Evidence does not exist simply because operational information has been
captured.

Before information can support audit conclusions, accountability, governance
decisions, or independent verification, it must first become governed evidence
through a standardized evidence formation process.

Building upon the governed audit observations established by AOS™, Evidence
Formation Standard defines how qualified observations become trustworthy
evidence capable of supporting the complete audit lifecycle.

EFS transforms governed observations into governed evidence.

Operational Role

Evidence Formation Standard governs the transformation layer between audit
observation and evidence. It establishes the governance framework required to
create, structure, qualify, document, and approve evidence before it enters
the governed evidence lifecycle.

Operational Space

EFS governs:

  • evidence formation
  • evidence creation
  • evidence qualification
  • evidence structuring
  • evidence composition
  • evidence acceptance
  • evidence registration.


This governed space ensures that evidence is formed consistently before
integrity, correlation, verification, and audit activities begin.

Why This Standard Exists

Audit observations provide visibility into operational reality.

However, observations alone do not automatically constitute trustworthy
evidence.

Organizations require a standardized governance framework that determines how
governed observations become formal evidence suitable for audit,
accountability, operational reconstruction, governance oversight, and
independent verification.

EFS exists because Evidence Formation represents the second governed space of
ADIT® — Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture™, providing the
canonical governance framework for transforming governed audit observations
into trustworthy evidence.

Foundational Principle

Evidence is not collected—it is governably formed from qualified audit

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Formation Standard establishes the canonical governance framework for
transforming governed audit observations into trustworthy evidence throughout
the complete lifecycle of every governed entity. By defining how observations
become structured, attributable, authentic, and governable evidence, EFS
enables continuous auditability, operational reconstruction, accountable
governance, and independent verification across all operational domains.
