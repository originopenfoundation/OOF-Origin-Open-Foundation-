About Evidence Verification Standard - (EVS)

Understanding Evidence Verification Standard

What Is Evidence Verification Standard?

Evidence Verification Standard is the fifth Parent Standard of ADIT® —
Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture™. It governs how
correlated evidence is objectively verified before it is accepted for audit
execution, governance decisions, regulatory review, legal proceedings, or
independent verification.

Evidence Verification Standard recognizes that authentic, complete, preserved,
and properly correlated evidence is not automatically verified. Before
evidence can support audit conclusions or governance decisions, it must be
objectively evaluated against defined verification criteria.

Canonical Definition

Evidence Verification Standard defines the governance conditions required to
verify the accuracy, validity, reliability, authenticity, completeness,
consistency, and sufficiency of correlated evidence before it is used for
audit execution. It establishes verification principles, validation
requirements, verification controls, governance mechanisms, and continuous
verification assurance necessary to determine whether governed evidence
satisfies the required level of trust for audit, investigation, operational
reconstruction, and independent verification.

Why Evidence Verification
Standard Exists

Reliable audit depends on verified evidence rather than assumed evidence.

Organizations need a standardized methodology to determine whether evidence
satisfies objective governance requirements before it is used to support
findings, decisions, accountability, or regulatory actions.

Evidence Verification Standard exists to ensure that only evidence meeting
defined verification criteria proceeds to audit execution.

What Evidence
Verification Standard Governs

Evidence Verification Standard governs:

  • Evidence verification
  • Evidence validation
  • Evidence reliability
  • Evidence sufficiency
  • Verification methodology
  • Verification decisions
  • Verification assurance

Application

Evidence Verification Standard governs the objective verification of the
correlated evidence before it is accepted for audit execution and governance
oversight.

Result

Only verified evidence is used to support audit conclusions, accountability,
operational reconstruction, and independent verification.

Use Case 2 —
Regulatory Investigation

Scenario

A regulatory authority receives evidence from multiple organizations during a
formal compliance investigation.

Application

Evidence Verification Standard governs the verification of the submitted
evidence by evaluating its reliability, completeness, consistency,
authenticity, and sufficiency before regulatory decisions are made.

Result

Regulatory decisions are supported by objectively verified evidence rather
than assumptions or unverified information.

Architectural Position

Evidence Verification Standard represents the fifth governed space within
ADIT® — Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture™, serving as the
formal gateway between evidence governance and audit execution.

Governance Flow

Audit Observation → Evidence Formation → Evidence Integrity → Evidence
Correlation → Evidence Verification → Audit Execution → Audit Findings → Audit
Response → Audit Preservation → Audit Assurance

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Verification Standard establishes the canonical governance framework
for objectively verifying governed evidence before audit execution. By
governing evidence verification, validation methodology, reliability
assessment, sufficiency evaluation, and continuous verification assurance,
Evidence Verification Standard enables reliable audit, operational
reconstruction, accountable governance, and independent verification across
all operational domains.
