Audit Preservation Verification

Canonical Definition

Audit Preservation Verification Module defines the governance framework for
verifying that preserved audit evidence, records, findings, responses,
approvals, and supporting governance documentation continue to satisfy
preservation requirements throughout their complete lifecycle. It establishes
verification methodology, validation controls, governance mechanisms,
lifecycle review procedures, and continuous preservation verification
assurance necessary to ensure that preserved audit information remains
complete, authentic, accessible, traceable, and independently reconstructable
over time.

Operational Role

Audit Preservation Verification Module governs the verification layer of audit
preservation by continuously confirming that preserved audit information
maintains its required integrity, accessibility, traceability, and governance
value throughout the preservation lifecycle.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Verification Requirements

Establish preservation verification objectives, governance requirements,
validation criteria, review schedules, responsible authorities, and acceptance
conditions.

2. Define Verification Methodology

Develop standardized procedures for reviewing preserved audit information,
validating preservation quality, confirming accessibility, and documenting
verification activities.

3. Define Verification Validation Logic

Verify that preserved audit records remain complete, authentic, retrievable,
traceable, governance-compliant, and suitable for independent reconstruction
and future audit use.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish procedures for preservation failures, inaccessible records,
validation deficiencies, governance exceptions, lifecycle risks, and
corrective preservation actions.

5. Preserve Verification Records

Maintain verification history, validation reports, governance approvals,
corrective actions, review schedules, timestamps, and complete audit trails
throughout the preservation lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Audit Preservation Verification Module establishes the canonical governance
