About Evidence Integrity Standard - (EIS)

Understanding Evidence Integrity Standard

What Is Evidence Integrity Standard?

Evidence Integrity Standard is the third Parent Standard of ADIT® — Continuous
Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture™. It governs how governed evidence
remains authentic, complete, trustworthy, protected, and continuously
preservable after successful evidence formation.

Evidence Integrity Standard recognizes that successfully formed evidence does
not automatically remain trustworthy over time. Once evidence has been
created, it must be continuously protected against unauthorized alteration,
corruption, deletion, substitution, manipulation, degradation, or loss
throughout its complete operational lifecycle.

Canonical Definition

Evidence Integrity Standard defines the governance conditions required to
preserve the integrity of governed evidence after successful evidence
formation. It establishes evidence integrity principles, preservation
requirements, protection mechanisms, integrity validation controls, governance
safeguards, and continuous integrity assurance conditions necessary to ensure
that governed evidence remains authentic, complete, trustworthy, unaltered,
and suitable for audit, operational reconstruction, and independent
verification throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Why
Evidence Integrity Standard Exists

Trustworthy evidence is valuable only while its integrity remains preserved.

Evidence may exist for years before it is needed during an audit,
investigation, regulatory review, legal proceeding, or operational
reconstruction. Without continuous integrity governance, evidence may become
unreliable, incomplete, manipulated, or impossible to trust.

Evidence Integrity Standard exists to ensure that governed evidence remains
continuously authentic, protected, and demonstrably trustworthy throughout its
entire lifecycle.

What Evidence Integrity Standard Governs

Evidence Integrity Standard governs:

  • Evidence integrity
  • Evidence authenticity
  • Evidence preservation
  • Evidence protection
  • Evidence consistency
  • Evidence completeness
  • Evidence integrity validation


Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An autonomous AI platform continuously generates governed evidence documenting
operational decisions, autonomous actions, and system behavior.

Application

Evidence Integrity Standard governs how that evidence is protected against
unauthorized modification while continuously validating its authenticity,
completeness, and integrity throughout ongoing operations.

Result

Governed evidence remains continuously trustworthy and suitable for audit,
operational reconstruction, governance oversight, and independent
verification.

Use Case 2 — Digital Forensics

Scenario

A cybersecurity investigation relies on governed evidence collected from
multiple operational systems following a security incident.

Application

Evidence Integrity Standard governs the preservation, protection, validation,
and continuous integrity of the collected evidence before forensic analysis
and formal audit activities begin.

Result

Digital evidence remains authentic, protected, and objectively trustworthy
throughout the investigation and any subsequent legal or regulatory
proceedings.

Architectural Position

Evidence Integrity Standard represents the third governed space within ADIT® —
Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance Architecture™, preserving the
trustworthiness of governed evidence before evidence correlation, evidence
verification, and audit execution begin.

Governance Flow

Audit Observation → Evidence Formation → Evidence Integrity → Evidence
Correlation → Evidence Verification → Audit Execution → Audit Findings → Audit
Response → Audit Preservation → Audit Assurance

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Integrity Standard establishes the canonical governance framework for
continuously preserving the integrity of governed evidence throughout the
complete lifecycle of every governed entity. By defining how evidence remains
authentic, complete, trustworthy, protected, and continuously governable after
evidence formation, Evidence Integrity Standard enables reliable audit,
operational reconstruction, accountable governance, and independent
verification across all operational domains.
