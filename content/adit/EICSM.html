Evidence Consistency

Canonical Definition

Evidence Consistency Module defines the governance framework for ensuring that
governed evidence remains logically, temporally, structurally, and
operationally consistent throughout its complete lifecycle. It establishes
consistency principles, validation requirements, conflict detection
mechanisms, governance controls, and continuous consistency assurance
necessary to identify and resolve contradictions, discrepancies, and
inconsistencies before evidence is used for audit, investigation, operational
reconstruction, or independent verification.

Operational Role

Evidence Consistency Module governs the consistency layer of evidence
integrity by ensuring that governed evidence remains coherent,
non-contradictory, and operationally reliable across all related evidence
records.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Consistency Requirements

Identify consistency rules, logical relationships, temporal dependencies,
structural requirements, and governance conditions required for consistent
evidence.

2. Define Consistency Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for evaluating evidence consistency,
detecting conflicts, validating relationships, and maintaining coherence
across evidence records.

3. Define Consistency Validation Logic

Define how evidence consistency is evaluated by identifying contradictions,
conflicting timestamps, incompatible data, broken relationships, missing
dependencies, and operational inconsistencies.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for detected inconsistencies, conflicting
evidence, unresolved contradictions, validation failures, and corrective
governance decisions.

5. Preserve Consistency Assurance

Maintain consistency validation history, detected conflicts, governance
resolutions, corrective actions, and assurance records throughout the complete
lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Operations

Scenario

Multiple autonomous AI agents generate governed evidence describing the same
operational process.

Application

Evidence Consistency Module validates that timestamps, operational events,
identities, decisions, and evidence relationships remain logically consistent
across all generated evidence.

Result

The complete evidence set remains coherent, trustworthy, and suitable for
audit and operational reconstruction.

Use Case 2 — Financial Audit

Scenario

An organization submits financial evidence from multiple business systems for
an external audit.

Application

Evidence Consistency Module identifies contradictions between records,
validates consistency across systems, and ensures that supporting evidence
forms a coherent audit trail.

Result

Auditors receive evidence that is internally consistent, reliable, and
suitable for independent verification.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Consistency Module establishes the canonical governance framework for
preserving the logical, structural, temporal, and operational consistency of
governed evidence throughout its complete lifecycle. By governing evidence
coherence, conflict detection, consistency validation, and continuous
consistency assurance, the module enables reliable audit, operational
reconstruction, accountable governance, and independent verification across
all operational domains.
