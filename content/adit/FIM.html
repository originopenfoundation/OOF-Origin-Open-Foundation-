Finding Identification

Canonical Definition

Finding Identification Module defines the governance framework for identifying
potential audit findings from verified evidence produced through governed
audit execution. It establishes identification principles, evaluation
criteria, governance controls, identification methodology, and continuous
identification assurance necessary to ensure that every audit finding is
objectively recognized, evidence-based, and methodologically justified.

Operational Role

Finding Identification Module governs the identification layer of audit
findings by ensuring that potential findings are recognized objectively and
consistently before they are classified, validated, documented, and formally
issued.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Identification Requirements

Identify finding criteria, governance objectives, identification thresholds,
evaluation requirements, responsible authorities, and operational conditions
for recognizing potential findings.

2. Define Identification Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for reviewing verified evidence, identifying
audit findings, documenting observations, and ensuring methodological
consistency.

3. Define Identification Validation Logic

Define how potential findings are evaluated by verifying supporting evidence,
governance conformity, operational significance, repeatability, and objective
justification.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for disputed findings, unsupported observations,
identification errors, validation failures, conflicting interpretations, and
corrective measures.

5. Preserve Identification Records

Maintain finding identification history, supporting evidence, governance
decisions, validation records, timestamps, and corrective actions throughout
the complete audit lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An organization audits an autonomous AI platform and evaluates verified
evidence generated during system operation.

Application

Finding Identification Module governs the systematic identification of
operational issues, governance deviations, and significant observations
supported by verified evidence.

Result

Potential audit findings are identified consistently, objectively, and with
complete evidential support before further evaluation.

Use Case 2 — Regulatory Compliance Audit

Scenario

A regulatory authority reviews verified audit evidence collected during a
compliance inspection.

Application

Finding Identification Module governs the identification of regulatory
findings using standardized governance criteria and documented evidence
evaluation.

Result

Every potential finding is objectively recognized, evidence-based, and
suitable for formal audit assessment.

Canonical Closing Statement

Finding Identification Module establishes the canonical governance framework
for objectively identifying audit findings from verified evidence. By
governing finding identification, evidence evaluation, governance controls,
validation, and continuous identification assurance, the module enables
reliable audit conclusions, operational reconstruction, accountable
governance, and independent verification across all operational domains.
