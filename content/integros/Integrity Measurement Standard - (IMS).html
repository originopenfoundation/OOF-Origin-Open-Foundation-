Governed Space

Integrity Measurement

Core Question

How is integrity measured?

Canonical Definition

Integrity Measurement Standard (IMS) defines the governance conditions
required to measure integrity by establishing objective metrics, indicators,
thresholds, evaluation models, and scoring mechanisms that quantify the
integrity of a governed entity throughout its complete lifecycle.

Governance Flow Position

Definition → Structure → Conditions → Measurement → State → Deviation → Breach
→ Response → Restoration → Assurance

Standard Abstract

Integrity cannot be governed objectively unless it can be measured
consistently.

Definitions establish what integrity means, structures preserve it, and
conditions determine when it remains valid. However, governance decisions
require objective evidence rather than subjective judgment.

Building upon the integrity conditions established by ICS, Integrity
Measurement Standard defines how integrity is objectively measured and
evaluated.

IMS transforms integrity from a conceptual governance principle into
measurable operational reality through standardized metrics and evaluation
methods.

Operational Role

Integrity Measurement Standard governs the objective measurement of integrity.
It establishes the governance framework used to quantify integrity
consistently across different entities, operational environments, and
lifecycle stages.

Operational Space

IMS governs:

  • integrity metrics
  • integrity indicators
  • measurement models
  • evaluation methods
  • integrity thresholds
  • integrity scoring
  • measurement consistency.


This governed space ensures that integrity can be measured objectively,
compared consistently, and evaluated independently.

Why This Standard Exists

Integrity cannot be effectively governed through assumptions or subjective
interpretation.

Without objective measurement, organizations cannot determine whether
integrity is improving, degrading, stable, or compromised.

IMS exists because Integrity Measurement represents the fourth governed space
of INTEGROS® — Integrity Governance Architecture, providing the objective
governance foundation required before integrity states, deviations, and
breaches can be identified.

Foundational Principle

Integrity can only be governed objectively when it can be measured
consistently. Objective measurement transforms integrity from a defined
condition into observable operational reality.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity Measurement Standard establishes the governance framework required
to measure integrity objectively and consistently. By governing metrics,
indicators, thresholds, evaluation models, and scoring mechanisms, IMS
provides the measurable foundation upon which integrity states, deviations,
breaches, restoration, and assurance are built throughout the complete
lifecycle of every governed entity.
