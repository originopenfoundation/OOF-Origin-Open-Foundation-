Integrity Restoration Evidence

Canonical Definition

Integrity Restoration Evidence Module (IREVM) defines the governance framework
for collecting, validating, preserving, and governing evidence demonstrating
that integrity has been successfully restored. It establishes the evidence
requirements, validation principles, traceability mechanisms, preservation
rules, and governance controls required to support objective proof of
integrity restoration throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

IREVM governs the evidence layer of integrity restoration by ensuring that
every restoration decision is supported by sufficient, trustworthy, traceable,
and auditable evidence.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Restoration Evidence Requirements

Identify the evidence required to demonstrate restored integrity, including
validation results, operational records, governance approvals, technical
evidence, and supporting documentation.

2. Define Evidence Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for evidence collection, verification,
classification, preservation, traceability, and governance management.

3. Define Evidence Validation Logic

Define how restoration evidence is evaluated for authenticity, completeness,
consistency, reliability, traceability, evidentiary integrity, and compliance
with the approved governance methodology.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for missing evidence, insufficient proof,
conflicting evidence, evidence integrity failures, corrective evidence
collection, and escalation where necessary.

5. Preserve Evidence Records

Maintain restoration evidence repositories, validation reports, governance
approvals, chain-of-custody records, supporting documentation, timestamps, and
audit archives throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An autonomous AI platform completes integrity restoration and requests formal
approval to resume unrestricted autonomous operation.

Application

IREVM governs the collection and validation of operational evidence
demonstrating that approved integrity conditions have been fully restored and
documented.

Result

The restoration decision is supported by objective, traceable, and fully
auditable governance evidence before operational approval is granted.

Use Case 2 — Aviation Safety Systems

Scenario

An aviation operator restores flight management systems following an integrity
breach affecting operational safety.

Application

IREVM governs the collection, verification, preservation, and traceability of
restoration evidence required by internal governance authorities and aviation
regulators.

Result

Integrity restoration is supported by complete, trustworthy, and
regulator-ready evidence, ensuring accountable return to safe operational
service.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity Restoration Evidence Module establishes the canonical governance
