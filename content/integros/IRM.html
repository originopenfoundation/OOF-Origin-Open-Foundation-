Governed Space

Integrity Rules

Canonical Definition

Integrity Rule Module (IRM) defines the governance rules that regulate how
integrity conditions are applied, interpreted, enforced, and maintained
throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed entity. It establishes the
canonical rule set that transforms integrity conditions into consistent and
governable operational behavior.

Operational Role

IRM governs the rule layer of integrity conditions. It ensures that integrity
is preserved through explicit governance rules rather than discretionary
interpretation, enabling consistent decision-making across every stage of the
integrity lifecycle.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Integrity Rules

Identify every governance rule required to preserve integrity. Define
mandatory rules governing acceptable behavior, operational decisions,
structural consistency, condition enforcement, and integrity preservation.

2. Define Rule Requirements

Establish the governance requirements that determine how integrity rules are
interpreted, prioritized, applied, monitored, and enforced across the governed
entity.

3. Define Rule Violation Logic

Identify the conditions under which an integrity rule is violated, ignored,
bypassed, contradicted, or becomes inconsistent with the governance conditions
required to preserve integrity.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for enforcing, correcting, escalating, overriding
through authorized governance procedures, or revising integrity rules whenever
violations or operational changes occur.

5. Preserve Rule Records

Preserve integrity rule definitions, governance approvals, revisions,
exception records, enforcement history, supporting evidence, and rule
lifecycle documentation throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — AI Governance Platform

Scenario

An enterprise AI platform operates under governance rules requiring
explainable decision-making, human oversight for high-risk actions, and
continuous compliance with organizational AI policies.

Application

IRM defines and governs these integrity rules, ensuring that every operational
decision follows the approved governance framework.

Result

The AI platform maintains consistent integrity by applying the same governed
rules across all autonomous and human-supervised operations.

Use Case 2 — Pharmaceutical Manufacturing

Scenario

A pharmaceutical manufacturer must comply with strict production rules
governing quality assurance, process validation, documentation, contamination
control, and regulatory compliance.

Application

IRM establishes these integrity rules as mandatory governance requirements and
ensures they are consistently applied throughout the production lifecycle.

Result

Manufacturing integrity is preserved through governed operational rules,
reducing regulatory risk and maintaining consistent product quality.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity is preserved through governed rules that are consistently applied
and enforced. Integrity Rule Module establishes the canonical governance
framework that transforms integrity conditions into repeatable, transparent,
and independently governable operational practice throughout the complete
lifecycle of every governed entity.
