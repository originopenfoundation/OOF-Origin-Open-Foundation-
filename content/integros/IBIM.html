Governed Space

Integrity Breach Identification

Canonical Definition

Integrity Breach Identification Module (IBIM) defines the governance framework
for identifying when integrity has been materially compromised. It establishes
the identification criteria, breach indicators, evidence requirements, and
governance controls required to recognize integrity breaches consistently
throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

IBIM governs the identification layer of integrity breach management by
determining when observed integrity conditions satisfy the governance criteria
required to recognize a material integrity breach.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Breach Identification Criteria

Identify the governance conditions, operational events, integrity thresholds,
and evidence required to recognize an integrity breach.

2. Define Identification Requirements

Establish breach indicators, supporting evidence requirements, observation
methods, governance responsibilities, and identification procedures.

3. Define Identification Validation Logic

Define how suspected integrity breaches are verified for accuracy,
completeness, consistency, evidence sufficiency, and compliance with approved
governance methodology.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for confirmed breaches, suspected breaches, false
breach identifications, emergency escalation, and immediate containment.

5. Preserve Identification Records

Maintain breach identification records, supporting evidence, governance
decisions, timestamps, investigation references, and audit documentation
throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Platform

Scenario

An autonomous AI system repeatedly executes prohibited actions despite
governance safeguards, exceeding the organization's approved integrity breach
criteria.

Application

IBIM evaluates the evidence against predefined breach identification criteria
and formally recognizes the event as an integrity breach.

Result

The organization initiates breach governance based on objective and
standardized identification rules rather than subjective judgment.

Use Case 2 — Financial Governance System

Scenario

A financial institution detects unauthorized modifications to regulated
transaction records that compromise data integrity.

Application

IBIM validates the evidence and determines that the integrity compromise
satisfies the approved breach identification criteria.

Result

The integrity breach is identified consistently, enabling immediate governance
response, regulatory reporting, and accountability.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity breach governance begins with objective recognition of material
integrity failure. Integrity Breach Identification Module establishes the
canonical governance framework for consistently identifying integrity
breaches, enabling timely intervention, accountable governance, and
evidence-based protection of integrity throughout the complete lifecycle of
every governed entity.
