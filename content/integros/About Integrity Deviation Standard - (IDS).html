About Integrity Deviation Standard - (IDS)

Detecting Integrity Before It Fails

Integrity rarely disappears without warning. In most governed systems,
integrity gradually deviates from its approved operational state before a
complete integrity breach occurs. Detecting these deviations early enables
organizations to intervene before operational integrity is compromised.

What Is Integrity Deviation Standard?

Integrity Deviation Standard (IDS) is the sixth Parent Standard of INTEGROS® —
Integrity Governance Architecture. It establishes the canonical governance
conditions required to detect, classify, evaluate, monitor, and manage
deviations from approved integrity states throughout the complete lifecycle of
a governed entity.

Canonical Definition

Integrity Deviation Standard (IDS) defines the governance conditions required
to detect, classify, evaluate, and manage deviations from an approved
integrity state by establishing deviation criteria, severity levels,
evaluation methods, and governance rules throughout the complete lifecycle of
a governed entity.

Why IDS Exists

Knowing the current integrity state is not sufficient for effective
governance.

Organizations must also recognize when integrity begins to move away from its
approved state, even before operational failure occurs.

Building upon the integrity states established by ISS, IDS defines how
integrity deviations are identified, evaluated, and governed before they
evolve into integrity breaches.

This enables proactive governance, continuous monitoring, early intervention,
and systematic risk reduction.

What IDS Governs

IDS governs the complete lifecycle of integrity deviations, including:

  • deviation detection
  • deviation classification
  • deviation severity
  • deviation evaluation
  • deviation monitoring
  • deviation tracking
  • deviation reporting.


This governed space ensures that integrity deviations are recognized
consistently and managed before they become integrity breaches.

Architectural Position

Within INTEGROS® — Integrity Governance Architecture, IDS is the sixth Parent
Standard in the governance flow:

Definition → Structure → Conditions → Measurement → State → Deviation → Breach
→ Response → Restoration → Assurance

IDS establishes the canonical governance framework for recognizing and
managing integrity deterioration before breach management becomes necessary.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity Deviation Standard establishes the canonical governance framework
for detecting, classifying, evaluating, and managing departures from approved
integrity states. By governing integrity deviations before they develop into
integrity breaches, IDS enables proactive governance, early intervention,
operational resilience, and continuous preservation of integrity throughout
the complete lifecycle of every governed entity.
