Governed Space

Boundary Conditions

Canonical Definition

Boundary Condition Module (BCM) defines the governance conditions that
establish the operational limits within which integrity remains valid. It
governs the thresholds, boundaries, environmental limits, operational ranges,
and contextual constraints beyond which integrity can no longer be considered
preserved.

Operational Role

BCM governs the operational boundary conditions that determine whether a
governed entity continues to operate within the limits required to preserve
integrity. It ensures that integrity is evaluated not only by internal
conditions but also by the operational boundaries within which those
conditions remain valid.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Boundary Conditions

Identify every operational, organizational, environmental, technical, legal,
and governance boundary within which integrity remains valid. Define the
acceptable operating limits for the governed entity.

2. Define Boundary Requirements

Establish the governance requirements that specify acceptable operating
ranges, threshold values, contextual limitations, tolerance levels, and
permissible boundary conditions necessary to preserve integrity.

3. Define Boundary Violation Logic

Identify the conditions under which operational boundaries are exceeded,
environmental limits are violated, tolerance levels are surpassed, or
contextual changes invalidate the integrity conditions of the governed entity.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for detecting, reporting, mitigating, correcting,
escalating, or suspending operations whenever boundary conditions are violated
or no longer support integrity.

5. Preserve Boundary Records

Preserve boundary definitions, threshold values, governance approvals,
operational exceptions, violation history, contextual changes, and supporting
evidence throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Vehicle

Scenario

An autonomous vehicle is approved to operate only within defined weather
conditions, geographic regions, road classifications, and operational speed
limits.

Application

BCM governs these boundary conditions and continuously validates that the
vehicle remains within its authorized operational limits.

Result

The autonomous vehicle preserves operational integrity by remaining within the
governed boundaries under which safe and legitimate operation has been
validated.

Use Case 2 — Cloud Infrastructure Governance

Scenario

A cloud platform operates under defined security classifications, regional
data residency requirements, infrastructure capacity limits, and regulatory
jurisdictions.

Application

BCM governs these operational boundaries and detects when changes in
infrastructure, regulation, or operating conditions exceed the approved
integrity limits.

Result

The cloud platform maintains integrity by ensuring that operations remain
continuously within the approved governance boundaries throughout its
lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity exists only within governed boundaries. Boundary Condition Module
establishes the operational limits within which integrity remains valid,
ensuring that every governed entity operates safely, consistently, and
legitimately throughout its complete lifecycle.
