Governed Space

Integrity Constraints

Canonical Definition

Integrity Constraint Module (ICNM) defines the mandatory governance
constraints that limit, regulate, and preserve integrity by establishing the
boundaries within which a governed entity may operate without compromising its
integrity throughout the complete lifecycle.

Operational Role

ICNM governs the constraints that preserve integrity by preventing actions,
conditions, configurations, or behaviors capable of compromising the governed
integrity of an entity. It transforms integrity conditions into enforceable
governance limitations.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Integrity Constraints

Identify every mandatory governance constraint required to preserve integrity.
Each constraint shall clearly define the operational limits beyond which
integrity can no longer be guaranteed.

2. Define Constraint Requirements

Establish the governance rules, thresholds, dependencies, permissions,
prohibitions, and operational requirements that determine whether each
integrity constraint remains satisfied.

3. Define Constraint Violation Logic

Identify the conditions under which an integrity constraint becomes violated,
bypassed, exceeded, ignored, or otherwise incapable of preserving integrity.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for detecting, preventing, correcting, approving,
escalating, or rejecting activities that violate established integrity
constraints.

5. Preserve Constraint Records

Preserve integrity constraint definitions, governance approvals, enforcement
records, violation history, revisions, supporting evidence, and lifecycle
documentation.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Vehicle Governance

Scenario

An autonomous vehicle operates under mandatory safety, operational,
regulatory, and environmental constraints that preserve the integrity of its
decision-making processes.

Application

ICNM establishes the governance constraints that limit operational behavior,
ensuring the vehicle cannot perform actions that would compromise its governed
integrity.

Result

The autonomous vehicle remains within explicitly governed operational
boundaries, preserving integrity while reducing the risk of unsafe or
unauthorized behavior.

Use Case 2 — Enterprise Data Governance

Scenario

A multinational organization manages sensitive business information subject to
regulatory, contractual, and organizational integrity requirements.

Application

ICNM defines the governance constraints controlling access, modification,
transfer, retention, and processing of governed information to preserve data
integrity.

Result

The organization maintains a governed operating environment where integrity is
preserved through continuously enforced constraints and transparent governance
controls.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity is preserved not only by what is permitted, but also by what is
constrained. Integrity Constraint Module establishes the mandatory governance
limits that protect integrity by preventing conditions, actions, and decisions
capable of compromising the governed entity throughout its complete lifecycle.
