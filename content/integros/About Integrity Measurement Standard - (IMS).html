About Integrity Measurement Standard - (IMS)

Measuring Integrity Objectively

Integrity cannot be effectively governed through assumptions or subjective
judgment. Once integrity has been defined, supported by an appropriate
structure, and preserved through mandatory governance conditions, it must be
measured objectively to determine whether it is being maintained throughout
the lifecycle of a governed entity.

What Is Integrity Measurement Standard?

Integrity Measurement Standard (IMS) is the fourth Parent Standard of
INTEGROS® — Integrity Governance Architecture. It establishes the canonical
governance conditions required to objectively measure integrity by defining
the metrics, indicators, thresholds, evaluation models, and scoring mechanisms
used to assess integrity consistently throughout the complete lifecycle.

Canonical Definition

Integrity Measurement Standard (IMS) defines the governance conditions
required to objectively measure integrity by establishing metrics, indicators,
thresholds, evaluation models, and scoring mechanisms throughout the complete
lifecycle of a governed entity.

Why IMS Exists

A governed entity may have a clearly defined integrity model, a structurally
sound architecture, and valid operating conditions, but governance still
requires objective evidence of whether integrity is actually being maintained.

Building upon the integrity conditions established by ICS, IMS defines how
integrity is objectively measured and evaluated.

IMS exists to transform integrity from a governance concept into measurable
operational reality before integrity states, deviations, and breaches are
identified.

What IMS Governs

IMS governs the objective measurement of integrity, including:

  • integrity metrics
  • integrity indicators
  • measurement models
  • evaluation methods
  • integrity thresholds
  • integrity scoring
  • measurement consistency.


This governed space ensures that integrity is measured objectively,
consistently, and independently across all governed entities.

Architectural Position

Within INTEGROS® — Integrity Governance Architecture, IMS is the fourth Parent
Standard in the governance flow:

Definition → Structure → Conditions → Measurement → State → Deviation → Breach
→ Response → Restoration → Assurance

IMS transforms governed integrity conditions into measurable evidence,
providing the objective foundation required for integrity state classification
and every subsequent governance activity.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity Measurement Standard establishes the governance framework required
to measure integrity objectively and consistently. By governing metrics,
indicators, thresholds, evaluation models, and scoring mechanisms, IMS
provides the measurable foundation upon which integrity state evaluation,
deviation detection, breach identification, restoration, and assurance are
built throughout the complete lifecycle of every governed entity.
