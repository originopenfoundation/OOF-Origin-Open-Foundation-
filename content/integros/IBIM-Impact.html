Integrity Breach Impact

Canonical Definition

Integrity Breach Impact Module (IBIM) defines the governance framework for
evaluating the operational consequences of an integrity breach. It establishes
the methods, criteria, impact dimensions, and governance controls required to
assess how an integrity breach affects the governed entity, its stakeholders,
dependent systems, and the surrounding operational environment throughout its
complete lifecycle.

Operational Role

IBIM governs the impact assessment layer of integrity breach management by
determining the operational, financial, legal, regulatory, technical,
reputational, and safety consequences resulting from an integrity breach.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Impact Assessment Scope

Identify the operational domains, assets, stakeholders, processes, systems,
and external dependencies affected by the integrity breach.

2. Define Impact Assessment Requirements

Establish impact evaluation criteria, evidence requirements, consequence
categories, measurement methods, and governance responsibilities.

3. Define Impact Validation Logic

Define how impact assessments are verified for accuracy, completeness,
consistency, objectivity, evidence sufficiency, and compliance with the
approved governance methodology.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions proportional to the assessed impact, including
containment, escalation, recovery planning, stakeholder communication,
regulatory reporting, and risk mitigation.

5. Preserve Impact Records

Maintain impact assessments, supporting evidence, governance decisions,
consequence analyses, historical assessments, and audit documentation
throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Infrastructure

Scenario

A verified integrity breach causes unauthorized autonomous decisions that
affect multiple operational services.

Application

IBIM evaluates the operational disruption, affected stakeholders, regulatory
exposure, and downstream system consequences before governance decisions are
made.

Result

The organization responds proportionally to the actual impact of the integrity
breach using objective governance evidence.

Use Case 2 — Financial Services Platform

Scenario

A breach compromises transaction integrity across several financial systems.

Application

IBIM assesses the financial losses, customer impact, regulatory obligations,
operational disruption, and reputational consequences.

Result

Governance decisions, recovery priorities, and regulatory actions are aligned
with the objectively assessed breach impact.

Canonical Closing Statement

The significance of an integrity breach is determined not only by its
existence but by its governed operational consequences. Integrity Breach
Impact Module establishes the canonical governance framework for objectively
assessing breach impact, enabling proportionate governance actions, informed
decision-making, and resilient operational recovery throughout the complete
lifecycle of every governed entity.
