Governed Space

Integrity Scope

Canonical Definition

Integrity Scope Module (ISPM) defines the extent of integrity governance by
specifying which entities, components, processes, relationships, and lifecycle
stages are subject to integrity requirements. It establishes the formal scope
within which integrity governance is applied and enforced.

Module Purpose

ISPM ensures that the application of integrity governance is explicit,
complete, and consistently bounded. It prevents uncertainty by defining
exactly what falls within the governed integrity space and what remains
outside its intended application.

Without a clearly defined scope, integrity governance may become incomplete,
inconsistent, or incorrectly applied.

Operational Role

ISPM establishes the official scope of integrity governance, ensuring that
every integrity definition, measurement, evaluation, restoration, and
assurance activity applies only to the intended governed space.

Operational Space

ISPM governs:

  • integrity scope
  • scope boundaries
  • governed entities
  • lifecycle coverage
  • applicability rules.


Minimum Implementation Framework

An implementation of ISPM should define:

  • governed entities
  • covered components
  • covered lifecycle stages
  • exclusions
  • scope modification rules.


Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous Systems

Define whether integrity governance applies only to the autonomous agent
itself or also to its training data, decision models, external interfaces, and
runtime operations.

Use Case 2 — Supply Chain Governance

Specify which organizations, assets, operational processes, and transfer
events are included within the governed integrity scope.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity governance is effective only when its scope is explicitly defined.
Integrity Scope Module establishes the formal extent of integrity governance,
ensuring that every integrity obligation, evaluation, and assurance activity
is applied consistently across the intended governed space throughout the
complete lifecycle of the entity.
