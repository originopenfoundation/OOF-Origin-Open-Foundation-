Governed Space

Structural Mapping

Canonical Definition

Structure Mapping Module (STMM) defines the canonical structural
representation of a governed entity by identifying its components,
relationships, dependencies, hierarchy, and organizational arrangement. It
establishes the structural reference required to preserve, evaluate, and
govern integrity throughout the complete lifecycle of the entity.

Operational Role

STMM establishes the official structural map upon which every subsequent
structural integrity activity depends. It provides the governed representation
of how the entity is organized before structural integrity can be measured,
monitored, evaluated, or assured.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Governed Structure

Identify the governed entity and define every structural component that forms
its integrity architecture. All components, organizational units, assets,
services, processes, or functional elements requiring integrity governance
shall be explicitly identified and documented.

2. Define Structural Relationships

Establish the relationships, dependencies, hierarchy, interactions, and
organizational connections between structural components. The structural map
shall accurately represent how components interact and collectively preserve
structural integrity.

3. Define Structural Failure Logic

Identify the conditions under which the structural model becomes incomplete,
inconsistent, fragmented, ambiguous, obsolete, or incapable of preserving
integrity. Define how structural weaknesses and missing relationships are
detected.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for validating, updating, correcting, restructuring,
approving, or rejecting structural changes whenever modifications affect the
governed integrity structure.

5. Preserve Structural Records

Preserve structural maps, governance approvals, dependency records,
relationship definitions, structural revisions, version history, and
supporting evidence required to demonstrate structural integrity throughout
the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Corporate Governance

Scenario

An organization prepares for an enterprise-wide governance assessment and must
establish a complete representation of its governance structure before
evaluating structural integrity.

Application

STMM maps all governance bodies, organizational units, reporting
relationships, operational processes, critical assets, and structural
dependencies into a single governed structural model.

Result

The organization obtains a complete structural reference that enables
consistent structural integrity assessment, governance analysis, and
controlled structural evolution.

Use Case 2 — Autonomous AI System

Scenario

An autonomous AI platform contains multiple decision engines, memory services,
identity services, governance controls, external interfaces, and runtime
components that operate together.

Application

STMM creates the canonical structural map of the complete AI architecture by
documenting every governed component, dependency, hierarchy, and interaction
before structural integrity is evaluated.

Result

The AI system becomes structurally governable, allowing integrity assessments,
governance decisions, and future architectural changes to be performed against
a complete and continuously maintained structural reference.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity can only be preserved when the underlying structure is fully
understood. Structure Mapping Module establishes the canonical structural
representation that enables every structural integrity assessment, governance
decision, and assurance activity throughout the complete lifecycle of a
governed entity.
