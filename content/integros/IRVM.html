Integrity Restoration Validation

Canonical Definition

Integrity Restoration Validation Module (IRVM) defines the governance
framework for validating that integrity has been successfully restored
following approved restoration activities. It establishes the validation
criteria, evidence requirements, verification methods, acceptance conditions,
and governance controls required to confirm that approved integrity conditions
have been objectively re-established before the governed entity proceeds to
continuous integrity assurance.

Operational Role

IRVM governs the validation layer of integrity restoration by ensuring that no
restoration is considered complete until restored integrity has been
objectively verified, documented, approved, and supported by sufficient
governance evidence.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Restoration Validation Requirements

Identify the integrity conditions, validation criteria, acceptance thresholds,
evidence requirements, responsible validators, and governance objectives
required to confirm successful restoration.

2. Define Validation Methodology

Establish standardized validation procedures, testing methods, verification
activities, review processes, approval workflows, and governance checkpoints.

3. Define Validation Logic

Define how restored integrity is evaluated for completeness, effectiveness,
consistency, operational readiness, governance compliance, and conformity with
approved restoration objectives.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for failed validation, partial restoration,
insufficient evidence, validation disputes, corrective restoration activities,
revalidation, and escalation where required.

5. Preserve Validation Records

Maintain validation reports, supporting evidence, governance approvals,
testing results, corrective actions, acceptance decisions, timestamps, and
audit documentation throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An autonomous AI platform completes all approved restoration activities after
a verified integrity breach and requests authorization to resume unrestricted
operation.

Application

IRVM validates restored governance conditions, verifies operational integrity,
reviews supporting evidence, and confirms that all approved restoration
objectives have been successfully achieved.

Result

Autonomous operations resume only after integrity restoration has been
objectively validated through documented governance evidence.

Use Case 2 — National Financial Infrastructure

Scenario

A central payment infrastructure completes restoration following a major
integrity breach affecting transaction processing.

Application

IRVM validates restored operational integrity, confirms compliance with
governance requirements, reviews recovery evidence, and authorizes the
transition to continuous integrity assurance.

Result

Critical financial services resume under verified integrity conditions
supported by objective validation, governance approval, and complete
auditability.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity Restoration Validation Module establishes the canonical governance
framework for objectively validating restored integrity through standardized
verification, accountable governance, documented evidence, and auditable
approval. It ensures that integrity restoration is accepted only when approved
integrity conditions have been demonstrably re-established throughout the
complete lifecycle of every governed entity.
