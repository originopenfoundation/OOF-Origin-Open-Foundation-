Governed Space

Integrity Indicators

Canonical Definition

Integrity Indicators Module (IIM) defines the governance indicators used to
detect, monitor, and communicate the observable characteristics of integrity.
It establishes the standardized indicators that reveal whether integrity is
being maintained, improving, degrading, or approaching unacceptable
operational conditions throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

IIM governs the indicator layer of integrity measurement by transforming raw
integrity metrics into meaningful governance signals that support continuous
monitoring, operational awareness, and evidence-based decision-making.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Integrity Indicators

Identify the observable indicators that represent the operational condition of
integrity and provide meaningful governance insight.

2. Define Indicator Requirements

Establish indicator definitions, data sources, measurement frequency,
calculation methods, interpretation criteria, and governance requirements for
each integrity indicator.

3. Define Indicator Validation Logic

Define how indicator accuracy, consistency, reliability, and operational
relevance are verified before indicators are used for governance decisions.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions when integrity indicators reveal degradation,
instability, abnormal trends, or unacceptable operational conditions.

5. Preserve Indicator Records

Maintain indicator definitions, historical values, governance decisions, trend
analyses, revisions, and supporting evidence throughout the complete lifecycle
of the governed entity.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Operations

Scenario

An autonomous AI platform continuously monitors indicators such as policy
compliance rate, human intervention frequency, anomaly occurrence, and
decision consistency.

Application

IIM governs these integrity indicators and converts operational measurements
into governance signals for continuous oversight.

Result

Integrity degradation is detected early, enabling timely governance actions
before significant failures occur.

Use Case 2 — Critical Infrastructure Monitoring

Scenario

A power grid operator monitors indicators including equipment health,
operational stability, security events, and maintenance compliance.

Application

IIM governs these indicators to provide continuous visibility into the
operational integrity of critical infrastructure.

Result

Operators receive objective governance indicators that support proactive risk
management and integrity preservation.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity is not governed by measurements alone but by understanding what
those measurements reveal. Integrity Indicators Module establishes the
governance framework that transforms objective measurements into actionable
operational signals, enabling continuous integrity monitoring, early detection
of degradation, and evidence-based governance throughout the complete
lifecycle of every governed entity.
