Canonical Definition System

Canonical Definition

Behavioral Evidence Preservation Module (BEPM) defines the structural
conditions under which AI behavioral evidence remains preserved, accessible,
protected, retrievable, governance-valid, and suitable for future review
throughout the complete behavioral lifecycle.

BEPM governs behavioral evidence preservation.

The module establishes the governance conditions required to ensure that
valuable behavioral evidence is not lost, degraded, overwritten, or rendered
unavailable before its governance purpose has been fulfilled.

Evidence creates value beyond the moment it is produced.

Preservation protects that value.

BEPM governs that preservation.

Module Operational Role

BEPM defines the behavioral-evidence-preservation layer of BVES.

Its role is to preserve governance evidence throughout its useful governance
lifecycle.

Module Operational Space

BEPM governs:

  • behavioral evidence preservation
  • evidence retention
  • evidence protection
  • evidence availability
  • governance record preservation
  • long-term evidence continuity
  • evidence accessibility
  • preservation governance


The module applies wherever AI behavioral evidence must remain available for
governance, accountability, compliance, investigation, or future
organizational learning.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • long-term behavioral evidence,
  • governance-valid evidence,
  • accessible evidence,
  • protected evidence,
  • retrievable evidence,
  • trustworthy governance history.


Its function is to ensure that governance evidence remains available whenever
organizations need to verify, investigate, explain, or improve AI behavior.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Behavioral Evidence Preservation Object

The organization must define which behavioral evidence requires preservation.

2. Define Preservation Conditions

The system must define preservation requirements including:

  • retention conditions,
  • accessibility requirements,
  • protection requirements,
  • governance availability,
  • retrieval capability,
  • preservation validation.


3. Define Preservation Failure Detection Logic

The system must identify:

  • missing evidence,
  • degraded evidence,
  • inaccessible records,
  • unauthorized deletion,
  • incomplete preservation,
  • governance-invalid preservation.


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

Governance response may include:

  • preservation review,
  • evidence recovery,
  • governance investigation,
  • compliance validation,
  • preservation improvement,
  • operational correction.


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • preservation activities,
  • governance reviews,
  • evidence access,
  • validation history,
  • preservation controls,
  • resulting governance outcomes.


An AI behavioral environment must not remain evidence-valid if materially
significant behavioral evidence cannot be preserved, reviewed, validated,
retrieved, or governed.

Use Case 1 — AI Regulatory Compliance

Scenario

A regulatory authority requests behavioral evidence generated by an AI system
several years after operational execution.

Application

BEPM ensures that the evidence remains complete, protected, retrievable, and
governance-valid despite the passage of time.

Result

The organization demonstrates long-term compliance, strengthens governance
credibility, and supports independent regulatory review.

Use Case 2 — Autonomous Manufacturing Investigation

Scenario

An organization investigates an operational incident involving autonomous
production systems months after the event occurred.

Application

BEPM preserves all relevant behavioral evidence, enabling investigators to
reconstruct events using complete governance records.

Result

The organization improves operational learning, strengthens accountability,
supports incident investigations, and preserves institutional knowledge.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence protects the past only when the future can still reach it.
Preservation ensures that governance does not lose the knowledge required to
explain, validate, and improve AI behavior across time.
