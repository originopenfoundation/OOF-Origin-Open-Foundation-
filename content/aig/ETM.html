Canonical Definition System

Canonical Definition

Escalation Trigger Module (ETM) defines the structural conditions under which
predefined behavioral events, governance thresholds, operational risks,
confidence limits, policy violations, or safety conditions automatically
trigger AI behavioral escalation.

ETM governs escalation triggers.

The module establishes the conditions required to ensure that AI systems
consistently recognize when escalation must begin, preventing delayed
intervention, missed governance actions, or uncontrolled autonomous execution.

Autonomous behavior should never depend on subjective escalation decisions.

Escalation begins when predefined governance triggers are activated.

ETM governs those triggers.

Module Operational Role

ETM defines the escalation-trigger layer of BES.

Its role is to identify the precise conditions that initiate behavioral
escalation.

Module Operational Space

ETM governs:

  • escalation triggers
  • behavioral trigger conditions
  • governance threshold activation
  • confidence threshold violations
  • operational trigger events
  • policy trigger conditions
  • safety trigger activation
  • escalation trigger validation


The module applies wherever AI must automatically determine when autonomous
behavior should transition into governed escalation.

Module Function

ETM ensures that escalation is initiated consistently, predictably,

  • traceably, and according to predefined governance conditions rather than
  • arbitrary system behavior.


Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define escalation trigger events

2. Define governance thresholds

3. Define confidence thresholds

4. Define trigger evaluation logic

5. Preserve complete traceability of every escalation trigger

Use Case

An autonomous healthcare AI detects that diagnostic confidence falls below an
approved threshold.

Instead of continuing autonomously, the predefined escalation trigger
activates and immediately transfers the case to a qualified physician.

The organization reduces clinical risk, improves Human-AI collaboration, and
preserves governance integrity.

Canonical Closing Statement

Escalation Trigger Module (ETM) defines the structural conditions under which
predefined governance conditions initiate AI behavioral escalation.

Every trustworthy escalation begins with a trustworthy trigger. Escalation
Triggers therefore become the first operational condition of Behavioral
Escalation Standard within AI Governance Architecture (AIG®).
