Governed Space

Integrity Restoration Planning

Canonical Definition

Integrity Restoration Planning Module (IRPM) defines the governance framework
for planning integrity restoration following a verified integrity breach and
completed response activities. It establishes the planning principles,
restoration objectives, recovery priorities, resource requirements, validation
criteria, and governance controls required to prepare a structured,
accountable, and auditable restoration process throughout the complete
lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

IRPM governs the planning layer of integrity restoration by ensuring that
restoration activities are systematically designed, prioritized, approved, and
aligned with the organization's governance objectives before restoration
execution begins.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Restoration Planning Requirements

Identify the integrity conditions to be restored, recovery objectives,
affected operational domains, resource requirements, dependencies, and
governance priorities.

2. Define Restoration Planning Methodology

Establish standardized planning procedures, restoration milestones, role
assignments, approval mechanisms, timelines, and governance checkpoints.

3. Define Planning Validation Logic

Define how restoration plans are evaluated for completeness, feasibility,
consistency, governance compliance, operational effectiveness, and alignment
with approved restoration objectives.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for incomplete restoration plans, planning
conflicts, resource limitations, changing operational conditions, approval
failures, and escalation requirements.

5. Preserve Planning Records

Maintain restoration plans, governance approvals, planning assumptions,
dependencies, resource allocations, revisions, supporting evidence, and audit
documentation throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

Following a verified integrity breach, an autonomous AI platform requires a
structured restoration plan before production services can safely resume.

Application

IRPM defines restoration objectives, prioritizes recovery activities,
allocates governance responsibilities, establishes validation milestones, and
prepares the approved restoration roadmap.

Result

Integrity restoration begins through a controlled, accountable, and
well-governed plan that minimizes operational uncertainty.

Use Case 2 — Financial Services Recovery

Scenario

A banking institution must restore operational integrity after containing a
breach affecting transaction processing systems.

Application

IRPM coordinates restoration planning across operational teams, governance
authorities, compliance functions, and technical specialists before recovery
activities commence.

Result

The organization executes restoration according to an approved governance plan
supported by documented priorities, validated objectives, and complete
accountability.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity Restoration Planning Module establishes the canonical governance
framework for preparing integrity restoration through structured planning,
approved governance objectives, validated recovery strategies, and accountable
decision-making. It ensures that every restoration activity begins with a
controlled, transparent, and fully auditable governance plan throughout the
complete lifecycle of every governed entity.
