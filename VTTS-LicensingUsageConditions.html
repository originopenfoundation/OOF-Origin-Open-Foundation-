VTTS™ — Licensing &
Usage Conditions

1. Licensing Model

The Value Transfer Taxation Standard (VTTS™) is released under the following structure:

Open Reference · Controlled Implementation

This model allows free access to knowledge while preserving controlled
use of implementation and system deployment.

2. Permitted Use (Free Use)

The following uses are permitted without authorization:
  • reading and studying the standard
  • academic and research use
  • public discussion and policy consideration
  • non-operational referencing
Such use must maintain full and accurate attribution to:

OOF™ Origin Open Foundation™

3. Restricted Use (License Required)

The following uses require prior authorization from OOF™:
  • commercial implementation of VTTS™
  • integration into platforms, systems, or applications
  • deployment within financial or economic infrastructures
  • state-level or governmental adoption
  • automated taxation systems based on VTTS™ logic
Unauthorized use in these contexts is not permitted.

4. Canonical Definition Requirement

All use of VTTS™ must preserve:
  • exact canonical definitions
  • structural logic
  • terminology integrity
Definitions must not be:
  • modified
  • reinterpreted
  • simplified
  • partially applied
Any deviation results in loss of canonical validity.

5. Attribution Requirement

All references, uses, or implementations must clearly state:

“Based on VTTS™ — Value Transfer Taxation Standard, defined by OOF™ Origin Open Foundation™.”

Removal or omission of attribution is considered misuse.

6. Compatibility Condition

Only systems that fully implement VTTS™ without deviation may be described as:

OOF™ Compatible

Any system that:
  • modifies definitions
  • partially implements logic
  • removes structural elements
must not claim compatibility.

7. Misuse Definition

Misuse includes:
  • unauthorized commercial use
  • altered or redefined core concepts
  • presenting partial implementation as VTTS™
  • removal of attribution
  • misleading claims of compatibility

8. Liability

Unauthorized use, modification, or misrepresentation of VTTS™ may result in:
  • claims for economic damages
  • claims for reputational damages
  • enforcement of corrective measures
Use of VTTS™ implies acceptance of these conditions.

9. Enforcement

OOF™ reserves the right to:
  • deny or revoke compatibility status
  • publicly classify systems as non-compliant
  • enforce licensing conditions

10. Ownership Statement

VTTS™ is a canonical methodological standard defined and governed by:

OOF™ Origin Open Foundation™

All derivative or applied systems must:
  • maintain reference to OOF™
  • respect canonical definitions
  • preserve structural integrity

11. Final Principle

VTTS™ may be referenced freely.
Its canonical form remains exclusively defined by OOF™
