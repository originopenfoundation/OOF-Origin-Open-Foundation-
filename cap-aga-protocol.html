CAP™ — Chain of Accountability Protocol

Accountability Governance Architecture (AGA™)

Official Operational Protocol for Accountability Reconstruction,
Responsibility Analysis, Authority Validation, Oversight Assessment
and Consequence Attribution

CAP™ (Chain of Accountability Protocol) is the operational protocol
of the Accountability Governance Architecture (AGA™).

While AGA™ defines the governance architecture and the governable
accountability spaces, CAP™ defines the operational methodology used
to reconstruct, analyze, validate, and attribute accountability
across complex operational environments.

CAP™ provides a structured process for identifying where
accountability originates, how it moves through operational
structures, how governance obligations are transferred or retained,
where failures occur, and who remains accountable for outcomes.

The protocol applies to:

  • organizations
  • institutions
  • contractor networks
  • subcontractor chains
  • staffing agencies
  • compliance investigations
  • incident investigations
  • operational failures
  • governance failures
  • AI systems
  • autonomous agents
  • structured agent systems
  • orchestrated agent systems
  • robotics environments
  • Human-AI ecosystems
  • future intelligent operational systems


Why CAP™ Exists

Modern operational environments rarely involve a single actor.

Most activities occur through multiple layers of:

  • organizations
  • managers
  • contractors
  • subcontractors
  • agencies
  • service providers
  • AI agents
  • autonomous systems
  • robotic systems


As complexity increases, accountability becomes increasingly
difficult to identify.

Organizations often know:

  • what happened


but struggle to determine:

  • who authorized it
  • who was responsible
  • who controlled it
  • who should have supervised it
  • who remains accountable


CAP™ exists to systematically reconstruct that chain.

Core Protocol Question

How did accountability move through the operational system from
origin to consequence?

Canonical Principle

Accountability cannot be assumed. Accountability must
be reconstructed.

Canonical Closing Statement

CAP™ (Chain of Accountability Protocol) is the operational protocol
of the Accountability Governance Architecture (AGA™), providing a
structured methodology for reconstructing accountability across
relationships, authority structures, responsibility chains,
capability environments, operational control systems, oversight
mechanisms, evidence layers, and consequence attribution processes.

As operational environments become increasingly distributed,
autonomous, orchestrated, and complex, accountability reconstruction
becomes a foundational governance capability. CAP™ provides the
protocol for that capability.
