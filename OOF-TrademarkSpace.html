OOF® Trademark Space

Registered and Protected Signs within the OOF® System

Foundation

This page identifies the trademark signs currently forming the protected
OOF® trademark space.

OOF® is derived from Origin Open Foundation and operates as the core
system sign of the OOF® methodological space.

OOF® does not claim generic categories. OOF® defines and protects
its own structured trademark space within them.

All elements listed on this page operate within the broader protection
framework of:

MIP® — Methodological Intellectual Property

Core Principle

Trademark rights protect the sign. MIP® protection preserves the
canonical meaning, structural role, methodological integrity,
architectural continuity, and system position attached to that sign.

Methodological Naming Spaces

OOF® also defines and protects methodological naming spaces through
MIP® where canonical meaning, structural role, and system position are
fixed by OOF® methodology.

This includes, among others:
  • Structured Reality
  • Operational Reality
  • Operational Architecture Spaces
  • Methodology Operating System
  • Governance Methodology Infrastructure
These terms are not presented as generic ownership claims over
ordinary language.
They are protected as OOF® methodological naming spaces where their
meaning, structure, and architectural role are defined within the OOF®
system.

Protection Scope

The elements listed on this page define the protected trademark and
methodological naming space currently established by OOF®.
This protection applies to:
  • distinctive trademark signs
  • trademark identity within the OOF® system
  • canonical definitions attached to protected signs
  • structural roles protected under MIP®
  • public-facing and commercial differentiation of OOF® elements
  • methodological naming spaces defined by OOF®
Use of these names without structural alignment:
  • does not grant compatibility
  • does not grant system participation
  • does not grant methodological validity
  • does not grant economic access within the OOF® system
