VIM™ — Voice Identity Module

OOF™ Origin Open Foundation™

Independent Methodological Authority

Category: AI and Interpretation
Subcategory: Content Origin Declaration
Module: Voice Identity Module (VIM™)
Type: Declaration Module
Standard: Origin Declaration Standard (ODS™)
Status: Canonical Module
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 17 March 2026


Canonical Definition

The Voice Identity Module (VIM™) defines how the identity of a voice
used in digital content is declared.

The module enables clear recognition of whether a voice belongs to a real person,
an authorized identity, an imitation, or is unknown.

Declaration

Only one voice identity code may be used:

  • HVI — Human Voice Identity (real person)
  • AVI — Authorized Voice Identity (consent granted)
  • SVI — Sound-alike Voice Imitation
  • UVI — Unknown Voice Identity

Usage

Content Level (required — single code)

One voice identity code must be associated with the content where voice is present.

Examples:

  • HVI
  • AVI
  • SVI
  • UVI

Description Level (required)

The declaration must be included in the description or metadata: 

Voice: HVI
OOF Compatible — ODS™ Standard
https://originopenfoundation.org

Identity Rule

Voice identity must reflect the actual source of the voice.

  • real person → HVI
  • authorized use → AVI
  • imitation or synthetic reproduction → SVI
  • unclear or undeclared → UVI

OOF Compatible — ODS™ Standard

Use Case 1 — AI Voice Transparency

Scenario

AI-generated voices are used in content, narration, or automation.

Application

Voice is declared as AVI or SVI depending on authorization.

Result

  • Clear distinction between real and synthetic identity
  • Reduced misuse of voice likeness
  • Increased audience trust

Use Case 2 — Public Figure Protection

Scenario

A voice similar to a known individual is used in content.

Application

Voice identity is declared (AVI or SVI).

Result

  • Clear disclosure of imitation or authorization
  • Reduced risk of deception
  • Protection of identity rights

Structural Principle

Voice is identity.
Identity must be declared.

Closing Statement

A voice may sound real.
Its identity must be known.
