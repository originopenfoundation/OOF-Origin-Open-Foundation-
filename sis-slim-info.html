Surface Integrity Standard (SIS) —
Labeling & Claim Usage (SLIM)

Purpose

This page defines how the Surface Label Integrity Module (SLIM), under the Surface Integrity
Standard (SIS), may be referenced across products, packaging, and digital environments.

The objective is to ensure clarity, consistency, trust, and correct system usage.

Core System Rule

All claims must be anchored to OOF.

Standards and modules provide structure.
OOF provides authority.

Short Product Marking (Recommended)

For physical products and limited surface space:

Primary:

OOF Aligned — SLIM

Optional variant:

OOF Aligned (Label Integrity Module)

These formats are designed for product labels, packaging, retail environments, and small surfaces.

Extended Reference (Optional)

Where more space is available:

OOF Aligned — Surface Integrity Standard (SIS) — Surface Label Integrity Module (SLIM)

Full Reference (Digital / Documentation)

For websites, product pages, or documentation:

OOF Aligned — Surface Integrity Standard (SIS) — Surface Label Integrity Module (SLIM) — defined
by OOF Origin Open Foundation

Usage Conditions

The claim may only be used if all module conditions are met:
  • the label is fully removable
  • no surface damage occurs
  • no residue remains
  • no chemical or mechanical force is required
  • the surface remains unchanged
If any condition is not met:

the claim must not be used.

Prohibited Claims

The following are strictly prohibited:
  • OOF Approved
  • OOF Certified
  • OOF Verified
  • Official OOF Product
OOF is a methodological authority, not a certification body.

Claims represent responsibility, not approval.

Responsibility

The entity using the claim is fully responsible for:
  • accuracy of the claim
  • real-world product behavior
  • alignment with module conditions
False or misleading use results in:
  • immediate loss of compatibility
  • reputational risk
  • full accountability of the entity

System Principle

Use of the system is open.
Use of the claim requires truth.

Consumer Meaning

A product marked as:

OOF Aligned — SLIM

means:
  • the label can be removed easily
  • the surface remains intact
  • no residue remains
  • no chemicals are required
  • the product remains unchanged after label removal

Why This Matters

Consumers should not need to:
  • repair a product after purchase
  • use chemicals to remove labels
  • accept damage caused by identification
This module ensures clean removal, preserved product value, safe user experience, and real usability
and reuse.

Core Insight

Identification must not damage what it identifies.

Final Statement

Short claim on product.
Full responsibility behind it.

OOF System Rules (Global)

Rule 1 — OOF First

All public claims must start with OOF.

Correct: OOF Aligned — SLIM
Incorrect: SLIM Compliant

Rule 2 — No False Authority

OOF must not appear as a certifier.

Not allowed:
  • OOF Certified
  • OOF Approved
 Rule 3 — Responsibility Over Verification

Claims represent accountability, not validation.

Aligned indicates responsibility.
Validated indicates an optional higher verification layer.

Rule 4 — Truth-Based Usage

A claim may only exist if conditions are real.

If conditions are not met, the claim must be removed immediately.

Rule 5 — Standard and Authority Separation

Standards define structure.
OOF defines authority.

Rule 6 — Module Clarity

The claim must reflect the specific module used.

Correct: OOF Aligned — SLIM
Incorrect: OOF Aligned (without module context)
