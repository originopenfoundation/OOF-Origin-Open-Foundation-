Structural Dependency Integrity

Canonical Definition

Structural Dependency Integrity Module (SDIM) defines the governance
conditions required to identify, validate, monitor, and preserve the integrity
of dependencies between structural components. It ensures that every
dependency remains legitimate, transparent, resilient, and governable
throughout the complete lifecycle of a governed entity.

Operational Role

SDIM governs the dependency layer of structural integrity. It ensures that
every governed dependency is explicitly identified, continuously validated,
and managed so that changes or failures in one component do not compromise the
integrity of the overall structure.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Structural Dependencies

Identify every dependency between structural components, including functional,
operational, organizational, technical, governance, and external dependencies
that influence structural integrity.

2. Define Dependency Integrity Requirements

Establish the governance conditions that every dependency must satisfy,
including legitimacy, availability, consistency, traceability, resilience, and
continuity.

3. Define Dependency Failure Logic

Identify the conditions under which a dependency becomes unavailable, invalid,
inconsistent, broken, unauthorized, obsolete, or otherwise incapable of
supporting structural integrity.

4. Define Governance Response

Define governance actions for validating, replacing, mitigating, removing,
restructuring, or approving dependency changes whenever dependency integrity
is compromised.

5. Preserve Dependency Records

Preserve dependency maps, governance approvals, dependency changes, validation
history, supporting evidence, and lifecycle records necessary to demonstrate
dependency integrity.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Financial Services Platform

Scenario

A financial institution operates multiple interconnected services, including
customer identity, transaction processing, fraud detection, and regulatory
reporting, all of which depend on one another.

Application

SDIM identifies and governs every dependency between these services, ensuring
that changes to one service do not unintentionally compromise the structural
integrity of the entire platform.

Result

The institution maintains resilient and transparent service dependencies,
reducing operational risk while preserving structural integrity across the
complete financial ecosystem.

Use Case 2 — Autonomous Multi-Agent System

Scenario

A fleet of autonomous agents depends on shared memory services, identity
infrastructure, orchestration components, external sensors, and governance
policies to coordinate operations.

Application

SDIM maps and continuously validates every operational and governance
dependency, ensuring that dependency failures are detected and managed before
they affect the integrity of the system.

Result

The autonomous ecosystem maintains dependable structural relationships,
allowing agents to operate safely and consistently despite changes in
individual components or supporting infrastructure.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity depends not only on components and relationships, but also on the
dependencies that connect them. Structural Dependency Integrity Module ensures
that every governed dependency remains visible, resilient, and continuously
governable, preserving the structural integrity of the complete governed
entity throughout its lifecycle.
