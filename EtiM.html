Canonical Definition System

Canonical Definition

Escalation Timing Module (EtiM) defines the structural conditions under which
AI behavioral escalation occurs at the appropriate moment according to
predefined governance thresholds, operational conditions, behavioral risk
levels, confidence limits, and escalation policies throughout the complete
behavioral lifecycle.

ETM governs escalation timing.

The module establishes the foundational conditions required to ensure that
escalation occurs neither too early nor too late, preserving operational
effectiveness while preventing unnecessary risk or delayed intervention.

Correct escalation is not only about whether escalation occurs.

It is also about when escalation occurs.

ETM governs that timing.

Module Operational Role

ETM defines the escalation-timing layer of BES.

Its role is to preserve the correct timing of AI behavioral escalation.

Module Operational Space

ETM governs:

  • escalation timing
  • escalation scheduling
  • intervention timing
  • governance timing thresholds
  • behavioral timing evaluation
  • operational response timing
  • escalation sequencing
  • escalation timing validation


The module applies wherever AI behavioral escalation depends on appropriate
timing.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • timely escalation
  • governance-valid intervention
  • operationally appropriate escalation
  • traceable escalation timing
  • behavioral timing consistency
  • trustworthy escalation execution


Its function is to ensure that escalation occurs at the optimal governance
moment based on predefined operational conditions.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Escalation Timing Conditions

The organization must define when escalation should occur.

2. Define Timing Thresholds

The system must define measurable timing criteria for escalation.

3. Define Timing Evaluation Logic

The system must continuously evaluate whether escalation timing conditions
have been reached.

4. Define Governance Response

The organization must specify governance actions for early, delayed, or
correctly timed escalation.

5. Preserve Traceability

The system must preserve complete traceability of:

  • escalation timing decisions,
  • trigger timestamps,
  • governance evaluations,
  • intervention timing,
  • resulting behavioral states.


Use Case 1 — Autonomous Vehicle

Scenario

An autonomous vehicle detects deteriorating weather conditions and escalating
uncertainty.

Application

ETM governs the exact point at which vehicle control is escalated to the human
driver or remote operations center.

Result

The organization reduces safety risk, improves operational trust, and
preserves governance integrity.

Use Case 2 — AI Clinical Decision Support

Scenario

A clinical AI identifies a rapidly deteriorating patient while confidence in
treatment recommendations decreases.

Application

ETM governs the precise timing for escalating the case to a physician before
patient safety is compromised.

Result

The healthcare organization improves patient safety, reduces clinical risk,
and strengthens Human-AI governance.

Canonical Closing Statement

Escalation Timing Module (EtiM) defines the structural conditions under which
AI behavioral escalation occurs at the appropriate moment according to
predefined governance thresholds, operational conditions, behavioral risk
levels, confidence limits, and escalation policies throughout the complete
behavioral lifecycle.

A correct escalation delivered too late may become ineffective. Escalation
Timing therefore becomes a foundational operational condition of Behavioral
Escalation Standard within AI Governance Architecture (AIG®).
