Canonical Definition System

Canonical Definition

Authority Delegation Module (ADM) defines the structural conditions under which
governance authority delegations remain legitimate, attributable, traceable, reviewable,
governable, enforceable, auditable, and operationally valid throughout the governance lifecycle.

ADM governs governance authority delegation.

The module establishes the foundational conditions required to determine how governance authority is delegated,
who received delegated authority, whether delegated authority remains legitimate, and whether
authority-delegation relationships remain reconstructable throughout operational reality.

Governance authority may originate from one governance actor.

Governance authority may be exercised by another.

ADM governs that delegation.

Module Operational Role

ADM defines the authority-delegation governance layer of GAS.

Its role is to establish governance-valid authority transfer relationships throughout governance environments.

Module Operational Space

ADM governs:

  • governance authority delegation
  • delegated authority relationships
  • authority-transfer traceability
  • delegated authority legitimacy
  • authority delegation validation
  • authority delegation governance
  • authority delegation auditability
  • authority delegation reconstruction


The module applies wherever governance authority may be delegated between governance actors.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • legitimate authority delegations
  • traceable authority transfers
  • reconstructable delegation history
  • attributable delegated authority
  • governance-valid delegation records
  • operational delegation visibility


Its function is to ensure that governance can reconstruct how authority moved throughout governance environments.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Authority Delegation Object

The organization must define which governance environments require authority-delegation governance.
This may include:

  • organizations
  • institutions
  • governments
  • executive structures
  • governance committees
  • AI governance systems
  • autonomous-agent ecosystems
  • Human-AI governance environments


2. Define Authority Delegation Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which governance authority delegations remain valid.
This includes:

  • delegation requirements
  • legitimacy requirements
  • authorization requirements
  • validation requirements
  • traceability requirements
  • governance-valid delegation conditions


3. Define Delegation Degradation Detection Logic

the system must define how authority-delegation failures are identified.
This may include:

  • unauthorized delegations
  • undocumented authority transfers
  • illegitimate delegation activities
  • hidden delegated authority
  • unverifiable delegation records
  • governance-invalid delegation relationships


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for authority-delegation failures.
Governance response may include:

  • delegation review
  • delegation validation
  • governance intervention
  • corrective actions
  • delegation reconstruction
  • authority verification
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • authority delegations
  • delegated authority relationships
  • governance reviews
  • intervention procedures
  • validation activities
  • resulting delegation states


A governance environment must not remain delegation-valid if materially significant authority delegations
cannot be reconstructed, reviewed, validated, enforced, preserved, or governed.

Use Case 1 — Executive Governance Environment

Scenario

A senior governance actor delegates governance authority to subordinate governance actors responsible for operational decision-making.

Application

ADM governs delegated authority relationships, delegation legitimacy, and authority-transfer traceability.

Result

Governance gains visibility into how authority was delegated, who received authority, and
whether delegation remains governance-valid.

Use Case 2 — Human-AI Governance Environment

Scenario

Human governance actors delegate limited governance authority to AI systems operating within predefined governance boundaries.

Application

ADM governs delegated authority ownership, delegation traceability, validation requirements, and governance legitimacy.

Result

Governance gains visibility into authority delegation across Human-AI operational ecosystems.
