Governed Space

Evidence Correlation

Core Question

How can independently governed evidence be accurately related, connected, and
analyzed to reconstruct operational reality without compromising its
integrity?

Canonical Definition

Evidence Correlation Standard defines the governance conditions required to
establish, validate, manage, and preserve relationships between multiple
governed evidence objects. It establishes evidence correlation principles,
relationship rules, dependency validation, contextual linkage, correlation
governance, and continuous correlation assurance necessary to reconstruct
operational reality from trustworthy evidence throughout the complete
lifecycle of a governed entity.

Governance Flow Position

Audit Observation → Evidence Formation → Evidence Integrity → Evidence
Correlation → Evidence Verification → Audit Execution → Audit Findings → Audit
Response → Audit Preservation → Audit Assurance

Standard Abstract

Individual evidence objects rarely provide a complete understanding of
operational reality.

Reliable audit requires multiple evidence objects to be accurately connected
through governed relationships, contextual dependencies, chronological
sequences, operational links, and causal associations.

Evidence Correlation Standard transforms individually trustworthy evidence
into a coherent and governable representation of operational reality without
modifying the evidence itself.

Operational Role

Evidence Correlation Standard governs the relationship layer of evidence
governance by establishing how independently governed evidence is connected,
contextualized, sequenced, and analyzed before verification and audit
execution.

Operational Space

Evidence Correlation Standard governs:

  • evidence relationships
  • evidence linkage
  • contextual association
  • chronological correlation
  • dependency mapping
  • causal relationships
  • evidence network integrity.


Why This Standard Exists

Trustworthy evidence may still fail to explain what actually happened if
individual evidence objects remain isolated.

Organizations require a standardized methodology for connecting evidence into
a coherent operational narrative while preserving the integrity of every
individual evidence object.

Evidence Correlation Standard exists because Evidence Correlation represents
the fourth governed space of ADIT® — Continuous Audit & Evidence Governance
Architecture™, enabling reliable reconstruction of operational reality before
evidence verification begins.
