Canonical Definition

Responsibility Traceability Accountability Integrity Module (RTAIM)
defines the structural conditions under which responsibility-history
relationships, responsibility chains, responsibility transitions,
reconstruction activities, traceability decisions, traceability
validations, responsibility outcomes, and responsibility-governance
activities remain attributable, accountable, traceable, governable,
reconstructable, enforceable, and operationally valid throughout
the responsibility lifecycle.

RTAIM governs responsibility-traceability accountability.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine who remained accountable for preserving responsibility
traceability, who approved traceability decisions, who governed
reconstruction activities, and who remains accountable when
responsibility-traceability failures occur.

Module Operational Role

RTAIM defines the traceability-accountability governance layer
of RTS.

Its role is to ensure that responsibility traceability remains
connected to accountable governance actors throughout
the responsibility lifecycle.

Module Operational Space

RTAIM governs:

  • traceability accountability
  • reconstruction accountability
  • responsibility-history accountability
  • traceability-decision accountability
  • governance accountability
  • responsibility-chain accountability
  • traceability-failure accountability
  • accountability attribution


The module applies wherever governance must determine accountability
arising from responsibility traceability.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • accountable traceability structures
  • attributable reconstruction decisions
  • reconstructable accountability history
  • traceable governance actions
  • governance-valid accountability relationships
  • operational traceability enforcement


Its function is to ensure that governance can determine who remains
accountable for preserving responsibility traceability
and reconstruction capability.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Traceability Accountability Object

The organization must define which traceability environments
require accountability governance.

This may include:

  • organizational structures
  • contractor networks
  • governance frameworks
  • compliance systems
  • audit environments
  • investigation environments
  • AI governance systems
  • Human-AI operational environments


2. Define Traceability Accountability Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which traceability
accountability remains valid.

This includes:

  • attribution requirements
  • accountability requirements
  • enforcement requirements
  • consequence requirements
  • governance requirements
  • accountability-valid traceability conditions


3. Define Accountability Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how traceability-accountability failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • anonymous reconstruction decisions
  • undocumented traceability approvals
  • accountability gaps
  • responsibility-history ownership ambiguity
  • governance-blind traceability activities
  • unverifiable traceability accountability


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic
for traceability-accountability failures.

Governance response may include:

  • accountability review
  • attribution verification
  • governance intervention
  • corrective actions
  • traceability reassessment
  • enforcement procedures
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • reconstruction decisions
  • traceability approvals
  • governance reviews
  • accountability relationships
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting traceability outcomes


A responsibility-traceability environment must not remain
accountability-valid if materially significant traceability
activities cannot be attributed, reconstructed, reviewed, enforced,
validated, or governed.

for responsibility-traceability failures.

Result

responsibility-traceability governance across
