Evidence Integrity Validation

Canonical Definition

Evidence Integrity Validation Module defines the governance framework for
continuously validating that governed evidence maintains its required
integrity throughout its complete lifecycle. It establishes integrity
validation principles, verification requirements, validation controls,
governance mechanisms, and continuous assurance conditions necessary to
confirm that evidence remains authentic, complete, consistent, protected, and
suitable for audit, operational reconstruction, and independent verification.

Operational Role

Evidence Integrity Validation Module governs the continuous validation layer
of evidence integrity by verifying that governed evidence satisfies all
established integrity requirements before it proceeds to evidence correlation
and subsequent audit activities.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Integrity Validation Requirements

Identify integrity criteria, validation objectives, governance rules,
acceptance thresholds, responsible authorities, and operational requirements.

2. Define Validation Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for validating evidence authenticity,
completeness, preservation, consistency, protection, and overall integrity.

3. Define Validation Logic

Define how evidence integrity is evaluated using objective validation
criteria, integrity controls, governance requirements, and documented
validation outcomes.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for failed integrity validation, detected
integrity violations, unresolved deficiencies, corrective actions, escalation
procedures, and revalidation requirements.

5. Preserve Validation Records

Maintain validation history, integrity assessments, governance decisions,
corrective actions, approvals, timestamps, and supporting documentation
throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

Before governed evidence is used for autonomous operational review, the
organization validates that all integrity requirements remain satisfied.

Application

Evidence Integrity Validation Module performs continuous integrity validation,
confirming authenticity, completeness, preservation, consistency, and
protection before evidence progresses further within ADIT®.

Result

Only evidence with validated integrity is accepted for correlation, audit, and
independent verification.

Use Case 2 — Regulatory Audit

Scenario

A regulatory authority requires confirmation that submitted evidence has
maintained its integrity throughout the complete retention period.

Application

Evidence Integrity Validation Module verifies compliance with all integrity
requirements and documents the validation results before evidence is accepted
for regulatory review.

Result

Regulators receive objectively validated evidence with demonstrable integrity
suitable for audit and legal proceedings.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Integrity Validation Module establishes the canonical governance
framework for continuously validating the integrity of governed evidence
throughout its complete lifecycle. By governing integrity verification,
validation controls, governance responses, and continuous assurance, the
module ensures that only evidence with demonstrably preserved integrity is
used for audit, operational reconstruction, accountable governance, and
independent verification across all operational domains.
