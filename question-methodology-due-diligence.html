Methodology Due Diligence™ Question Index

OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™)

Understanding Methodologies Before Investing, Licensing, Acquiring,
Implementing, or Scaling Them

Most organizations evaluate products, technologies, vendors,
companies, and investments.

Far fewer evaluate the methodologies behind them.

Methodology Due Diligence™ exists to help organizations understand a
methodology before making strategic, operational, governance,
investment, licensing, acquisition, implementation, or
commercialization decisions.

This Question Index™ provides a structured collection of questions
designed to support methodology evaluation, investigation, analysis,
and intelligence gathering.

The objective is not to immediately provide answers.

The objective is to ensure the right questions are being asked.

Methodology Existence

Does a methodology actually exist? Is the methodology documented?
Can the methodology be demonstrated? Is the methodology repeatable?
Is the methodology independent from specific individuals? Is the
methodology clearly defined? Is the methodology operational or
theoretical? Can the methodology be applied consistently? Has the
methodology been formally structured? Is there evidence supporting
its existence?

Methodology Ownership

Who created the methodology?

Who owns the methodology? Is ownership clearly documented? Has
ownership changed over time? Are ownership rights disputed? Can
ownership be verified? Are there multiple ownership claims? Does the
methodology contain third-party components? Are licensing rights
clearly defined? Can ownership continuity be demonstrated?

Methodology Integrity

Is the methodology internally consistent? Are core principles
clearly defined? Are there contradictions within the methodology?
Can methodology outputs be reproduced? Are methodology boundaries
clearly established? Is the methodology traceable? Does the
methodology maintain structural integrity? Can methodology decisions
be explained? Is the methodology auditable? Are methodology
assumptions clearly identified?

Methodology Maturity

How mature is the methodology? Has the methodology evolved over
time? Are historical versions available? Has the methodology been
tested? Has the methodology been implemented successfully? Can
maturity be demonstrated through evidence? Has the methodology been
independently reviewed? Does the methodology have documented use
cases? Has the methodology been refined through operational
experience? Is the methodology suitable for scaling?

Methodology Validation

Has the methodology been validated? What evidence supports the
methodology? Can outcomes be independently verified? Has the
methodology been externally reviewed? Are validation criteria
clearly defined? Are validation results documented? Can validation
processes be reproduced? Are validation limitations known? Does the
methodology rely on assumptions or evidence? Is validation ongoing?

Methodology Scalability

Can the methodology scale? Can it be applied across industries? Can
it be applied internationally? Can it support large organizations?

Can it support complex environments? Does scalability create new
risks? Can the methodology operate across different technologies?
Can the methodology adapt over time? Are scalability limitations
documented? Has scalability been demonstrated?

Methodology Risk

What risks are associated with the methodology? Are methodology
dependencies known? Does the methodology rely on key individuals?
Are operational risks documented? Are governance risks understood?
Could the methodology fail under certain conditions? Are failure
scenarios identified? Are limitations openly acknowledged? Does the
methodology create unintended consequences? How resilient is
the methodology?

Methodology Commercialization

Can the methodology be licensed? Can the methodology be
commercialized? Does the methodology create measurable value? Is
there market demand for the methodology? Can the methodology support
multiple use cases? Can it be integrated into existing environments?
Does the methodology have competitive advantages? Is differentiation
clearly defined? Can value creation be demonstrated? Is
commercialization realistic?

Methodology Governance

Who governs the methodology? How are methodology changes managed? Is
version control maintained? Are governance processes documented? How
are disputes resolved? Is methodology continuity protected? Can
governance decisions be audited? Are governance responsibilities
defined? Is methodology stewardship established? Can governance
integrity be demonstrated?

Methodology Intelligence

What problem does the methodology solve? Why was the methodology
created? What makes the methodology different? What assumptions does
the methodology rely upon? Which environments benefit most from the
methodology? Which environments may not benefit? What
dependencies exist?

What opportunities does the methodology create? What future
evolution pathways exist? Is the methodology ready for
long-term adoption?

Related OOF® Service

Methodology Due Diligence™

Methodology Due Diligence™ helps organizations assess methodology

investing, licensing, implementing, acquiring, or scaling
methodological assets.

About the OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™

The OOF Problem Space Intelligence Index™ (PSII™) is a growing
collection of structured questions designed to help identify,
understand, classify, and investigate problems before searching
for solutions.

Questions are organized into categories, clusters, and problem
environments to improve clarity, analysis, governance,
and decision-making.

The objective is not merely to provide answers.

The objective is to ensure that the right questions are being asked.
