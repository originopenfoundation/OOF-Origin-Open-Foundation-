Canonical Definition

Authority Escalation Criteria Integrity Module (AECIM) defines the
structural conditions under which the criteria, thresholds,
triggers, requirements, rules, risk conditions, governance
boundaries, and operational circumstances used to initiate
escalation remain identifiable, traceable, governable, accountable,
consistent, and operationally valid throughout the
escalation lifecycle.

AECIM governs escalation criteria.

The module establishes the integrity conditions required to
determine why escalation was required and whether the escalation
decision was based upon legitimate and governable conditions.

Module Operational Role

AECIM defines the escalation-criteria governance layer of AES.

Its role is to preserve visibility of the conditions that
trigger authority escalation.

Module Operational Space

AECIM governs:

  • escalation criteria
  • escalation thresholds
  • escalation requirements
  • escalation triggers
  • risk-based escalation
  • governance-boundary escalation
  • escalation rules
  • criteria accountability


The module applies wherever governance requires justification
for escalation decisions.

Module Function

The module applies wherever systems must preserve:

  • identifiable escalation criteria
  • traceable escalation requirements
  • reconstructable escalation decisions
  • accountable escalation thresholds
  • governance-valid escalation triggers
  • operationally valid escalation conditions


Its function is to ensure that governance can determine why
escalation occurred and whether escalation was justified.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define the Escalation Criteria Object

The organization must define which escalation environments
require criteria governance.

This may include:

  • management escalation systems
  • compliance escalation environments
  • incident-management systems
  • contractor governance structures
  • safety escalation frameworks
  • AI governance systems
  • autonomous-agent environments
  • Human-AI operational systems


2. Define Escalation Criteria Conditions

The system must define the conditions under which escalation
criteria remain valid.

This includes:

  • threshold requirements
  • trigger requirements
  • risk requirements
  • accountability requirements
  • governance requirements
  • escalation-valid criteria conditions


3. Define Criteria Degradation Detection Logic

The system must define how escalation-criteria failures
are identified.

This may include:

  • undefined escalation triggers
  • inconsistent escalation thresholds
  • undocumented escalation criteria
  • governance-blind escalation conditions
  • obsolete escalation rules
  • unjustified escalation decisions


4. Define Operational Response or Governance Logic

The system must define governance logic for
escalation-criteria failures.

Governance response may include:

  • criteria review
  • threshold verification
  • governance intervention
  • escalation-path review
  • corrective actions
  • criteria revision
  • operational invalidation where required


5. Preserve Traceability & Restrict Invalid Conditions

The system must preserve reconstructable traceability of:

  • escalation criteria
  • escalation thresholds
  • governance reviews
  • validation activities
  • intervention procedures
  • resulting escalation states


An authority-escalation environment must not remain criteria-valid
if materially significant escalation criteria cannot be
reconstructed, reviewed, validated, or governed.

Use Case 1 — Safety Escalation Environment

Scenario

A supervisor encounters a safety incident exceeding predefined
operational risk thresholds.

Application

AECIM governs the criteria used to determine whether escalation
is required.

Result

Governance gains visibility into why escalation occurred and whether
escalation criteria remained valid.

Use Case 2 — AI Governance Environment

Scenario

An AI agent encounters a decision exceeding predefined authority
limits and risk thresholds.

Application

AECIM governs the criteria that trigger escalation to a
supervisory authority.

Result

Governance gains visibility into the conditions that initiated
escalation across intelligent operational systems.

Canonical Closing Statement

Authority Escalation Criteria Integrity Module (AECIM) defines the
structural conditions under which the criteria, thresholds,
triggers, requirements, rules, risk conditions, governance
boundaries, and operational circumstances used to initiate

Escalation should never occur arbitrarily. Authority escalation

trustworthy escalation governance.
