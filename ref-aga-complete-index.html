Accountability Governance Architecture

Official Accountability Governance Map for Organizations,
Institutions, Governments, Autonomous Systems and Human-AI
Operational Environments This document serves as the official OOF®
accountability governance map and structural index used for
accountability space identification, accountability governance
classification, accountability diagnostics, accountability
architecture navigation, accountability investigation, and
accountability problem placement across organizational,
institutional, autonomous, and intelligent systems.

AGA™ consists of 17 Parent Standards , 85 Core Modules , and 1
Protocol Layer . Additional standards, modules, and protocols may be
added when new governable accountability spaces or
accountability-navigation needs are identified. However, the
standards, modules, and protocol listed below form the core
structural backbone of AGA™ 1.0.

AGA™ Protocol Layer

CAP™ — Chain Accountability Protocol

Type: Governance Investigation & Accountability Navigation Protocol

Core Question: How can accountability be reconstructed across a
complete governance chain?

Canonical Definition

CAP™ defines a structured governance investigation and
accountability navigation protocol used to identify, validate,
reconstruct, analyze, and govern accountability relationships across
complete governance chains. CAP™ serves as the operational
navigation protocol of Accountability Governance Architecture
(AGA™). CAP™ transforms accountability investigation into a
structured and repeatable governance process capable of identifying
relationships, authority structures, responsibility structures,
capability conditions, control environments, oversight obligations,
evidence environments, accountability ownership, decision
accountability, accountability continuity, and accountability chains
across complex governance systems.

Architectural Position

AGA™ governs the complete accountability lifecycle. The
architecture progresses:

  • Relationship → Authority → Responsibility → Capability → Control → Oversight → Evidence → Accountability →
  • Decision Accountability → Accountability Continuity → Accountability Chain


CAP™ operates as the protocol layer that navigates across these
spaces. Together these standards and CAP™ govern how accountability
remains identifiable, attributable, reconstructable, enforceable,
reviewable, and governable throughout operational reality.

Canonical Principle

AGA™ does not govern what a system does. AGA™ governs how
accountability itself remains identifiable, attributable,
reconstructable, enforceable, reviewable, and governable throughout
operational reality.

Canonical Closing Statement

AGA™ serves as the official OOF® accountability governance map for
organizations, institutions, governments, autonomous systems,
contractor ecosystems, and Human-AI operational environments. By
defining the primary governable accountability spaces of operational
reality and providing CAP™ as its operational navigation protocol,
AGA™ provides a structured accountability reference architecture
used to identify, classify, investigate, govern, validate,
reconstruct, and understand accountability throughout the complete
accountability lifecycle.
