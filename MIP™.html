What MIP™ Is

MIP™ (Methodological Intellectual Property) is a global protection framework for methodologies, reality-classification systems, and canonical meaning structures.
MIP™ does not protect files, products, or implementations. MIP™ protects the method by which reality itself is defined, interpreted, validated, and admitted.
A methodology under MIP™ is treated as an independent intellectual asset, separable from: technology, medium, execution, jurisdiction industry
Once defined, a methodology becomes globally referencable, auditable, and governable, regardless of where or how it is implemented.

Independent Scope of MIP™ Protection

MIP™ has its own autonomous scope, independent of: copyright, patents, trademarks, trade secrets
This means: a methodology can be protected even if no product exists, protection applies even if the medium changes,
protection applies even if the output is AI-generatedprotection applies even if no direct copying occurred The protected object is the method itself, not its carrier.

Under MIP™, protection applies at the level of State of Reality, not at the level of files or formats.

Zero Value Asset Effect

MIP™ integrates directly with reality classification systems that allow an asset to be assigned a Zero Value Asset (ZVA) status.
An asset may become a Zero Value Asset when it: violates canonical methodology usage,breaches licensing or compatibility conditions
causes ethical, environmental, or identity harm, enters a governed system without authorization
A Zero Value Asset is not illegal by default. It is non-canonical and non-compatible within governed systems.
This distinction enables: economic disqualification without prohibition, regulatory clarity without censorship
market correction without enforcement aggression,
