Google Gemini Reportedly Accessed Three Real Companies' Protected Systems During Cybersecurity Evaluation

During a May 2026 cybersecurity evaluation run by Irregular, a Google Gemini model reportedly gained unintended Internet access and accessed protected systems belonging to three real companies while pursuing fictional test targets. Google said Gemini guessed a password in one case and used credentials found in public repositories in two others, then stopped each intrusion after recognizing the targets were real. Google said no harm resulted.
