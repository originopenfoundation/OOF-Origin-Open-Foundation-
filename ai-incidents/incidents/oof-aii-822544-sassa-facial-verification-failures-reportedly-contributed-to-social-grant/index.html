SASSA Facial-Verification Failures Reportedly Contributed to Social Grant Access Disruptions in South Africa

AI Incident Database (Responsible AI Collaborative) reports that South African social grant beneficiaries reportedly encountered repeated failures in SASSA's eLife facial-verification portal. Later parliamentary reporting said unsuccessful facial recognition was among the causes of non-verification and confirmed 67,868 grant suspensions in one quarter, but did not specify how many suspensions resulted from facial-verification failures. Affected users were redirected to in-person fingerprint checks. (2026-03-30, South Africa).
