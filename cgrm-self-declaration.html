CGRM — Self-Declaration

Declaration Statement

We declare that our system operates under defined crisis governance
conditions where responsibility, authority, and decision-making remain
clear, traceable, and enforceable.

We accept full responsibility for this declaration.

Core Compliance Checklist

1. Authority & Responsibility
  • ☐ Decision Authority is defined
  • ☐ Execution Authority is defined
  • ☐ Responsibility Holder is defined
  • ☐ Responsibility is identity-bound and non-anonymous
 2. Decision Control
  • ☐ Decision levels (Operational / Critical / Crisis) are defined
  • ☐ Authority scope and decision limits are defined
  • ☐ Approval conditions are established
 3. Escalation & Override
  • ☐ Escalation triggers are defined
  • ☐ Authority transfer rules are defined
  • ☐ Maximum response time is defined
  • ☐ Fallback authority exists
  • ☐ Human override exists (if AI is used)
 4. Traceability & Audit
  • ☐ All decisions are recorded
  • ☐ Decisions are time-stamped
  • ☐ Decisions are attributable
  • ☐ Decision history is reconstructable
 5. Integrity & Enforcement
  • ☐ Governance rules are enforceable
  • ☐ Violations are detectable
  • ☐ System integrity is observable
  • ☐ Breach conditions are defined
  • ☐ Response mechanisms exist

AI Conditions (if applicable)

  • ☐ Human accountability is defined
  • ☐ AI is not a responsibility holder
  • ☐ Decisions are traceable
  • ☐ Override is enforceable

Operational Condition

  • ☐ Governance operates continuously in real time during crisis conditions
  • ☐ All core conditions are active simultaneously

Compliance Statement

We confirm that all required governance conditions are implemented and
actively maintained.

We understand that:
  • this declaration is publicly attributable
  • responsibility remains with the declaring entity
  • compliance is continuous
  • compliance is verifiable
  • compliance may be challenged at any time
