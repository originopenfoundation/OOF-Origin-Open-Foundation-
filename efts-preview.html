Category: Economic & Value Standards
Subcategory: Energy Value Attribution


Electricity today is accounted for through certificates, averages,
and administrative claims that do not reflect real system behavior.

EFTS™ defines a flow-based methodology where energy origin, value,
and carbon impact are derived from real-time physical conditions.

The standard removes the separation between what is consumed
and what is declared.

Energy is attributed based on actual production, cross-border flows,
temporal variation, and system state.

This enables differentiation between energy sources in real conditions,
including day, night, and peak load scenarios.

EFTS™ transforms energy from a generic commodity into a traceable value flow.

It establishes a truth layer for energy systems,
where claims must match measurable reality.

Without flow-based attribution, energy cannot be considered
validly valued or reported.
