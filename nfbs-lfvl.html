Compatibility: Origin Eco™ · Product Integrity Standards · Natural Systems


Canonical Definition

Lactic Fermented Vegetable Liquid defines a fermented beverage
produced through natural lactic acid fermentation of plant material
in a water-based system.

The liquid is valid only if it is biologically transformed through
fermentation, resulting in a stable, acidic, and safe consumable
system.

A. Module Abstract

LFVL™ defines the minimum conditions required for a liquid to be
classified as a true fermented vegetable beverage.

It distinguishes real fermentation from flavored or artificially
modified liquids.

B. Core Principle

Fermentation must transform the liquid into a new biological
system.

C. Core Conditions

1. Biological Transformation
The liquid must result from active lactic fermentation.

2. Acidity Threshold
The liquid must reach:

pH ≤ 4.2

 This ensures microbial safety and fermentation validity.

3. Non-Simulation Condition
Artificial acidification or chemical simulation is not permitted.

4. Safety Condition
The product must be safe for consumption and free from harmful
contamination.

5. Stability Condition
The liquid must remain stable within a defined consumption window.

D. Prohibited Condition

The product is invalid if:
  • fermentation does not occur
  • acidity threshold is not reached
  • artificial acids replace fermentation
  • contamination is present
  • spoilage characteristics appear


E. Scope

Applies to:
  • fermented vegetable liquids
  • plant-based lactic fermentation systems
  • non-alcoholic fermented beverages


F. MIF — Minimum Implementation Framework

A system producing LFVL™ must:
  • use clean water and plant material
  • enable natural lactic fermentation conditions
  • maintain controlled environment (temperature, oxygen
    limitation)
  • monitor fermentation progression
  • verify acidity threshold (pH ≤ 4.2)
  • ensure product safety and stability
  • prevent contamination and process deviation


G. System Output

A valid LFVL™ system produces:
  • biologically transformed liquid
  • measurable acidity
  • stable and safe fermented beverage
  • non-simulated fermentation profile
