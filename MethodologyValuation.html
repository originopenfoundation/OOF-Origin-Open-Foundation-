Methodology Valuation™

Understanding the Strategic, Operational, Intellectual, and
Economic Value of Methodologies

What Is Methodology Valuation™?

Methodology Valuation™ is a professional assessment service designed to estimate
the strategic, operational, intellectual, governance, and economic value of methodologies.

Organizations routinely evaluate:
  • companies
  • patents
  • trademarks
  • technologies
  • financial assets
  • real estate
However, methodologies increasingly influence:
  • governance
  • artificial intelligence
  • operations
  • compliance
  • decision-making
  • organizational performance
while often remaining unevaluated as assets.

Methodology Valuation™ was created to address this challenge.

Why This Service Exists

Many methodologies create value.

However, organizations often struggle to answer:
  • How valuable is the methodology?
  • What strategic significance does it possess?
  • What future opportunities exist?
  • What risks remain?
  • Can the methodology support investment decisions?
  • Can the methodology support licensing decisions?
  • Can the methodology support acquisition decisions?
Methodology Valuation™ exists to provide a structured answer to these questions.

What We Assess

Methodology Valuation™ evaluates methodologies as assets.

Depending on scope, assessments may include:
  • Verified Methodology Value™
    Value already demonstrated through implementation,
    operational results, adoption, licensing, and measurable outcomes.
  • Estimated Methodology Value™
    Value estimated through future opportunities, ecosystem
    potential, strategic significance, scalability, and expansion capability.
  • Strategic Value™
    Long-term significance and positioning.
  • Operational Value™
    Practical usefulness within operational environments.
  • Governance Value™
    Contribution to governance, accountability, auditability, and decision-making.
  • Intellectual Asset Value™
    Value created through structure, originality, knowledge
    organization, and methodology architecture.
