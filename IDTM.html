Governed Space

Integrity Deviation Tracking

Canonical Definition

Integrity Deviation Tracking Module (IDTM) defines the governance framework
for continuously tracking integrity deviations throughout their lifecycle. It
establishes the tracking mechanisms, status management, progress monitoring,
historical recording, and governance controls required to ensure that every
detected integrity deviation remains visible, traceable, and properly managed
until resolution.

Operational Role

IDTM governs the lifecycle tracking layer of integrity deviation management by
maintaining continuous visibility into the status, progression, ownership, and
resolution of every integrity deviation.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Tracking Scope

Identify which integrity deviations require continuous tracking, lifecycle
monitoring, ownership assignment, and governance supervision.

2. Define Tracking Requirements

Establish tracking identifiers, lifecycle statuses, ownership rules, progress
monitoring requirements, reporting intervals, and governance responsibilities.

3. Define Tracking Validation Logic

Define how tracking records are verified for accuracy, completeness,
timeliness, consistency, and compliance with the approved governance
methodology.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for overdue deviations, unresolved deviations,
repeated deviations, ownership changes, escalation events, and closure
approvals.

5. Preserve Tracking Records

Maintain tracking histories, lifecycle status changes, ownership records,
governance actions, supporting evidence, closure documentation, and audit
records throughout the complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

An AI governance platform detects several integrity deviations requiring
investigation, corrective action, and ongoing monitoring.

Application

IDTM assigns each deviation a unique tracking record, monitors its lifecycle,
records governance actions, and verifies final resolution.

Result

Every integrity deviation remains fully traceable from initial detection
through final closure, ensuring complete governance accountability.

Use Case 2 — Critical Infrastructure Operations

Scenario

A power grid operator manages multiple integrity deviations affecting
operational reliability across different facilities.

Application

IDTM tracks each deviation through investigation, corrective actions,
verification, and closure while maintaining complete governance history.

Result

Deviation management becomes transparent, auditable, and continuously governed
across the operational lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Integrity deviations must remain governed until they are fully resolved.
Integrity Deviation Tracking Module establishes the canonical governance
framework for continuously tracking integrity deviations, ensuring complete
visibility, accountability, traceability, and lifecycle governance throughout
the complete lifecycle of every governed entity.
