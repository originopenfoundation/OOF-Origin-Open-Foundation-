Canonical Definition

Energy Flow Truth Standard (EFTS™) defines the mandatory conditions
under which electrical energy must be attributed, measured, validated,
and valued based on its real-time physical flow, source origin,
temporal state, and consumption context.

Energy cannot be considered validly accounted for unless its value,
environmental impact, and origin are bound to verifiable physical
flow conditions at the moment of use.

EFTS™ operates within a Structured Reality™ framework, where energy value
is derived from actual system behavior, not from administrative
or statistical abstractions.

A. Standard Abstract

EFTS™ establishes a unified methodology for transforming electricity
accounting from statistical approximation and certificate-based claims
into real-time, flow-based, verifiable truth.

The standard eliminates the separation between:

  • physical electricity flow
  • declared origin
  • environmental impact
  • economic value

All energy valuation, carbon attribution, and reporting must reflect
actual system conditions.

B. Core Principle

Electricity behaves as a continuously moving, mixed-flow system.

Energy value, origin, and impact must be derived from real-time physical
flow conditions, not from detached market instruments, annual averages,
or certificate abstractions.

C. Scope

This standard applies to:

  • national and cross-border electricity systems
  • transmission and grid operators
  • energy producers (all source types)
  • energy markets and exchanges
  • industrial and institutional consumers
  • AI and compute infrastructure dependent on energy sourcing
  • regulatory and reporting frameworks

D. System Position

EFTS™ operates as:

  • a truth attribution layer above physical infrastructure
  • a value-binding mechanism within VFM™
  • a validation-dependent system state under MTVF™

EFTS™ does not replace:

  • grid infrastructure
  • market mechanisms

EFTS™ defines whether their outputs are valid representations of reality.

E. Methodology

Energy attribution under EFTS™ must be calculated using:

  • real-time production data (by source type)
  • cross-border physical flow data
  • temporal energy state (time-dependent variation)
  • storage charge and discharge attribution
  • system load and transmission losses

Derived outputs may include (non-exhaustive):

  • Carbon Flow Index™ (CFI)
  • Energy Origin Profile™ (EOP)
  • Flow Attribution State™ (FAS)

All outputs must be:

  • traceable
  • time-bound
  • auditable
  • non-reconstructible post factum

F. System States

VALID STATE

A system is EFTS™ valid only if:

  • energy origin is bound to real-time flow data
  • cross-border flows are included in attribution
  • temporal variation is fully reflected
  • storage attribution is correctly linked to charge state
  • reported values match physical system conditions

INVALID STATE

A system is EFTS™ invalid if:

  • energy is attributed using certificates without physical linkage
  • annual or statistical averages replace real-time data
  • imports or exports are excluded or abstracted
  • storage output is treated as origin-free
  • carbon intensity is declared independently of actual flow

DECEPTIVE STATE (DCS — Distorted Carbon State)

Occurs when:

  • low-carbon claims are made while consuming high-carbon flow
  • energy origin is administratively reassigned
  • "green energy" claims are detached from real consumption

DCS constitutes a critical integrity failure.

G. Non-Bypassable Clause

No system may claim:

  • low-carbon operation
  • green energy usage
  • sustainable sourcing

unless fully compliant with EFTS™ real-time flow attribution.

Any claim made outside EFTS™ conditions is methodologically invalid.

H. Enforcement Logic

If EFTS™ conditions are not met:

  • all energy-related claims are invalidated
  • carbon reporting is considered non-verifiable
  • system loses compatibility status under OOF

Persistent violation results in:

  • classification as non-truth-compliant system
  • exclusion from validated reporting environments

I. System Impact

EFTS™ transforms:

  • energy from a commodity → into a traceable value flow
  • carbon accounting from declared → into measured reality
  • energy markets from price abstraction → into truth-bound systems

EFTS™ enables differentiation of energy based on:

  • time
  • origin
  • physical flow
  • system conditions
