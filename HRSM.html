Has the signal changed state?

→ preserve historical continuity.

Does the signal itself establish Harm Recognition?

→ not by default.

Is the signal sufficiently governed for downstream Recognition latency,
delay, and confirmation analysis?

→ produce the Governed Recognition Signal State.

conflicting, or meaningful only when combined with other signals; none of
those conditions makes it methodologically disposable.

HRSM ensures that Harm-relevant signals remain traceable from origin
through correlation and transformation without allowing signal volume,
visibility, repetition, or urgency to become a substitute for governed
Recognition.

A fragmented signal must remain governable as part of the whole reality it
may reveal.
