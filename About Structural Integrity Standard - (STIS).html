About Structural Integrity Standard - (STIS)

Preserving Integrity Through Structure

Integrity is not preserved by definition alone. Every governed entity depends
on a structure capable of maintaining integrity as it operates, evolves, and
interacts with other entities. Without a coherent structure, even a
well-defined integrity model will eventually degrade.

What Is Structural Integrity Standard?

Structural Integrity Standard (STIS) is the second Parent Standard of
INTEGROS® — Integrity Governance Architecture. It establishes the canonical
governance conditions required to ensure that the structure of a governed
entity is capable of preserving integrity consistently throughout its complete
lifecycle.

Canonical Definition

Structural Integrity Standard (STIS) defines the structural conditions
required for a governed entity to preserve integrity by governing its
organization, components, relationships, dependencies, and structural
coherence throughout its complete lifecycle.

Why STIS Exists

Integrity can only exist when supported by a stable and governable structure.
Weak relationships, hidden dependencies, fragmented components, or
inconsistent organization can undermine integrity even when its definition is
correct.

STIS exists to ensure that integrity is supported by a structural foundation
capable of sustaining it under normal operation, change, and long-term
evolution.

What STIS Governs

STIS governs the structural foundations of integrity, including:

  • structural organization
  • component integrity
  • relationship integrity
  • dependency integrity
  • structural coherence
  • integrity-preserving architecture.


This governed space ensures that integrity remains structurally consistent
before it is conditioned, measured, evaluated, or restored.

Architectural Position

Within INTEGROS® — Integrity Governance Architecture, STIS is the second
Parent Standard in the governance flow:

Definition → Structure → Conditions → Measurement → State → Deviation → Breach
→ Response → Restoration → Assurance

STIS transforms the integrity definition established by INDS into a structure
capable of preserving that integrity throughout the governed entity's
lifecycle.

Canonical Closing Statement

Structural Integrity Standard establishes the structural foundation upon which
integrity can endure. By governing components, relationships, dependencies,
and structural coherence, STIS ensures that integrity remains resilient,
sustainable, and governable throughout the complete lifecycle of any governed
entity.
