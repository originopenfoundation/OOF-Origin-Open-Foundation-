Herbal Infused Water Module (HIW™)

Parent Standard: Infused Water Standard (IWS™)
Category: Product & Design
Subcategory: Infused Water
Type: Product Module

Version: 1.0
Status: Canonical · Open Module
Effective Date: 25 April 2026

Authority: OOF®
Protection: MIP® — Methodological Intellectual Property
Canonical Language: English (UCL™)

Compatibility: IWS™ · Origin Eco™ · Product Integrity
Standards · Natural Systems


Canonical Definition

Herbal Infused Water Module (HIW™) defines the product-specific
conditions under which water may be naturally enriched through
contact with herbal source material while remaining structurally
identifiable as water and suitable for continuous hydration.

Herbal infused water is valid only when plant-based infusion
enriches the medium without transforming water into tea, extract,
supplement, or flavored beverage.

Module Position

This module operates under the Infused Water Standard (IWS™).

IWS™ defines the parent category boundary.
HIW™ defines the herbal application of that boundary.

The module specifies when herbal source materials may be used to
produce valid infused water without loss of hydration identity.

Base

Water remains the primary medium.
Herbal material provides natural contact-based release.
The output remains water.

The process does not create a new beverage system.
It preserves hydration while allowing controlled natural enrichment.

Core Principle

Herbal infusion may enrich water.
It must not redefine it.

A valid herbal infused water system depends on controlled release,
low transformation intensity, and preservation of water identity.

Product Conditions

For herbal infused water to remain valid within this module,
it must:
  • use water as the dominant medium
  • use herbal source material suitable for human consumption
  • rely on passive contact-based release
  • avoid direct additive substitution
  • remain suitable for repeated hydration use
Valid herbal source materials may include:
  • leaves
  • flowers
  • roots
  • stems
  • whole herbs suitable for water contact
All source materials must remain:
  • safe
  • clean
  • naturally suitable for infusion use


Integrity Principle

Herbal infused water remains valid only when:
  • infusion is real and contact-based
  • no additive logic replaces herbal release
  • no excessive intensity transforms the product into a beverage
    system
  • output remains structurally water
If herbal identity becomes dominant over hydration identity,
the product exits this module.

Minimum Implementation Framework (MIF)

Step 1 — Water Base
Use clean water suitable for repeated hydration.

Minimum requirement:
  • water must remain the dominant medium
  • no direct additive enrichment may replace infusion
 Step 2 — Herbal Source Selection
Use herbal source material suitable for water contact and human
consumption.

Minimum requirement:
  • source material must be clean
  • source material must be naturally suitable
  • no synthetic flavor substitution is permitted
 Step 3 — Infusion Application
Apply a valid infusion process through contact-based release.

Permitted methods may include:
  • cold infusion
  • hot infusion
  • controlled low-intensity decoction where water identity
    remains preserved
Minimum requirement:
  • release must occur through contact
  • no direct substance addition may simulate infusion
 Step 4 — Intensity Control
Control time, temperature, and source intensity.

Minimum requirement:
  • the process must not transform the output into tea, extract,
    or flavor-dominant beverage
  • hydration identity must remain stronger than sensory
    dominance
 Step 5 — Output Validation
Confirm final state.

Minimum requirement:
  • water remains dominant
  • output is suitable for continuous hydration
  • product is not structurally classified as a flavored
    beverage, extract, or supplement

Output Definition

Herbal Infused Water (HIW™ compliant) is water that:
  • contains naturally released herbal compounds
  • remains structurally water
  • supports hydration through low-intensity natural enrichment
  • is suitable for repeated consumption within hydration context

Structural Limitation

This module excludes:
  • concentrated herbal extracts
  • medicinal decoctions
  • additive-based hydration systems
  • flavor-dominant beverages
  • products whose transformation exceeds water identity
These may be valid products in other categories.
They are not valid within Herbal Infused Water under HIW™.

Use Case 1 — Daily Herbal Hydration

Scenario
An individual integrates low-intensity herbal infusion into daily
hydration.

Application
  • herbal material is infused into water under controlled
    conditions
  • no additives are used
  • product remains suitable for repeated daily use
 Result
  • hydration is naturally enriched
  • water intake becomes more appealing without category drift
  • the product remains structurally clean and trustworthy


Use Case 2 — Clean Hydration Product

Scenario
A brand develops plant-based hydration products using herbal
infusion instead of synthetic flavoring.

Application
  • herbal infusion replaces artificial flavor logic
  • source material remains transparent
  • process remains compatible with HIW™ and IWS™ conditions
 Result
  • clean-label hydration product
  • scalable product logic
  • clearer distinction from flavored beverages and
    supplement-like drinks


One-Line Definition

Herbal Infused Water is water naturally enriched through herbal
contact-release while remaining structurally valid for continuous
hydration.

Canonical Closing Statement

Plants may enrich water.
They must not redefine it.
