Governed Space

Evidence Relationships

Canonical Definition

Evidence Relationship Module defines the governance framework for
establishing, validating, managing, and preserving governed relationships
between individual evidence objects. It establishes relationship principles,
association rules, dependency structures, governance controls, and continuous
relationship assurance necessary to ensure that evidence is connected
accurately, consistently, and objectively throughout the complete lifecycle of
a governed entity.

Operational Role

Evidence Relationship Module governs the relationship layer of evidence
correlation by ensuring that every evidence object is connected to other
relevant evidence through governed, verifiable, and meaningful relationships.

Minimum Implementation Framework

1. Define Relationship Requirements

Identify the relationship types, dependency rules, contextual requirements,
governance objectives, and operational conditions necessary for connecting
evidence.

2. Define Relationship Methodology

Establish standardized procedures for creating, documenting, validating,
maintaining, and governing evidence relationships throughout the evidence
lifecycle.

3. Define Relationship Validation Logic

Define how evidence relationships are evaluated by verifying their
correctness, consistency, relevance, completeness, and governance compliance.

4. Define Governance Response

Establish governance actions for invalid relationships, missing links,
conflicting associations, broken dependencies, and corrective relationship
management.

5. Preserve Relationship Records

Maintain relationship history, dependency mappings, validation records,
governance decisions, timestamps, and supporting documentation throughout the
complete lifecycle.

Example Use Cases

Use Case 1 — Autonomous AI Governance

Scenario

Multiple autonomous AI agents generate separate evidence while collaborating
on a complex operational process.

Application

Evidence Relationship Module governs how every evidence object is connected
through validated operational relationships, dependencies, and contextual
associations.

Result

The complete operational process can be reconstructed using governed evidence
relationships without compromising evidence integrity.

Use Case 2 — Digital Investigation

Scenario

Investigators analyze evidence collected from multiple devices, systems, and
operational environments.

Application

Evidence Relationship Module governs how all evidence objects are linked into
a coherent evidence network based on validated relationships.

Result

Investigators obtain a trustworthy evidence graph that accurately represents
operational reality and supports audit, investigation, and legal review.

Canonical Closing Statement

Evidence Relationship Module establishes the canonical governance framework
for governing relationships between independently managed evidence objects
throughout their complete lifecycle. By governing evidence associations,
dependency structures, relationship validation, and continuous relationship
assurance, the module enables reliable operational reconstruction, accountable
governance, independent verification, and trustworthy audit across all
operational domains.
