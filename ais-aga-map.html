AGA™ Architecture Map

Accountability Governance Architecture

Official Accountability Governance Reference Map for Organizations,
Institutions, Governments,

Autonomous Systems and Human-AI Operational Environments

AGA™ serves as the official accountability governance reference map
used to identify, classify, navigate, investigate, validate, and
understand accountability spaces across organizational,
institutional, autonomous, and intelligent systems.

The purpose of AGA™ is not to investigate accountability.

The purpose of AGA™ is to identify, define, and map the governable
accountability spaces of operational reality.

AGA™ serves as an official accountability governance reference map
for:

  • Organizations
  • Institutions
  • Governments
  • Regulatory Authorities
  • Contractor Networks
  • Supply Chains
  • Corporate Groups
  • Human-AI Environments
  • Autonomous Agents
  • Multi-Agent Systems
  • Robotics
  • Intelligent Infrastructure
  • Future Autonomous Systems


Core Architectural Principle

Every accountability space answers one primary question.

No accountability space duplicates the purpose of another
accountability space.

Every accountability space begins where the previous accountability
space ends.

Together these accountability spaces form the official
accountability governance reference map of AGA™.

1. Relationship Accountability Standard (RAS)

Core Question

What is the actual relationship between the actors?

Canonical Definition

RAS defines the conditions under which operational relationships
remain identifiable, reconstructable, governable, accountable, and
operationally valid.

Governance Boundary

Begins when two or more actors enter an operational relationship.

Ends when authority relationships can be evaluated.

Core Scope

  • operational relationships
  • actor relationships
  • relationship origin
  • relationship validity
  • relationship traceability
  • relationship accountability

Architectural Position

AGA™ governs the complete accountability lifecycle.

The architecture progresses:

Relationship → Authority → Responsibility → Capability → Control →
Oversight → Evidence → Accountability → Decision Accountability →
Accountability Continuity → Accountability Chain

Together these accountability spaces govern how accountability
remains identifiable, attributable, reconstructable, enforceable,
reviewable, and governable throughout operational reality.

CAP™ Protocol Note

Architecture Map during accountability investigation, accountability
reconstruction, and governance analysis.

AGA™ defines the accountability spaces.

CAP™ navigates through them.

operational reality.
