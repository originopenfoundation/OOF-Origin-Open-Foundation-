Website analytics notice

When enabled, independent website analytics records daily aggregate counts of page and content categories, broad referral categories, language, and selected interactions. It does not record search text, full page URLs, IP addresses, cookies, or visitor identifiers in the analytics database. These counts cannot identify unique visitors or distinguish people from bots.

Aggregate records older than 90 days are scheduled for deletion; separate private backups are retained for up to seven days. Cloudflare operates the request gateway and Supabase stores the aggregates; service providers may process network metadata. The independent analytics tracker respects browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. Cloudflare Web Analytics is a separate service.
