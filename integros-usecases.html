Use Cases — INTEGROS® Integrity Standard

Governance & Enforcement

Use Case 1 — Certification Integrity Failure

Scenario

A company operates under a valid certification.

Over time:

  • production processes change
  • internal shortcuts are introduced
  • documentation no longer reflects reality

Without INTEGROS®

  • certification remains valid
  • real system behavior is not traceable
  • manipulation is not detectable

Result:

  • false trust
  • regulatory risk
  • unsafe or non-compliant output

With INTEGROS®

  • all process changes are traceable
  • system behavior is continuously verifiable
  • manipulation is detectable

Result:

  • certification reflects reality
  • integrity is continuously maintained

Use Case 2 — AI Integrity Breakdown

Scenario

An organization deploys AI systems for decision-making.

Over time:

  • models are updated
  • training data changes
  • decisions become less explainable

Without INTEGROS®

  • AI decisions cannot be traced
  • model behavior is unclear
  • responsibility becomes ambiguous

Result:

  • loss of control
  • legal exposure
  • untrustworthy AI decisions

With INTEGROS®

  • decision paths are traceable
  • model changes are recorded
  • behavior is verifiable

Result:

  • accountable AI system
  • controlled decision environment
