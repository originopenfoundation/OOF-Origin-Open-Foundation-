Open Contribution Roles (Global · Remote)

OOF™ (Origin Open Foundation™) is building an independent, methodology-driven
infrastructure for standards, governance frameworks, and integrity architectures across AI,
ecology, identity, and value systems.

OOF™ operates as an independent methodological authority defining system-level standards
across multiple domains.

OOF™ is building a new category of system-level standards connecting identity, integrity,
execution, and responsibility into a unified methodology framework.

We are currently in an early-stage build phase. Many roles are contribution-based at the
start, with a structured pathway toward funded rolesand independent implementation
partnerships as grants, institutional collaborations, and contracts are secured.

Working language: English (canonical standard language)
Work mode: Remote / global (asynchronous-friendly)
Core need right now: People who build and ship (documentation, web, structure)

What We Need Most Right Now

1) Web & HTML Implementation (High Priority)

For people who can help improve and maintain the website structure and pages.

Examples:
  • Static websites (GitHub Pages), HTML/CSS, lightweight JS
  • Clean page layouts, navigation structure, internal linking
  • Consistent formatting of standards pages and "About" pages
  • Performance, accessibility, and simple SEO fundamentals

Role titles:
  • Systems & Website Contributor
  • Web Implementation Contributor
  • Documentation-to-Web Publisher

 2) Standards & Documentation Editors (High Priority)

For people who can polish English, improve clarity, and keep documents consistent.

Examples:
  • Editing standards text for clarity, consistency, and regulator-grade tone
  • Maintaining unified structure (headers, sections, canonical definitions)
  • Building templates and checklists for adoption and self-declaration

Role titles:
  • Standards Development Contributor
  • Documentation Editor (English)
  • Governance Framework Contributor

 3) Implementation Framework Builders

For people who can convert standards into minimal implementation blueprints.

Examples:
  • Creating minimum implementation frameworks
  • Self-declaration templates and checklists
  • Adoption guidance usable by small and medium organizations

Role titles:
  • Implementation Framework Coordinator
  • Standards Adoption Contributor
  • Governance Implementation Facilitator

 4) Data / Registry / Database Contributors (Future-Core)

For people experienced in building structured registries and simple databases.

Examples:
  • Schema design for standards registry
  • Index pages, identifiers, versioning logic
  • Foundations for a future public reference database
Role titles:
  • Data Systems Contributor
  • Registry Architecture Contributor
  • Database & Indexing Contributor

 5) Grants & Institutional Funding (Strategic)

For people who know how to secure grants, partnerships, or institutional funding.

Examples:
  • Grant research, application drafting, partnership outreach
  • Funding strategy, eligibility mapping, proposal writing

Role titles:
  • Institutional Funding Coordinator
  • Grants & Partnerships Coordinator
  • Strategic Funding Liaison

 6) Systems & Architecture Contributors (Strategic Core)

For people who think in systems, structures, and cross-layer architecture.

Examples:
  • Connecting standards across identity, integrity, execution, and responsibility
  • Structuring new methodology layers
  • Defining relationships between modules and frameworks

Role titles:
  • Systems Architecture Contributor
  • Methodology Structure Contributor
  • Cross-Layer Framework Designer

Contribution Model (Transparent & Professional)


  • Early roles are contribution-based during the build phase.
  • Contributors who actively build core infrastructure are prioritized for future
    funded roles and partnerships.

As OOF™ grows, roles may evolve into:

  • funded positions (part-time or full-time)
  • commission-based coordination (case-by-case)
  • independent third-party implementation partnerships
  • direct collaboration within OOF™ operational structures

OOF™ maintains structural ownership under MIP™ (Methodological Intellectual
Property).

If You Don't Fit a Listed Role


If you want to contribute but don't see a perfect match above, contact us anyway.

Tell us:
  • what you can do
  • what you want to focus on (AI / eco / identity / web / registry / funding)
  • how much time you can contribute weekly

We will try to place you into a suitable role within the OOF™ infrastructure.
If there is no immediate match, a suitable role may open in the next phase.

How to Apply / Contact

Send a short message with:

  1. Your name + location/timezone
  2. Skills (bullet list)
  3. Links (GitHub / website / writing samples / LinkedIn)
  4. Your preferred focus area
  5. Weekly availability (hours)
